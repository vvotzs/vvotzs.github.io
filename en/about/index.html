About Deal Hunter

A hand-checked directory of global deals: only offers we can trace to official terms.

Last updated 2026-11-21

What this is

Deal Hunter is a hand-checked directory of global deals in six sections: AI Tools & Credits, Crypto Airdrops & Points, Global Banks, Cards & Transfers, Global VPS & Cloud, Global eSIM Guide, and Software & Digital Services.

Every entry lists the expected value, the requirements, the regions it works in and the deadline, and its main button goes to the official offer page. Where a community thread broke the news first, a second link points to that original post.

How we check

We start from the brand's own terms page and verify the amount, eligibility, end date, payout and clawback rules, then read community reports (US Card Forum, NodeSeek, Linux.do, r/beermoneyuk, OzBargain, X) to see whether the offer is already dead, has a hidden catch, or gets declined on risk checks.

Where the two disagree, the official terms win and the difference goes into the risk note. Anything we cannot confirm is marked unverified instead of being stated as fact.

Update cadence

The site re-checks every 2.5 hours: new offers are found and verified against the official page, and entries ending within 7 days are flagged separately. Expired offers are moved to the archive rather than deleted, so their links keep working.

Submissions and corrections

If you spot a new offer, or an entry that is wrong or has a dead link, write to vvotzs@gmail.com or use the Submit button on the home page.

Every submission is checked against the official page before it is listed, and the source line says it came from a reader. All we need is a name, a link, and optionally the region and a short note; no account details.
