Privacy policy

A static site with no accounts and no server-side storage; no tracking script loads until you allow analytics or ads.

Last updated 2026-11-21

No sign-in data

Deal Hunter is a static site: there are no accounts, no sign-in and no backend database. Every page is built as plain HTML before publishing, so reading the site needs no sign-up and no personal details.

What we actually store

As of the update date shown on this page, the site loads no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking script, and sets no tracking cookies.

Two local values are used, both necessary: a tab-level sessionStorage entry (key ydj_last_sent) that only records when you last sent a submission, so the button ignores double taps for 5 seconds and expires when the tab closes; and a localStorage entry on this site (key dh_consent_v1) written only when you change Cookie Settings, holding the necessary flag, your analytics and ads switches, and the time you chose.

Both stay in your browser and are never uploaded here, because there is no server to receive them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Cookie settings and consent

Cookie Settings in the footer opens the consent panel at any time: choose Essential only, or allow analytics and ads separately, and change it whenever you like. The panel floats over the bottom of the page and takes no space in the layout.

No analytics or ad script loads before you agree, and after you withdraw consent any third-party script that was loaded stops appearing on the next page load.

With private browsing or storage disabled the site still reads normally; only the saved choice is not kept between pages.

Analytics and ads, if we add them

If Google Analytics 4 is added later, it will load only after you allow analytics, to measure anonymous traffic and see which pages help. If Google AdSense ads run here, they will load only after you allow ads; Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this or other websites, and may collect anonymous data such as page, referrer, device type and rough region.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads), or withdraw consent in Cookie Settings. Either way the site stays readable.

Legal bases (GDPR)

For users in the EU/EEA/UK we process data under Article 6 of the General Data Protection Regulation: necessary storage and keeping the site secure are needed to provide the pages you ask for and rest on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f)); analytics and advertising rest on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting processing that already happened; submission details are used to verify an offer and reply if needed, under Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f).

Your rights under the GDPR

You can ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. We do not profile readers or make automated decisions with legal effects.

If you believe our processing breaks the GDPR, you can complain to your local supervisory authority: the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, or the data protection authority of your EU/EEA member state.

To exercise these rights, write to vvotzs@gmail.com with what you want. We answer within one month of receiving the request, and for complex requests may extend that by up to two further months with an explanation. Exercising your rights is free.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising; we have not done so in the past 12 months and have no plans to. The site has no user accounts, collects no sensitive personal information as defined by California law, and holds no customer lists or payment details.

California residents have the right to know (the categories of information collected, their sources and purposes), to delete, to correct, to opt out of sale or sharing, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we neither sell nor share personal information, a separate Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link is not required; you can still send a request by email.

To make a request, write to vvotzs@gmail.com with the subject CCPA request and say whether you want to know, delete or correct. We answer within 45 days, and may extend once by a further 45 days with an explanation. You may use an authorised agent with written permission. The site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Submissions

When you submit an offer through the form or by email, we receive only the name, link, region and note you enter, plus your sending address. That is used to verify the offer and to reply if needed; your address is never published and never used for ad profiling. Submission records are kept for 24 months after verification and archiving, and you can ask us to delete them sooner.

Hosting and international transfers

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). GitHub processes technical data such as IP address and request time under its own logging policy, which is outside our control; see the GitHub Privacy Statement (docs.github.com/site-policy). Where that amounts to a transfer to the United States, it rests on providing the pages you requested or on your consent for analytics and ads.

External links

Links here point to brand sites or community posts. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

The date at the top of this page is the last update. If analytics or ads are switched on, or our processing changes, we will update this page and the consent settings first, and note material changes at the top.

Contact

Privacy questions, access or deletion requests: vvotzs@gmail.com.
