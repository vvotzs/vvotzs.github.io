Privacy policy

This is a static site: no accounts and no server-side storage; the only cookies come from analytics and ads.

Last updated 2026-11-20

No sign-in data

Deal Hunter is a static site: there are no accounts, no sign-in and no backend database. Every page is built as plain HTML before publishing, so reading the site needs no sign-up and no personal details.

Analytics and advertising cookies

We set no cookies of our own to identify you. To understand traffic and improve the site we may use Google Analytics; if Google AdSense ads run here, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this or other websites, and may collect anonymous data such as page, referrer, device type and rough region.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads), or clear or block cookies in your browser. Either way the site stays readable.

Submissions

When you submit an offer through the form or by email, we receive only the name, link, region and note you enter, plus your sending address. That is used to verify the offer and to reply if needed; your address is never published and never used for ad profiling. Ask us to delete a past submission and we will.

External links

Links here point to brand sites or community posts. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.
