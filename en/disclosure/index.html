Links and listing disclosure

The main link goes to the official offer page; affiliate or partner links are always labelled and marked sponsored, at no extra cost to you.

Last updated 2026-11-21

Linking

The main link on every entry is the official offer page. If the offer first appeared in a community thread (a forum, Reddit, X), a second link points to that original post so you can check where the information came from.

Affiliate and partner links

Some offer or product links may be affiliate or partner links. If you sign up, buy or top up through one of them, the brand may pay us a commission; that adds nothing to your cost and does not change the price, amount, eligibility or terms of the offer itself.

Every affiliate link is labelled Sponsored in the entry and marked rel="sponsored noopener". A link without that label means we are paid nothing by the brand.

Editorial independence

Whether an offer is listed, how it is described and how its value and risks are judged depend only on whether it can be verified against official terms, never on whether it pays. A commission does not get an expired, unclear or flawed offer listed, and does not soften a risk note.

Invite and referral codes

Invite codes and referral links shared by other people, such as a submitter's own code, are never used as the source link, and codes inside submissions are not adopted. Entries link to the official page.

Amounts and terms

Amounts, eligibility, deadlines and payout conditions follow the brand's own terms. We check them carefully but promise nothing, and an offer can change or end early.

Corrections

Spotted an error or a dead link? Write to vvotzs@gmail.com.
