Highlight

The Zhipu ZCode client issues a GLOBAL BUILD card every night at 19:00: 300M GLM-5.3-Flash tokens on weekdays and 500M at weekends, active from 23:00 and zeroed at 09:00 next day (Monday 09:00 for weekend cards); free accounts qualify, no card needed, and new users also get a five-day trial of 8M tokens a day

What you get

iGetoken's standing campaign page (from 12 Nov 2026) and allagent.wiki's daily free-quota posts (14 and 16 Nov) agree: ZCode turned GLOBAL BUILD into a daily card on 27 Oct, claimed from the client's lower-left corner from 19:00, active at 23:00 and zeroed at 09:00 (weekend cards carry 500M and last to Monday 09:00), with no accumulation and no stacking between cards. After claiming you must switch the channel to Global Building (Start Plan) and keep the model on GLM-5.3-Flash, or your paid plan quota is charged instead. ZCode's official docs also list a new-user Free Trial Quota: five days after linking BigModel with 3M GLM-5.3 and 5M GLM-5.3-Flash tokens a day. These are in-product tokens, not exportable API keys, and cannot feed Cursor or Claude Code.

Conditions

Install the ZCode client (official notes say versions below 3.10 have no claim entry); any signed-in user, including free accounts, with no card; cards appear at 19:00, activate at 23:00 and expire at 09:00; switch to the Global Building channel and GLM-5.3-Flash after claiming; tokens cannot be exported as API; concurrency is capped at 3 tasks.

Steps

  1. Download and update ZCode from zcode.z.ai
  2. Sign in and claim the day's GLOBAL BUILD card in the lower-left corner
  3. Wait for the tokens to land at 23:00
  4. Switch the model channel to Global Building and pick GLM-5.3-Flash
  5. Confirm in Settings > Usage stats that usage is not hitting the paid plan

Watch out

Only covers the ten hours overnight, so nothing in the daytime; the tokens cannot be exported as an API; the wrong channel charges your paid plan; Zhipu publishes no public campaign page, so the card in the client governs and can change at any time; peak hours bring queues and rate limits, so keep important changes on a Git branch.
