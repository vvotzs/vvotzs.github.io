Highlight

First-time Qianfan users get 1M tokens on each of 17 models, 17M in total and 90 days, including DeepSeek-V3.1 and Kimi-K2

What you get

Activating Qianfan for the first time and accepting the agreement automatically grants free tokens on listed models: the New User Free Quota document (updated 11 November 2026) lists 17 services, each with 1M tokens valid three months, 17M in total. The list covers ERNIE-4.5-Turbo-128K, 32K and VL, ERNIE-X1-Turbo-32K, DeepSeek-R1, DeepSeek-V3, V3.1 and V3.1-Think, Kimi-K2-Instruct, Qwen3-235B-A22B and 30B-A3B, Qwen3-Coder-30B and 480B, plus the non-chat models bge-large-en, bge-large-zh and qianfan-sug-8k. Each allowance is separate, and remaining quota shows under Model Square then the model version detail. The API is OpenAI-compatible at https://qianfan.baidubce.com/v2.

Conditions

First activation only, with no retroactive grant; covers online inference on preset models but not batch inference; each model has 1M tokens for three months counted from activation, not first call; models off the list bill normally; quota is shared across sub-accounts of one primary account.

Steps

  1. Register on Baidu AI Cloud and complete real-name verification with a mainland phone number
  2. Activate the Qianfan model and Agent platform for the first time; the quota posts automatically with no coupon to claim
  3. Check each model page in Model Square for remaining free quota and time
  4. Create an API key and call https://qianfan.baidubce.com/v2 through the OpenAI SDK, turning on the stop-at-zero option so overage does not bill automatically

Watch out

The official list still reflects the late-2025 model lineup, so what the console offers may have moved on; quota does not stack, does not cross models and is not reissued; a mainland phone number and real-name verification are required; overage bills automatically unless the stop-at-zero option is on; some listed models may be retired.
