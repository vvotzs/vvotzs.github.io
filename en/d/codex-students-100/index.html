What you get

OpenAI's Help Center terms for Codex credits for Students (cross-checked with llmperks on 12 Nov 2026, TopUseAI on 3 Nov 2026 and gptx on 8 Nov 2026): the $100 appears as 2,500 credits on the usage page and is spent once the plan's own Codex allowance runs out, without upgrading the plan. The earlier four free months of ChatGPT Plus for students closed on 31 Oct 2026; this Codex offer remains open, and French students instead claim €50 (about 1,250 credits) through UNiDAYS.

Conditions

Current student at a degree-granting university in the US or Canada, living there when claiming; verify with a school email through SheerID; a ChatGPT account on Free, Go, Plus or Pro; one per student and non-transferable; credits expire 12 months after the grant; not API credits, and students outside the US and Canada (including mainland China) cannot pass verification.

Steps

  1. Sign in to your own ChatGPT account (the free plan works)
  2. Open the Codex for University Students page and press Claim now
  3. Complete SheerID verification with your school email
  4. The credits are added to your personal workspace automatically
  5. Check the Codex usage page for 2,500 credits

Watch out

Usable only inside Codex, not the API; unused credits expire after 12 months; a VPN will not pass student verification, so ignore agents offering to claim it for you; one shot per student with no fallback if verification fails.
