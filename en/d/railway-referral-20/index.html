Highlight

Sign up to Railway through an existing user's invite link and the new account can receive $20 of platform credit, equivalent to a month of Pro; Railway support confirms it is applied when the account upgrades to a paid plan

What you get

Railway's official docs state that any user who is referred gets $20 in Railway credits, equivalent to a free month on the Pro tier, while the referrer earns 15% commission on what the referred account pays. One account per person is enforced across email, GitHub and payment method. The timing matters: Railway support replied in its official community on 1 October 2026 that 'the $20 referral credit isn't granted at signup. It's applied when the referred account upgrades to a paid plan (Hobby or Pro)', and that the $5 a new workspace shows is the standard trial grant everyone gets. So the $20 is a discount on paying, not free cash. It is still worthwhile for small projects: $20 covers months of the Hobby plan or a month of Pro, credits carry no cash value, cannot be transferred and are non-refundable, and a card is required for usage beyond the credit. Checked on docs.railway.com and Railway's community on 21 November 2026.

Conditions

New Railway account only, and the invite link must be opened before signing up; one account per person; the $20 applies when the workspace upgrades to Hobby or Pro; credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred or refunded; a card is required for usage above the credit.

Steps

  1. Ask an existing Railway user for their invite link, found on the Refer page of the dashboard
  2. Sign up through the link and connect GitHub
  3. Deploy a project, then upgrade the workspace to a Hobby or Pro paid plan
  4. The $20 credit offsets the bill, roughly a free month on Pro

Watch out

The $20 is not granted at sign-up - it applies only after upgrading to a paid plan, which means binding a card first; signing up before opening the link forfeits it; one account per person, and duplicates get closed; the credit cannot be withdrawn.
