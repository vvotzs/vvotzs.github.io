Highlight

No card-opening fee on the first card and no monthly or annual fee, from a US$10 minimum top-up; direct charges at about 60 AI merchants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor earn 30% (Standard) or 50% (Professional/Elite) back, plus 1.5% or more on ordinary spending

What you get

Personal Infini cards: the first card costs $0 to open (each extra card $10), there is no monthly or annual fee, and the minimum initial top-up is US$10, which goes straight to the card balance. Lite (Visa, Apple/Google Pay, 3% FX fee, works for ChatGPT/Claude); Lite Plus (US BIN, 2% FX fee, works for ChatGPT/Claude/Cursor/OpenRouter, Professional tier or above); Max (Mastercard, 0% FX fee, $0.30 on transactions under $50, not available in the EU); Pro (1% FX fee, does not support ChatGPT/Claude). AI cashback follows the tier on the settlement date: 30% on Standard and 50% on Professional, capped at $10 a month; 50% on Elite, capped at $100, paid on the 2nd of the following month. Ordinary spending earns 1.5% and up, with 3% on the first $1,000 a month for Professional and 5% on the first $10,000 for Elite. Tiers are reassessed daily from the 30-day average balance.

Conditions

Sumsub identity verification plus an initial top-up of at least US$10; AI cashback counts only merchant-direct charges, so App Store/Google Play, gift cards and third-party top-ups do not qualify; the $100 Elite cap needs a 30-day average balance of $100,000; the tier is taken on the settlement date, so a downgrade shrinks that month's allowance.

Steps

  1. Register at infini.money and complete Sumsub verification (passport or local ID plus a selfie)
  2. Top up at least 10 USDT/USDC (that is spendable card balance, not an opening fee)
  3. Open one card: Lite or Lite Plus for ChatGPT/Claude payments, Max for large purchases
  4. Pay the AI service directly on its own site (not through an app store) and check the cashback on the 2nd of the next month

Watch out

A stablecoin platform, so the issuer, card BIN and rules can change; Lite charges 3% on non-USD purchases and Max charges $0.30 on transactions under $50; AI cashback only counts merchant-direct charges, so in-app subscriptions and third-party top-ups earn nothing; opening from mainland China comes from one channel report and KYC may still be refused (the official site does not promise it); the balance is held in stablecoins, with platform risk on you.
