Highlight

KDDI's zero-monthly-fee povo2.0 via Hapitas: sign up as a new line, activate the eSIM and buy any paid top-up within 3 days of activation (the 24-hour unlimited pack at 330 yen is cheapest) to bank 6,500 points after about 90 days, about 6,170 yen net; code HAPITAS1GB also adds 1GB

What you get

KDDI's povo2.0 is a zero-monthly-fee SIM, and the Hapitas offer requires a new application with the eSIM activated, then any paid data top-up within 3 days of activation (the 24-hour unlimited pack at 330 yen is the cheapest), with 6,500 points credited after roughly 90 days of validation. Points are 1 point to 1 yen and convert to cash or e-money, leaving about 6,170 yen net after the 330 yen pack. The code HAPITAS1GB must be entered during sign-up and adds a 1GB/24-hour data bonus. The same offer pays 5,600 points on Moppy and 5,700 on Point Income, so Hapitas is currently highest; the boosted rate is listed until 30 November.

Conditions

Apply through the Hapitas offer page and choose eSIM; anyone who has applied for povo2.0 before (even if cancelled) is excluded; physical SIMs and applications started on povo's own site or app do not count; the paid top-up must be bought within 3 days of activation; validation takes about 90 days; Japanese ID and address required.

Steps

  1. Log in to Hapitas, open the povo2.0 offer and click through
  2. Choose the contract type (voice plus data or data only) and eSIM, entering HAPITAS1GB in the code field
  3. Complete identity verification in the povo app and activate the eSIM
  4. Buy any paid top-up within 3 days of activation (330 yen for 24 hours unlimited is cheapest) and wait about 90 days for the points

Watch out

Points are validated only after about 90 days and a wrong click path or missing code cannot be appealed (this offer does not accept ledger enquiries); povo suspends lines left without a top-up for long periods; points are not cash on the spot and withdrawing takes a payout request; Japanese ID and address are required, so applying from mainland China is not possible.
