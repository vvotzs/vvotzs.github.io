Highlight

The foreigner-only "data worry-free USIM 7GB" is 22,900 won a month, and self-activating at a direct store pays 180,000 won of Naver Pay (30,000 a month for 6 months), with another 90,000 won from a friend's referral — up to 270,000 won (about $186) — and both USIM and eSIM activation fees waived

What you get

The foreigner-only Data Speed USIM 7GB plan costs 22,900 won a month (7GB fast plus unlimited 3Mbps) and a store promotion adds 15GB a month (10GB for the launch plus 5GB from a friend referral). Self-activating any plan of 15,900 won or more with the promo code gives everyone 180,000 won of Naver Pay (30,000 a month for six months), and joining through a friend's referral link adds 90,000 won (15,000 a month for six months), up to 270,000 won. USIM and eSIM activation fees are waived, and cards bought at convenience stores or Coupang activate online.

Conditions

An alien registration card with 30+ days' stay, a Korean credit or debit card in your name and the simplified authentication app; the promo code must be entered during self-activation and cannot be added later; Naver Pay is paid over six months and can stop on early cancellation; the 90,000 won referral needs joining through the link.

Steps

  1. Buy a Hello Mobile USIM or eSIM at a direct store, convenience store or Coupang
  2. Go to direct.lghellovision.net, pick a plan of 15,900 won or more and enter the promo code
  3. Pass simple authentication, the Alien Registration Card check and your own-name card check
  4. Register for the payout after activation so Naver Pay arrives monthly
  5. Join through a friend's referral link to add another 90,000 won

Watch out

An Alien Registration Card is mandatory, so short-stay tourists cannot use it; Naver Pay is split over six months and later instalments may be lost on early cancellation; the unlimited part is throttled to 3 Mbps; using someone else's Korean card breaches the terms.
