Highlight

3 Hong Kong's SoSIM sells a HK$33 eSIM online or in its WeChat mini programme, with a QR code by email and a +852 number live in about ten minutes; a new card gets 50GB for 30 days in Hong Kong or 5 days of Asia-Pacific roaming, the number lasts 120 days and a top-up of HK$20 or more extends it, and with Hong Kong registration the card receives SMS while roaming

What you get

SoSIM is the prepaid brand of Hutchison Telecommunications Hong Kong (3HK) built around one card with two uses: new customers buy an eSIM for HK$33 on the SoSIM web store or in the WeChat mini programme, the QR code arrives at the verified email, and scanning it brings a real +852 number live in about ten minutes. The HK$33 choice: 50GB of full-speed Hong Kong data plus 1,500 local minutes for 30 days, or five days of Asia-Pacific roaming data covering Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand among 24 destinations plus 3GB of extra Hong Kong data for 30 days, switched in the account within 48 hours of activation. No contract and no admin fee. Keeping the number is the selling point: card validity, which is the number's validity, is separate from the starter package and starts at 120 days. A top-up of at least HK$20, or buying a designated service, extends it, and community guides budget about HK$60 a year. New cards also get free conversion to eSIM and an HK$8 number change as standing offers, against a normal HK$28 conversion fee. SMS in mainland China: the official FAQ states the card works in any SoSIM roaming destination as long as there is roaming coverage, Wi-Fi is off and mobile data is on, so activation outside Hong Kong is possible. Hong Kong local service requires registration first, with a passport or a mainland travel permit and manual review of one to two hours, up to 48 hours; roaming-data-only use can skip registration. The official FAQ also states that once registered, SMS can be received free of charge abroad while the mainland data card is valid, so codes need no package. On verification codes: the +852 native carrier range works in tests for WhatsApp, Telegram, Hong Kong banks and brokers, Apple ID and WeChat, but many AI platforms including ChatGPT and Claude apply extra risk checks to Hong Kong ranges and often refuse them, which is where this card differs from a Philippine DITO number.

Conditions

New customers buy an eSIM at HK$33 or more on the SoSIM web store or in the WeChat mini programme; install and activate within 60 days of purchase; registration with a passport or mainland travel permit is required for Hong Kong local use, with manual review up to 48 hours, while roaming-data-only use needs none; number validity starts at 120 days and extends with a top-up of HK$20 or more or a designated purchase; SMS reception while roaming in mainland China is package-dependent and not itemised officially.

Steps

  1. Buy the HK$33 eSIM on the SoSIM web store or in the WeChat mini programme, which accepts mainland Alipay and WeChat Pay
  2. Scan the eSIM QR code from the email, connect to the network and receive the activation SMS with your number
  3. Install the SoSIM app and complete registration with your passport or mainland travel permit if you need Hong Kong local service
  4. Switch to the 50GB Hong Kong or Asia-Pacific package within 48 hours of activation, and top up at least HK$20 in the app before expiry to keep the number

Watch out

Hong Kong local use requires registration, and an unregistered card gets data only with no calls or SMS; number validity starts at only 120 days and forgetting to top up loses the number and the balance with no recovery; mainland China SMS reception is not itemised officially and depends on the package; Hong Kong ranges are often refused by AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Claude; eSIM support differs between Hong Kong and China-market iPhones, and China-market phones usually need an external eSIM adapter.
