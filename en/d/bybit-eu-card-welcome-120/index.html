Highlight

EEA residents apply for the Bybit EU virtual card and top up €100 within 30 days: 10% cashback on eligible spending for the first 30 days (up to €120) plus 100% back on ChatGPT/Netflix/Spotify and similar subscriptions (up to €50)

What you get

Bybit EU's Bybit Card welcome offer (the EU entity runs it long-term, alongside the global 120 USDT version that ended on 11 November): EEA residents who apply and are approved for the virtual card, then top up at least €100 within 30 days (crypto or fiat), get 10% cashback on eligible spending for 30 days from when Cashback Booster appears, capped at €120 regardless of card tier; at the same time subscriptions get 100% back (ChatGPT, Netflix, Spotify, TradingView, Amazon Prime and others), capped at €50. Cashback accrues as points that Auto-Cashback converts to USDC/BTC, or you redeem manually in the rewards store.

Conditions

EEA residents only (excluding Malta), card approved after 2026-08-20, KYC Lv.1, and no Bybit Card held before; unlocking requires €100 topped up within 30 days; the 10% covers only selected MCCs — transport, clothing, dining, beauty, travel, airlines, hotels — and excludes e-wallets, prepaid and financial spending such as Alipay, WeChat, Revolut and Wise; transactions must settle inside the 30-day window; Bybit EU can change or cancel the offer.

Steps

  1. Apply for the virtual card in the Bybit EU app and complete KYC Lv.1 (approved after 2026-08-20)
  2. Register for the offer on the official page
  3. Top up €100 or more into the Funding Account within 30 days (crypto or fiat)
  4. Wait for Cashback Booster on the card dashboard, then spend with the card during the next 30 days
  5. Convert points to USDC/BTC under Rewards or redeem them in the store

Watch out

Open to EEA residents only — mainland China can't take part and a VPN won't help because KYC will refuse; cashback is limited to selected MCCs and counted at settlement, so spending after the window doesn't count; €120 is a cap you won't reach at normal spending; crypto price risk is yours.
