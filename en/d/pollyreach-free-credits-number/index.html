Highlight

Say "book me a table for 7pm" and the agent finds the number, dials, navigates the IVR and reports back with a summary, recording and transcript. It also answers your phone 24/7.

What you get

PollyReach gives an AI agent a real phone number and a real voice. Outbound: give it a task and it finds the number, dials, works through phone menus, holds the conversation, handles interruptions and hangs up when done, then sends you a summary, recording and transcript. Inbound: it answers 24/7, screens spam, takes messages and tags what matters. New users start free. The Product Hunt launch post says everyone gets 200 credits plus a free phone number with no card. After the credits run out it becomes credit-based: the pricing page script lists Star at $20/month (2,000 credits, one agent, one US number), Pro at $40 (4,000 credits, two agents) and Max at $100 (10,000 credits, five agents), with annual billing around 15% off.

Conditions

The 200 free credits are a one-off; paid plans take over afterwards. The credit-to-minute conversion is not published (unverified). Daily outbound call limits apply and bulk robocalling is blocked. Complex IVR trees can still defeat it.

Steps

  1. Sign up at pollyreach.ai (no credit card) and collect the 200 credits
  2. Claim a free number and set the agent's identity and script
  3. Send it a task in chat, e.g. "book a table for two on Friday at 7pm", then read the summary and recording

Watch out

Paid tiers are only in the page script and not fully published, so confirm charges in the app. The callee is talking to an AI and some businesses refuse. The free number is US-based, so international calls can cost the other side.
