Highlight

A free coding agent from the Codebuff team, launched on 19 November: terminal, desktop, mobile and web, with no API key and no credit card. Small text ads in the terminal pay for the models, and 100 Freebucks refill at midnight Pacific time.

What you get

The free tier from the Codebuff open-source agent team, launched on 19 November 2026. Its site calls it the 100% free coding agent, $0 forever with unlimited usage, no card and no credit watching, funded by text ads in the terminal. It runs as a CLI (npm install -g freebuff), desktop apps on macOS, Windows and Linux, iOS and Android and on the web, with threads stored in the cloud to continue across devices. Models include DeepSeek v4, MiMo 2.5 Pro, GLM 5.2 and MiniMax M3, Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite finds files and you can bring your own Claude key. The allowance is 100 Freebucks a day, reset at midnight Pacific without rollover, spent at hourly rates that heavy models can burn in a few hours.

Conditions

100 Freebucks a day, reset at midnight Pacific without carry-over; the full model list is for 25+ countries only, with others pushed into limited mode (DeepSeek V4 Flash only, six one-hour sessions a day) even behind a VPN; text ads appear in the terminal, the company says messages, traces and files may be used for ad targeting and some models for training (marked in the picker); sign in once to start.

Steps

  1. Install with npm install -g freebuff, or download the desktop app from freebuff.com
  2. Log in once, then open /model and pick a model without the training note
  3. Give it one verifiable change in a small project
  4. Continue the same thread from mobile or the web, remembering the daily reset

Watch out

The price is privacy: the launch material admits messages, traces and files may feed ad targeting and some models train on data, so keep private code on a non-training model or your own key. Region limits can hide the best models, hourly rates drain the allowance quickly, and a brand-new service has no reliability record.
