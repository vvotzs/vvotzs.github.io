Highlight

Existing customers send a personal link or code from Refer and Earn in the app or site, and a new customer uses it on their first online international transfer; the official wording only says the discount and minimum are whatever the Refer and Earn page shows, while third parties put it at £10

What you get

MoneyGram UK Invite Friends: an existing customer who has completed an online transfer can generate a personal link or code, a new customer registers with it and gets a discount on their first online international transfer, and the existing customer gets a discount once the friend's transfer arrives, up to 10 friends. The official Program Information (UK 2026) sets out only the mechanism and leaves the discount amount, minimum transfer and number of usable vouchers to the Refer and Earn page in the app, so no fixed figure is published — third-party deal sites (My Side Gig 2026-11-14 and coupon sites) all say £10. The new customer's discount doesn't expire, the existing customer's lasts 60 days, and neither can be combined with other promo codes; Plus Rewards members also get 50% off the second transfer's fee.

Conditions

A new customer is anyone who has never opened an online MoneyGram profile (people who only sent in a store still count as new); the transfer must be online or in the app, not in a store; the amount and minimum are whatever the Refer and Earn page shows; one voucher per transfer and no stacking with other promotions.

Steps

  1. Ask a friend who has used MoneyGram for their Refer and Earn link or code
  2. Register an online MoneyGram profile with that link and complete verification
  3. Confirm the discount is applied on your first online international transfer (card payments don't get the related fee waiver)
  4. Once it arrives, the existing customer's discount appears on their next transfer

Watch out

The amount isn't published — £10 comes from third parties, so confirm it in the app after registering; the discount covers only one transfer and can't be stacked; MoneyGram can change or end the programme at any time, and the FX spread is still there.
