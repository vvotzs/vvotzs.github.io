Highlight

Every individual paid subscriber (Pro, Pro+, Pro Max, Power) gets 10,000 bonus credits automatically on 2 December, usable with any model including Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol and Grok 4.7, valid through 31 January 2027

What you get

Kiro, AWS's AI coding IDE and CLI, announced on its official X account on 23 November 2026 that every individual paid subscriber gets an extra 10,000 bonus credits as a thank-you. They land automatically on 2 December with nothing to claim, work with any model and stay valid through 31 January 2027. At the official add-on price of $0.04 per credit that is about $400, the same as a month of allowance on the $200 Power plan. Current individual plans are Pro $20, Pro+ $40, Pro Max $100 and Power $200, billed on the first of each month. A first upgrade to Pro also comes with a free first month and 500 trial credits.

Conditions

You need an individual paid subscription from Pro at $20 a month; Free users do not qualify; the credits are credited automatically on 2 December and Kiro has not said which date's subscription status counts; team and enterprise accounts are not mentioned; the credits expire on 31 January 2027.

Eligibility

Funding
Paid plan required

Steps

  1. Pick an individual plan at kiro.dev/pricing (from Pro at $20 a month) and subscribe
  2. Sign in to Kiro after 2 December and confirm the 10,000 bonus credits in the balance
  3. Use any model, premium ones included, in the IDE or CLI before the 31 January 2027 expiry
  4. Cancel the renewal once the credits are used if you only wanted this round

Watch out

This is an X announcement only, with no matching note on the official blog or pricing page, so the details (which subscription date counts, whether team accounts share it) are unpublished; it is compensation for paying at least $20, not something free; the credits expire on 31 January 2027; model availability is restricted by country, as Kiro states some premium models are unavailable in some regions.
