Highlight

New users of the Doubao Work desktop app get a 30-day subscription with no card. Daily check-ins pay 200 credits (double on Mondays) with milestone bonuses, adding up to 8,800 over 30 days, more than the 8,400 a standard ¥68 plan includes.

What you get

Doubao Work, the AI agent office product under Doubao, shipped a 2026-11-12 update adding Kimi K3.1 Pro, GLM 5.3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash with credit multipliers shown per model, and a limited-time 0.01x rate on Doubao 2.1 Lite and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Alongside it Doubao offers two promotions: first-time users of the Doubao or Doubao Work desktop app can claim a 30-day free subscription covering everything except video generation and Pro models, and every user can check in daily for 200 credits, doubled to 400 on Mondays, with extra bonuses of 200, 500 and 1,300 credits at 500, 2,000 and 5,000 cumulative credits, totalling 8,800 over 30 consecutive days. Gift credits last 30 days, are spent first, and cannot be used on Pro models or video generation without a paid plan.

Conditions

The 30-day subscription is only for new users who first sign in on desktop, with no entry point on mobile. Check-ins happen once a day from the desktop pop-up or the avatar menu and are not made up if missed, and Mondays pay double. Gift credits expire after 30 days. Unpaid accounts cannot spend gift credits on Pro models or video generation. The 0.01x rate and discount windows are limited-time and undated.

Steps

  1. Download Doubao Work for Windows or macOS and sign in with a phone number
  2. Confirm the 30-day free subscription shows as active on the subscription page
  3. Check in daily from the bottom-left pop-up, remembering Monday doubles
  4. Run everyday tasks on the 0.01x models and use the gift credits within 30 days

Watch out

The promotion can change or end early, so the client's own display is the source of truth. Gift credits expire in 30 days and skip Pro models and video generation. A missed day delays milestone progress. The desktop entry point is China-only, so accounts elsewhere may not see it.
