What you get

A welcome campaign from True and Thailand's tourism authority: ASEAN and Indian passport holders first click How to Redeem at aseanindiashopper.com, answer a short survey and register for a voucher, then show it at a True dtac shop at Suvarnabhumi, Don Mueang, Phuket or another participating airport for a free True 5G tourist SIM with 8GB valid the same day and travel and shopping coupons worth over ฿10,000. It is a physical tourist SIM rather than an eSIM.

Conditions

ASEAN member and Indian passport holders only; register at aseanindiashopper.com (to 28 November 2026) and collect at a participating airport shop (to 31 December 2026); the 8GB lasts one day; Thai local data with no mainland China roaming; passport registration required, at most three SIMs per person per brand with each valid up to 60 days.
