Highlight

Meta's personal AI agent Muse comes with its own Linux computer in the cloud (Muse Secure VM) and a full browser, so it can compare prices, book, email and order by itself; new users who enter a friend's invite code in Settings within 48 hours of joining get 1,000,000,000 tokens each, on top of the free weekly allowance.

What you get

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, launched 2026-09-08, on iOS, Android, the web at muse.ai, Mac and WhatsApp. Every user gets a persistent Linux computer in the cloud (Muse Secure VM) with a full browser where the agent browses, fills forms and gets things done; sensitive actions such as sending email or paying need your approval first, and there is a complete audit trail. It connects to Gmail, Google Calendar, Instagram and more, and supports voice calls and custom Spaces. Pricing: free with a weekly usage limit; Power is $20/month (500M tokens a week, 10 calls a day) and Maximum $100/month (3B tokens a week, 20 calls a day); when the allowance runs out you can wait for the refresh or buy additional tokens (purchased and promotional tokens don't expire). Referral: on 2026-09-16 Meta's official @Muse account announced that you can invite up to 20 friends and that, once a friend onboards and redeems your code, each side gets 1 billion Muse tokens — up to 20 billion for the inviter. New users must redeem within 48 hours of creating the account; after that the option disappears and no code can be added.

Conditions

18 or older; must be located in a country where Muse is available (US, Canada, Japan, Singapore, Mexico, Australia); must be a new account and redeem within 48 hours of joining; one code per account; each code has a limited number of redemptions (Meta's copy says invite up to 20 friends; the exact per-code cap isn't published consistently).

Steps

  1. Sign up at muse.ai/join or in the Muse app with a Meta account (must be in a launched market; the web works)
  2. Within 48 hours of joining open Settings: web Settings → General → Usage → Redeem invite code; app Settings → Redeem token
  3. Enter your friend's invite code and confirm — each side gets 1 billion tokens, added on top of the weekly free allowance
  4. Later, share your own code from Invite friends: each friend who redeems gives you another 1 billion (up to 20; filling all 20 unlocks early access to new features)

Watch out

Meta's help centre does not publish whether bonus tokens expire, how they are drawn down against a subscription allowance, or an official dollar value for 1 billion tokens (the ~$9 in this listing is our arithmetic at the Power rate, so it is unverified). Each code has a limited number of redemptions and public codes are often used up. Redeem only inside Settings in the app or on muse.ai; anyone asking for your password, a code, card details or money to "activate" a code is scamming you. Missing the 48-hour window cannot be fixed. Meta has not published the free tier's exact weekly token figure (Zuckerberg said about 100 million a week; it is not in the help page).
