Highlight

Klook accounts in Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia or Vietnam can open a mystery data gift box (0.5–50GB), store it in the Data Bank to add to a later eSIM purchase, or pass it to 2–5 friends

What you get

Klook's official Data Bank campaign (2026-11-16 to 2026-12-31, or while gift boxes last): signed-in Klook users in qualifying markets can claim one mystery data gift box worth 0.5GB–50GB, with the larger amounts less likely; it's stored in the Data Bank as a "Gift from Klook", valid 180 days and resetting 180 days on each deposit. Claiming your own box unlocks 2–5 boxes to give to friends (0.5/1/3GB unlock 5 of the same size, 7GB unlocks 2×3.5GB, 15GB unlocks 3×5GB, 25GB unlocks 5×5GB, 50GB unlocks 5×10GB).

Conditions

Klook accounts resident in Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia or Vietnam only; one claim per account; Data Bank data must be added to a purchased eligible eSIM and can't be used as a standalone free eSIM; can't be combined with promo codes or vouchers on the same order; domestic-market eSIMs don't qualify; Argentina, Brunei, Central and South America, Colombia, Guam, Kenya, the Maldives, Mongolia, Nigeria, Panama, Greenland, Africa and Indian Ocean destinations aren't supported.

Steps

  1. Sign in with a qualifying Klook account and open the Data Bank campaign page on klook.com
  2. Tap a gift box to claim the mystery data, revealed on the spot and stored in the Data Bank
  3. When you next buy an eligible eSIM, add the Data Bank balance at checkout (gift data is used first)
  4. To give to friends, use "Gift a friend" on the campaign page; they must also be in a qualifying market and can receive only one

Watch out

The box is random and mostly small (0.5–3GB); you must buy an eSIM to use it, so it isn't free data on its own; residency is decided by the Klook profile and mainland Chinese accounts don't see the entry; the data isn't cash, can't be withdrawn and disappears if unused for 180 days.
