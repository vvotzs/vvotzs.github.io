Highlight

A virtual card costs 1 USDT to open and the system refunds the 1 USDT immediately, so the card is free; USDT top-ups on TRC-20, BEP-20 and ERC-20 all carry 0% fees, there is no monthly or annual fee, and referrals pay up to 50% (a limited-time offer with no stated end date)

What you get

UUPAY is a Hong Kong-registered crypto payment platform (Enterprise Square Three, Kowloon Bay) issuing USDT-funded Visa and Mastercard virtual and physical cards. Its official blog and promotions page describe the current offer: a new user opens a virtual card for 1 USDT and the system returns 1 USDT, making the card free; top-up fees are waived (TRC-20 in about a minute, BEP-20 in about 30 seconds, ERC-20 in 3-5 minutes) and the currency conversion fee is waived during the promotion; referring friends pays up to 50% commission. U Card Radar (updated 2026-09-20) rates it tier D with a risk score of 40-49: virtual card 1 USDT with 1 USDT back and no monthly fee; physical card a 100 USDT issuance fee; transaction fees 0.9%-3%; ATM withdrawals 2.50 euros for sterling and 3 euros plus 1.5% for other currencies.

Conditions

Tiered KYC with an ID card, passport or driving licence and sometimes proof of address; the 1 USDT refund is once per new user; 0% top-up fees are a promotional policy with no published end date; whether the virtual card binds to Apple or Google Wallet and which merchants accept it depends on platform risk checks; the 100 USDT physical card does not qualify for the refund.

Steps

  1. Register with an email or phone number and complete KYC
  2. Apply for the virtual card on the card page and pay the 1 USDT opening fee
  3. Confirm the 1 USDT came back, then make a small USDT top-up to test
  4. Bind a subscription you know is accepted, confirm it charges, and only then keep a balance

Watch out

Crypto card platforms carry high risk (tier D on U Card Radar), so do not park large balances; funding depends on exchanges and chain congestion; KYC and merchant risk checks can tighten at any time; the "Black Friday" framing comes from the submission while the official page only says limited time, so the end date is unverified.
