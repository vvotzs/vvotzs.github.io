Highlight

Manage eSIM profiles through a card reader and call or text over Wi-Fi Calling on iOS, macOS and Android, now free to sideload from GitHub. The iOS build left the App Store on 7 Oct 2026, so the developer posts IPA, APK and macOS packages in its place; the card ledger and expiry reminders work without any reader.

What you get

A SIM and eSIM card manager for iOS/iPadOS 17+, macOS 14+ and Android 8+. With a USB CCID reader (USB OTG on Android) or a 9eSIM B0/B1 Bluetooth reader attached, it reads the EID and ICCID, enables, disables, renames and deletes profiles, downloads new ones from a QR code or activation code, and handles pending card notifications; 5ber, Xesim, eSTK, 9eSIM and multi-chip cards such as eSTK.me Max are supported. A card in the reader can place and take calls and send and receive SMS over IKEv2/IPsec and IMS, and a reader or module on a Mac or NAS can be lent to an iPhone over the local network or Tailscale. Pricing: SIMHub X still sells for $2.99 on Google Play with no ads and no in-app purchases listed; the iOS build has been off the App Store since 7 Oct 2026 pending an appeal, and the developer now offers a 29.3 IPA, a 29.22 macOS package and a 23.1 APK free on GitHub.

Conditions

Reader and Wi-Fi Calling features need a compatible CCID reader (USB OTG on Android) or a 9eSIM B0/B1 Bluetooth reader plus a writable eSIM card such as eSTK, 9eSIM, 5ber or Xesim, and the carrier must have Wi-Fi Calling enabled; iOS 17+ is required (older devices must use SIMKit); a free Apple ID signature expires every 7 days and has to be refreshed.

Steps

  1. Open the Releases page of the maow318/simhubforum repository on GitHub and download the package for your platform: SIMHub-29.3.ipa for iOS/iPadOS, SIMHub-macOS-29.22.zip for macOS, SIMHub-Android-23.1.apk for Android
  2. On iOS, re-sign the IPA with your own Apple ID using AltStore, Sideloadly or a similar tool and install it; a free Apple ID needs re-signing every 7 days, and app data stays on the device
  3. On macOS, unzip it, move SIMHub.app to Applications and open it (it is notarized); on Android, allow installing unknown apps and open the APK, with no re-signing needed
  4. Attach a reader, read the EID and profile list, then download a new profile by scanning a QR code or entering an activation code; for Wi-Fi Calling pick the carrier profile and test with a number already on the card

Watch out

The iOS build has been off the App Store since 7 Oct 2026 and the appeal is unresolved, so it cannot be downloaded there again and only a self-signed IPA works; a free Apple ID signature expires after 7 days and the app stops opening until it is refreshed; the Android build does not support emergency calls and blocks calls to +86 numbers; whether Wi-Fi Calling registers depends on the carrier having enabled it; this is closed proprietary software and the licence forbids redistributing the packages, commercial use and removing branding, so keep it to lawful personal use; the Play build and the GitHub package are signed differently and cannot update each other, so export a backup before switching channels.
