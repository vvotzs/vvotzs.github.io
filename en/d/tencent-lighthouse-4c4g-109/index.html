What you get

Tencent Cloud's Lighthouse sale page (read 2026-11-20) leads with "4-core 4 GB 3 Mbps Lighthouse, 109 yuan for a new customer's first year", running until 30 December 2026 for mainland accounts that have completed real-name verification. Other tiers on the same page: the flash sale 4-core 4 GB 3 Mbps with 300 GB traffic at 109 yuan a year (first order for the product, one unit); 2-core 2 GB 3 Mbps at 68 yuan; 2-core 4 GB 6 Mbps at 199 yuan with the same price on renewal (once); 2-core 2 GB 4 Mbps at 99 yuan for a year plus three months. At the official price list an entry tier 2-core 4 GB 5 Mbps is 65 yuan a month (about 663 yuan a year with the 15% annual discount), so 109 yuan for a 4-core 4 GB year saves roughly 550 yuan. NodeSeek also carried a separate "Thursday community flash sale": 2-core 2 GB 5 Mbps at 129 yuan a year (list 660 yuan), open to new and existing customers, every Thursday at 18:00.

Conditions

Only for a first order on the product (the account has never bought Lighthouse, including refunded or released instances), once per real-name entity; cannot be combined with coupons or vouchers; payment within 60 minutes; renewal is at list price, not this rate.

Steps

  1. Confirm the account passed real-name verification and has never bought Lighthouse
  2. Open cloud.tencent.com/act/pro/lhsale and take the 4-core 4 GB 3 Mbps tier
  3. Choose a region (Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou) and an image, then confirm 109 yuan a year
  4. Set up the firewall and a snapshot, and plan whether to renew at list price

Watch out

The "first order" rule is strict, so any past Lighthouse purchase (even refunded) disqualifies the account; flash-sale stock is limited and sells out; renewals revert to list price, so weigh the long-term cost; 3 Mbps is a peak bandwidth that is throttled at busy times.
