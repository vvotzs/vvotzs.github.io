Highlight

The Lighthouse sale page puts a 4-core 4 GB 3 Mbps instance with 300 GB of monthly traffic at 109 yuan for a new customer's first year; 2-core 2 GB 3 Mbps is 68 yuan a year, 2-core 4 GB 6 Mbps is 199 yuan with the same price on renewal, and 2-core 2 GB 4 Mbps is 99 yuan for 15 months, all until 30 December 2026

What you get

A Lighthouse server with 4 cores, 4GB and 3Mbps for 300GB of monthly traffic costs ¥109 for the first year as a new customer's first order (one unit); other tiers are 2 cores/2GB/3Mbps at ¥68 a year, 2 cores/4GB/6Mbps at ¥199 a year with the same price on renewal (once), and 2 cores/2GB/4Mbps at ¥99 a year with three months free. The official price list puts entry 2-core/4GB/5Mbps at ¥65 a month (about ¥663 a year), so ¥109 for a year of 4 cores and 4GB saves roughly ¥550 in year one. A separate Thursday community flash sale offers 2 cores/2GB/5Mbps at ¥129 a year (list ¥660, open to existing customers too) every Thursday at 18:00.

Conditions

First-order new customers only (the account has never bought a Lighthouse server, including refunded or released ones), once per verified identity; no coupons or vouchers; pay within 60 minutes; renewal is not at the same price, so year two bills at list; the offer runs to 30 December 2026.

Steps

  1. Confirm the account passed real-name verification and has never bought Lighthouse
  2. Open cloud.tencent.com/act/pro/lhsale and take the 4-core 4 GB 3 Mbps tier
  3. Choose a region (Beijing, Shanghai or Guangzhou) and an image, then confirm 109 yuan a year
  4. Set up the firewall and a snapshot, and plan whether to renew at list price

Watch out

The "first order" rule is strict, so any past Lighthouse purchase (even refunded) disqualifies the account; flash-sale stock is limited and sells out; renewals revert to list price, so weigh the long-term cost; 3 Mbps is a peak bandwidth that is throttled at busy times.
