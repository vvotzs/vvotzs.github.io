Highlight

New UK customers get £50 LemFi Cash with a referral code, and the inviter gets £50 once the friend sends a single transfer of £50+; it can't be withdrawn — instead £5 comes off each £50+ transfer, up to 3 a month

What you get

LemFi's official Invite and earn: a new customer who signs up with a referral code or link gets £50 LemFi Cash, and once the invitee sends a single transfer of £50 or more to any supported country the inviter gets £50 too. The official referral note (2026-10-21) states that European registrations must send outside Europe. The £50 isn't cash: the official terms give £5 off each transfer of £50 or more, up to 3 a month and non-withdrawable — at most £15 a month, used up over about 3–4 months; the account itself charges no transfer fee.

Conditions

New LemFi customers only; a single transfer of £50 or more; the referral code must be entered at signup and can't be your own referral; LemFi Cash can't be withdrawn or transferred, gives £5 off each £50+ transfer, 3 a month.

Steps

  1. Download the LemFi app, register with the referral code and complete passport/ID KYC
  2. Fund the account and send an international transfer of £50 or more (e.g. to your own Wise euro account)
  3. See the £50 in your LemFi Cash balance; each £50+ transfer afterwards takes £5 off
  4. To refer others, share your code — you get £50 once their first transfer qualifies

Watch out

The "£50" is credit, not cash, and you can't take it in one go; real value depends on whether you'll send more transfers. Sites such as Budgetfitter advertise code FATILPG7 for "£10 cash straight to your balance", which doesn't match the official LemFi Cash terms — trust the app; FX spread is still charged.
