What you get

The National Supercomputing Internet (SCNet) MaaS campaign, extended to 31 December 2026, gives users who register for the first time during the campaign a 10M-token welcome pack (officially shown as GLM-5.3-Flash with 1024K context, credited instantly, valid 30 days, one per account, while stock lasts). A first-order deal sells ¥0.1 packs at 10:00 daily: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 50M tokens (list ¥20.50), GLM-5.3-Flash 50M (list ¥17.00), DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash 20M and Qwen3.8-Flash 10M, one purchase per account in total. Endpoints are OpenAI-compatible (api.scnet.cn/api/llm/v1) and Anthropic-compatible, and work with Claude Code, Cursor, Cline, RooCode, OpenCode, Codex and OpenClaw.

Conditions

Only accounts first registered between 13 Aug and 31 Dec 2026; phone sign-up with real-name verification; gifted tokens expire 30 days after credit and do not roll over; multiple accounts of the same person are merged and duplicates count as abuse; the ¥0.1 packs open at 10:00 daily with limited stock.

Steps

  1. Register and sign in at scnet.cn with a phone number (first registration during the campaign)
  2. Open the MaaS campaign page and claim the 10M-token welcome pack
  3. Optionally grab a ¥0.1 50M-token pack at 10:00 each day
  4. Create an API key under Console → Model Service → API Key management (shown once)
  5. Point your client's base_url at https://api.scnet.cn/api/llm/v1

Watch out

The welcome tokens expire after 30 days, so claim them when you will use them; the model granted can change with supply; when gifted tokens run out the platform charges your cash balance without a prompt, so be careful if you have topped up; the daily ¥0.1 stock is limited.
