What you get

AMD and ModelScope's joint developer programme: complete AMD Developer Program (ADP) registration and authorise the ModelScope account link, and after review 100 hours of AMD GPU credit lands automatically in ModelScope's "My Notebook" with an extra "AMD GPU environment" row. The machine has 23 CPU cores, 200GB of RAM and 192GB of VRAM (MI300X or MI308X, gfx942), with ROCm 7.2.3 and PyTorch 2.12 preinstalled — enough to benchmark open models around the 27B–Qwen3.6 class. Hours stack: an original technical post in the study group adds 25 hours (max 50), a Notebook walkthrough in the inspiration feed adds 50 (max 150) and an approved Space deployment adds 700+. The global AMD Developer Program also gives new members $100 of AMD Developer Cloud credit (single-MI300X instances).

Conditions

Only newly registered AMD Developer Program (ADP) users — existing members don't get it again; the form needs a real name, province or city, field and a one-line use case and is reviewed (usually minutes to days); after it lands you must link an Alibaba Cloud account with real-name verification and a slider check before starting; community testing puts a single session at about 8 hours with only /mnt/workspace kept on shutdown (not an official written promise); instances and data are cleared when the entitlement expires or runs out; sign-ups close 2026-12-31 and the campaign runs to 2027-01-30.

Steps

  1. Log in to modelscope.cn with a +86 number, open My Notebook and click the AMD banner at the top
  2. It redirects to developer.amd.com.cn, where one click authorises your ModelScope account for the AMD Developer Program
  3. Fill in name, email, phone, individual developer, province or city, field and use case, then submit with the email and SMS codes
  4. Back in My Notebook, confirm the "AMD GPU environment · 100 hours left" row appears

Watch out
