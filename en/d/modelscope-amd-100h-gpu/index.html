Highlight

Register for the AMD Developer Program and link your ModelScope account for 100 hours of AMD GPU Notebook after approval: MI300X/MI308X, 192GB HBM3 per card, 23 cores / 200GB RAM, billed only while running

What you get

Register for AMD's AI Developer Program and link it to a ModelScope account, and once approved 100 hours of AMD GPU credit lands in My Notebook with an AMD GPU environment row added. Machines have 23 CPU cores, 200GB of RAM and 192GB of VRAM (MI300X or MI308X, gfx942) with ROCm 7.2.3 and PyTorch 2.12 preinstalled, enough to run 27B to Qwen3.6-class open models. Extra hours stack: 25 per original technical article in the study group (up to 50), 50 per Notebook write-up in the inspiration stream (up to 150) and 700+ for an approved app deployment in the creation space. AMD's global developer programme separately gives new members $100 of Developer Cloud credit (one MI300X).

Conditions

New AI Developer Program registrations only, with no repeat grant to existing members; the form needs a real name, province or city, field and a one-line purpose, then review (minutes to days); starting an instance also needs an Alibaba Cloud account with real-name and slider checks; community reports put a single session at about eight hours with only /mnt/workspace kept on shutdown (not in the official terms); instances and data are cleared when the credit expires or runs out; register by 31 December 2026, with the campaign running to 30 January 2027.

Steps

  1. Log in to modelscope.cn with a +86 number, open My Notebook and click the AMD banner at the top
  2. It redirects to developer.amd.com.cn, where one click authorises your ModelScope account for the AMD Developer Program
  3. Fill in name, email, phone, individual developer, province or city, field and use case, then submit with the email and SMS codes
  4. Back in My Notebook, confirm the "AMD GPU environment · 100 hours left" row appears
  5. Link Alibaba Cloud with real-name verification before launching; mind the 8-hour limit and keep code and models in /mnt/workspace

Watch out

It's an application, so the only proof is the AMD GPU environment row appearing in My Notebook; peak times can fail to start with a resource warning (no hours charged); claiming from multiple accounts can be treated as abuse; don't keep important data only on the instance.
