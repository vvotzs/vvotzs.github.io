What you get

Hoody sells Linux hosts where each cloud computer is reachable at its own URL; the free tier is $0/mo, needs no card and doesn't expire: up to 3 computers sharing 2 CPU cores, 3GB of RAM, 20GB of storage and 10Mbps, with 2 snapshots each, plus 5 .hoody.bot bot domains, 5 Realms and a free Hoody AI allowance. With root and systemd you can run code, a browser, a GUI desktop, scheduled jobs and HTTP services (bind a port and the URL is published), and mount cloud storage (S3, Google Drive, Dropbox, WebDAV) for backups. Good for practice, small scripts, a temporary site or browser automation. The paid Plus tier at $10/mo gives 12 computers (3 cores/6GB/75GB, 8 regions).

Conditions

The free allowance is shared across all three computers; hosts are shared with others and capacity is limited, so the region chosen at signup is only a preference; no virtual machines (that needs the paid Bare Metal tier); the models and availability of the Hoody AI allowance aren't guaranteed; activity and capacity limits can change at any time.

Steps

  1. Open hoody.com and sign up (no credit card needed)
  2. Claim the free server and open the web terminal or log in over SSH
  3. Install dependencies and run scripts; for a public service, bind the port and the URL is published
  4. Sync important files to external storage so they aren't lost when the instance is cleaned up

Watch out

The free tier has no SLA on shared hosts, so resources can be reclaimed or changed at any time; don't keep the only copy of important data there; the platform is new and its terms and free policy can change.
