Highlight

From 18 Nov 2026, households with a 5–18 year old signing up or porting to Y!mobile Simple 3 M/L get ¥1,000 (¥1,100 with tax) off the monthly fee for 12 months, stackable with the PayPay Card discount (including a ¥200 boost) and the Hikari set discount, taking Simple 3 M (30GB) as low as ¥858 a month

What you get

From 18 November 2026, a 5-18 year-old or a family member starts or ports to Y!mobile Simple 3 M/L and the monthly bill drops ¥1,000 (¥1,100 with tax) for 12 months from the month after applying. A PayPay Card boost from the same date raises the PayPay Card discount from ¥300 to ¥500 a month (¥550 with tax) for 5-18s and their families, and the two combine: Simple 3 M with the Ouchi Wari Hikari Set (A) comes to ¥780 a month (¥858 with tax), returning to ¥1,958 from month 14. The separate Y!mobile SIM campaign 5 pays up to ¥15,000 in PayPay for a SIM-only switch, but its stacking and exclusions differ, so check the application page.

Conditions

Live in Japan with ID verification; the user is 5-18 (family members share the boost); a new line or MNP port-in on Simple 3 M/L; the 12-month discount runs from the month after applying and list price returns in month 14; the PayPay Card discount needs payment by PayPay Card with PayPay ID verification and a linked Yahoo! JAPAN ID; ports from SoftBank, LINEMO and LINE Mobile fall outside the SIM cashback.

Steps

  1. Prepare ID for the 5–18 year old user and a payment method
  2. Order Simple 3 M/L as a new line or MNP on Y!mobile's official online store or Yahoo! store
  3. Link the PayPay Card and Yahoo! JAPAN ID as prompted
  4. After activation confirm the bill drops by ¥1,100 with tax from the next month
  5. Apply separately for the Hikari set discount or SIM cashback if you want them stacked

Watch out

The discount lasts only 12 months and the price returns to normal from month 14, so it is poor value long term; the SIM cashback conflicts with some older campaigns and excludes past Y!mobile online-store PayPay recipients; a PayPay Card is a credit card, so decide whether it is worth opening one for this.
