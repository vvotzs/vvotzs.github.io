Highlight

One MCP URL plugs into Claude / Claude Code / Cursor / VS Code / Windsurf / n8n and gives it live web search, breaking world news, full article text and papers, from its own index, on a flat monthly price rather than per search.

What you get

NestDaddy is an MCP server built to give agents a live internet (launched on Product Hunt in November 2026). It runs its own search index instead of reselling another provider's results and exposes nine read-only tools: live web search, real-time breaking news from around the world, full article text and research papers. Get a key, then add https://mcp.nestdaddy.com/mcp as a Streamable HTTP server — in Claude via Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, with claude mcp add in a terminal, or in Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf and n8n. After that, a question like "what happened in AI this week?" makes the agent search, read the sources and answer with links instead of guessing from a training cut-off. Pricing: free is 1,000 credits a month, no card, no time limit; paid plans start at $19/month on a flat monthly price with no per-search bills; the launch code PH50 takes 50% off any paid plan for three months until 2026-11-24.

Conditions

The free tier gives 1,000 credits a month with no card and no trial timer. Credits are consumed by calls and fetches, and pulling full articles costs more than a single search. PH50 works only on a key claimed at the official mcp page before 2026-11-24. Read-only: it searches, fetches and reads, but does not operate pages for you.

Steps

  1. Register at nestdaddy.com/mcp for a free key (1,000 credits a month, no card)
  2. Add a custom MCP connector in your client: https://mcp.nestdaddy.com/mcp?key=YOUR_KEY (or an Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY header)
  3. Ask directly, e.g. "what new free APIs launched in the AI news today?" or "what method does this paper use?"
  4. If you want a paid plan, use PH50 before 11/24 for 50% off three months
  5. Track credit use on the dashboard so full-article fetches do not drain the month in one sitting

Watch out

1,000 credits look generous until you pull full articles and poll news, which burn them fast. PH50 ends 2026-11-24. The site does not say whether unused free credits roll over or reset monthly. Results come from its own index, so obscure long-tail sites may be covered less thoroughly than Google.
