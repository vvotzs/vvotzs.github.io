Peacock is back with a Black Friday sale: Select at $2.99/month for 3 months or $29.99 a year (from $89.99, about 66% off), Premium at $8.99/month for 3 months or $89.99 a year (from $129.99); ends 11/30 and only for people not currently paying

What you get

Peacock returns to Black Friday this year. The Select tier (NBC and Bravo shows, without sport, films or Peacock originals) is $2.99/month for 3 months or $29.99 for the first year against $89.99, about 66% off; Premium (Premier League, NFL, NBA plus originals) is $8.99/month for 3 months or $89.99 for the first year against $129.99, 30% off. It runs from 11/23 to 11/30 and is limited to people not currently paying. The public homepage still shows list prices, so confirm the Black Friday price at checkout (unverified as of 25 November 2026). Anyone with Walmart+ already has Peacock Premium included (either Peacock or Paramount+, not both), so no need to buy it again.

Conditions
