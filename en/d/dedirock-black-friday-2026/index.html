The Black Friday page lists nine annual plans at the same price in LA and NY: Promo VPS Saver at $9.88 a year (1 vCore, 1GB, 10GB SSD, 1TB, 1Gbps), Economy at $17.68 (1/2GB/20GB/2TB) and Value at $27.68 (2/3GB/40GB/3TB), plus storage boxes: Storage Promo Starter at $11.88 (256GB disk, 512MB, 1TB), Essentials at $17.68 (1TB disk, 1GB, 2TB) and Plus at $27.68 (2TB disk, 2GB, 4TB).
