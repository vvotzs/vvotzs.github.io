Highlight

csl's build-your-own prepaid card: no monthly fee and a free eSIM ordered online, with a +852 number once the first order reaches HKD 50; after that one HKD 15 Asia pass within 365 days adds another year, and mainland China receives SMS free

What you get

A build-your-own prepaid card from csl: order an eSIM online without using a SIM slot, pay no monthly fee and keep the number under Hong Kong stored-value rules, where any purchase within 365 days of the last one extends validity by another year. The old HK$6 SMS pack retired on 30 June 2026 with no return planned, leaving the HK$15 24-hour Asia roaming pass as the cheapest renewal, so a year of upkeep rose from HK$6 to HK$15. New cards need a first order of HK$50 in total, with eSIM free, and the usual trick is HK$38 of mainland and Macau data for 90 days plus the HK$15 Asia pass, HK$53 on one card. Incoming texts are free worldwide, so codes arrive in the mainland without roaming; there are no calls or outgoing texts, and data needs the roaming packs (1GB for HK$38 or 3GB for HK$88 in the mainland and Macau, both 90 days). Real-name registration takes a passport or travel permit, not a mainland ID, photographed in the app, and eSIM and physical SIM can swap in the app (HK$40 per extra swap within a year).

Conditions

Order an eSIM online with a first order of HK$50+ (eSIM issuance free); local service needs real-name registration with a passport or permit, not a mainland ID, and without it there are no calls or texts; keep it by buying any pack within 365 days, now from the HK$15 Asia pass; packs carry their own validity (90 days) separate from the number; the buffer after expiry is short.

Steps

  1. On clubsim.com.hk choose an eSIM and packs totalling at least HKD 50, for example mainland and Macao 1GB at HKD 38 plus the HKD 15 Asia pass
  2. Install the QR code from the confirmation email on a native eSIM phone or an external eUICC
  3. Open the Club SIM app and register with a passport or mainland travel permit, then wait for approval
  4. Note the last pack date plus 365 days in a calendar and buy a HKD 15 pass before it comes round

Watch out

Forgetting the yearly renewal loses the number for good, and every bank or platform tied to it must be changed. The HKD 6 era is over and the yearly cost is now HKD 15. In the mainland it only receives SMS, with no calls or outgoing messages, and data is a separate purchase. Registration is mandatory and a mainland ID is not accepted, so without a passport or travel permit the card is out of reach, and spot checks can follow. Hong Kong numbers have a poor record on AI platforms such as ChatGPT. Packs are non-refundable once ordered.
