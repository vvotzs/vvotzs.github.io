Highlight

New customers activate a 30-day trial over eSIM in the AT&T app: 100GB of data, 25GB of personal hotspot, unlimited calls and texts across the US, Canada and Mexico and full 5G access, with no number port-in and no credit card

What you get

New customers check handset compatibility in the app and activate a trial eSIM, keeping their existing number while using AT&T for 30 days: 100GB of data, 25GB of hotspot, unlimited calls and texts between the US, Canada and Mexico, unlimited texts from the US to 200+ countries and full 5G access. AT&T says no credit card is needed, and you can switch or stop at any time during the trial.

Conditions

New AT&T customers only; an unlocked eSIM phone (iPhone XR or later, or a compatible Android); once per person and device; the trial does not roll into a paid plan automatically; some areas and network types are limited, subject to the in-app eligibility check; US carriers run eUICC EID allowlists, so third-party physical cards need IMEI and EID registered with support first.

Steps

  1. Download the myAT&T app from the App Store or Google Play (or scan the code at att.com/wireless/free-trial/)
  2. Tap "Start your trial" and confirm the phone is unlocked and eSIM-compatible
  3. Create a simple profile and activate the trial eSIM, noting the trial number assigned
  4. Set the AT&T line as the data and calling line in your phone settings to use the 100GB plus 25GB hotspot
  5. Decide before the trial ends whether to port in; if not, delete the eSIM line

Watch out

You need a US network environment and US account details, so non-US users may fail eligibility; the eSIM needs an unlocked phone and carrier-financed handsets usually don't qualify; the trial number can't be ported out and service stops when the trial ends; AT&T can change or end the trial at any time.
