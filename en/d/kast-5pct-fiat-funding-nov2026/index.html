Highlight

From 00:00 UTC on 2026-11-18 to 23:59 UTC on 2026-12-09, new users or invited existing ones who fund KAST by fiat get 5% back on the total, one reward per person capped at $15 (BRL Pix, COP Bre-B and ARS are capped at $10); the pool is $50,000 and ends early when it runs out

What you get

KAST's official blog post of 2026-11-18 and its campaign terms say that between 18 November and 9 December 2026 (UTC) eligible users who send fiat into KAST and convert it to their USD spending balance receive a 5% promotional reward on the total, paid once per person up to $15 overall, with per-route caps: $10 for BRL Pix, COP Bre-B and ARS, and $15 for EUR over SEPA, GBP over Faster Payments, and USD over ACH, FedNow, Fedwire or SWIFT. The reward is calculated after fees, the pool is $50,000 and is paid out in the order transfers complete, and rewards arrive in the KAST Reward Balance within 14 days after the campaign ends with no claim needed. SEPA and Faster Payments or ACH are the sensible routes: SEPA has 0% FX and no fixed fee, so a $300 transfer earns the full $15; Faster Payments and ACH charge $2; Fedwire charges a flat $15 and SWIFT a flat $30, which eats the reward. Do not use those two. Eligibility: users who sign up during the campaign, or existing users selected and invited by KAST who have never funded by fiat or last did so at least 45 days before the start, with selection confirmed by email or in the app; KYC is required; each qualifying transfer must be at least $50 equivalent; card and Apple Pay top-ups, crypto transfers, KAST Tag receipts, refunded and reversed transfers do not count.

Conditions

KYC and a healthy account are required; new users or invited existing ones, since meeting the funding criteria does not select existing users; each transfer must be at least $50 equivalent and must land during the campaign; one reward per person, capped at $15 (lower on some routes); card, crypto, Tag and MXN transfers are excluded; the offer cannot stack with another offer on the same transfer; the reward is promotional, not interest, and does not refund fees.
