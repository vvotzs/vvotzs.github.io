Create a first Public Cloud project and pay at least $0.99 to receive $200 within 30 days; a first Managed Databases purchase adds $100. The credit lasts 90 days and covers Compute, Local Zones, object storage and managed Kubernetes

What you get

OVHcloud's US Black Friday offer: create a first Public Cloud project and pay at least $0.99 to get $200 in credit within 30 days, plus another $100 when you buy a first Managed Databases service, for up to $300. The credit is valid for 90 days from issue and applies automatically to bills for Compute instances, Local Zones, object storage, managed Kubernetes and managed databases; VPS and dedicated servers do not qualify. The global site runs a similar offer with code FREETRIAL and one month of validity.

Conditions

The $0.99 is an anti-fraud pre-authorisation that appears on the first bill; only accounts that never bought OVHcloud Public Cloud qualify; a valid payment method is required; US credit lasts 90 days, global credit one month.

Steps

  1. Sign in or register an OVHcloud account and add a payment method
  2. Create a Public Cloud project in the control panel (the $0.99 line appears at checkout)
  3. Pay the $0.99 and wait for the credit, up to 30 days
  4. Buy a first Managed Databases service for the extra $100
  5. Use the credit within 90 days

Watch out

The credit can take up to 30 days to land, so early usage is billed; VPS and dedicated servers are excluded; validity differs between the US and global sites; overage is billed at standard rates immediately.
