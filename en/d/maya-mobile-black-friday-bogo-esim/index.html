What you get

Maya Mobile's Black Friday offer runs from November 23, 0:00 EST to December 2, 0:00 EST: buy a 14-day ($27.99) or 30-day ($49.99) plan on the website or in the app and, once you land and activate the first plan, Maya emails a code for the same plan free. The free plan is for your next trip, must be redeemed within three months and works only on the same Maya account, never on the order that earned it. The 3-day and 7-day plans, purchases through resellers or marketplaces, and other promo codes do not qualify.

Conditions

Buy the 14-day or 30-day plan directly on the site or app; the free code is only issued after the purchased plan is activated; redeem within three months and only on a later order; one per account; 3 and 7-day plans, resellers and other promo codes are excluded.

Steps

  1. Pick the 14-day ($27.99) or 30-day ($49.99) plan on maya.net or in the Maya app
  2. Activate and use that plan when you land
  3. Watch for the free plan code in your email or the app after activation is confirmed

Watch out
