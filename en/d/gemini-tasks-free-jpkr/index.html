Highlight

Flip the toggle in Gemini from Chat to Task and give it multi-step, recurring work: a morning brief across Gmail and Calendar, a trip itinerary, long-running research — it runs in the background and tells you when it's done. Free in Japan, Korea, the US, Canada, Australia and India since 2026-11-17.

What you get

On 2026-11-17 Google renamed the Gemini Spark personal agent to Tasks in Gemini and made it free for hundreds of millions of users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India — previously it required Google AI Pro or Ultra. Open the Gemini app, flip the input toggle from Chat to Task and describe the job in plain language: Gemini works across Gmail, Calendar and 60+ connected apps to run multi-step tasks (a morning brief of what matters, a trip itinerary, recurring research, tracking subscription perks), supports recurring schedules, and notifies you when results are ready; you can jump in with more instructions while it works. Each task runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine dedicated to your account on Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine, with prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limiting the links it may open, and it is designed to ask for confirmation before critical steps such as clicking Buy (you can set routine steps to "Always allow"). US-first extras: calling businesses to check quotes, stock or appointments; watching for restocks and checking out with Google Pay under a price cap you set; starting tasks by forwarding mail to gemini@google.com or texting Gemini.

Conditions

Needs an 18+ user, a personal Google account and Web & App Activity history turned on. Works in the Gemini app on iOS, Android and Mac and on the web. In Japan and Korea only the task-running part is live; phone calls, Google Pay checkout and text/email task entry are US-only. Tasks can still fail or miss the mark.

Steps

  1. Update the Gemini app (iOS / Android / Mac or web) and confirm you're signed in with a personal Google account, 18+, with history on
  2. Flip the toggle above the input box from Chat to Task
  3. Describe the job: "Every morning at 7, list the three things in my mail and calendar that can't slip" or "Plan next week's Osaka trip with transport and restaurants"
  4. Let it run in the background and open the task to watch progress or add instructions; it pauses to ask before important steps
  5. Check the result when it notifies you, then turn recurring jobs into scheduled tasks

Watch out

In Japan and Korea only the task-running core is open — phone calls, Google Pay orders and text/email task entry stay US-only, so don't expect the US demo. Tasks read your mail, calendar and connected apps, so start with small jobs before granting anything that spends money. Gemini still fails and misreads instructions; confirm before it buys or books. It requires activity history, which leaves a record.
