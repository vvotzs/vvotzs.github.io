Highlight

It produces finished files rather than chat: PowerPoint, PDF, websites, brand kits, fonts and video. The app is free, and free of charge entirely if you already pay for Claude or ChatGPT.

What you get

Tesseract from Eightcell Labs launched on Product Hunt on 2026-11-14. It does not chat, it delivers: ask for an investor deck for a bakery and it shows a plan, does the work and hands back an editable PowerPoint or PDF. The same flow builds websites from 220+ templates and 14 themes, logos and brand kits, fonts, video, spreadsheets and sourced research reports, all saved to your computer. Its pricing is the interesting part. There is no subscription and no credit pack; it drives the AI subscription you already have — Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, Cursor Agent, Kimi Code, Qwen Code and more — signed in with your own account. The site says that with a Claude or ChatGPT plan it costs nothing on top and never meters your usage, and it moves to the next account when one runs out. Without any AI subscription you start on a free allowance that refills, with prepaid credit from $10 for premium models.

Conditions

Free in full only if you already hold a Claude / ChatGPT-class subscription (its own limits still apply). Without one you get the official free allowance, whose size is not published (unverified). Desktop only, no mobile app.

Steps

  1. Download the build for your OS at tesseract.dev (no credit card)
  2. On first launch pick the agent you already use and sign in, or start on the free allowance
  3. Pick one of the 33 guided makers or describe the job in one sentence and collect the editable files

Watch out

The free allowance size is unpublished. A desktop agent reads and writes local files, so try it in an empty folder first. Third-party agent CLIs may need their own authorisation.
