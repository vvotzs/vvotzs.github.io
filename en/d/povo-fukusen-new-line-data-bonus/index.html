What you get

povo2.0 (au network) second-line sign-up campaign, running since June 19, 2026 with no announced end: sign up new, or port in from au, UQ mobile, povo1.0 or another carrier, enter the campaign code AUKAITEKI on the contract application screen, and finish SIM activation during the campaign period. You get 0.1GB for 24 hours (applied right after activation) and ten 24-hour unlimited-data promo codes sent to the registered email, which must be entered by the end of the month after the SIM activation date. The monthly fee is ¥0 with no contract term and no activation fee (from a sixth line under the same name it is ¥3,850 per line). The data-only plan is eSIM only and needs one topping purchase at sign-up; the voice-plus-data plan has no such requirement.

Conditions

Aged 13 or over with Japanese ID and address; the code must be entered on the application screen and the SIM activated within the campaign window (activating between 9:30 and 20:00 is recommended); only the first line under one name counts; promo codes must be entered by the end of the month after activation; the data-only plan is eSIM only.

Steps

  1. Install the povo2.0 app, tap the new-customer option and enter AUKAITEKI in the code field to confirm the data you get
  2. Complete the new sign-up or port-in and finish SIM activation within the campaign window
  3. Open Promo Code at the bottom of the Home tab and enter each of the ten 24-hour unlimited codes from the email
  4. Use each pass within its own 24 hours; they do not accumulate

Watch out

Promo codes expire without replacement, so enter them before the end of the month after activation; porting in needs the old line cancelled with an MNP reservation; unlimited passes are throttled at busy times; a line with no paid purchase for 180 days can be suspended or cancelled; this is a Japanese local plan, not a travel eSIM, and cannot roam abroad including mainland China.
