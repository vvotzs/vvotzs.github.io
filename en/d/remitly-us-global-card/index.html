Highlight

A Lead Bank Visa debit card on a Stripe account that can hold USD or USDC, with no monthly or foreign transaction fees; a first qualifying $300 payroll deposit pays a bonus about seven business days later.

What you get

Remitly launched the Global Card on 2026-07-30: a Lead Bank Visa debit card on a Stripe Payments Company account with funds at Fifth Third Bank (FDIC), holding dollars or USDC, free withdrawals at 40,000+ US ATMs, no monthly or foreign transaction fees, and Remitly's best rate when a transfer is funded from the card. Direct deposit arrived on 2026-09-29, with a switch form generated in the app or a few taps to link an employer. The official direct-deposit promotion runs 2026-08-10 to 12-31: a first qualifying payroll, salary, pension or government-benefit ACH deposit of $300 or more earns a bonus about seven business days later, once per customer and per account. The terms only say the amount is the one shown in the app; the submission puts it at $50. A first-transfer discount sits alongside it ($10 off a $50+ send per the submission, $20 in our multi-region Remitly entry, which differs by market and channel). The $9.99-a-month Global Card Plan (Pay Later credit, 1% rewards) is an optional upgrade and is not needed for the bonus.

Conditions

The card is currently invitation-only for eligible US users and has a waitlist. The deposit must be recurring salary, wages or benefits sent by an employer or payroll provider over ACH; transfers, P2P, wallets, cheques, cash, wires and tax refunds do not count. The bonus is once only, and both amounts vary by market and campaign.

Steps

  1. Confirm the Global Card invitation in the Remitly app, open the card and activate it
  2. Generate a direct deposit form in the app for your employer, or link the employer directly
  3. Check the bonus in the app about seven business days after the first $300 deposit; send a transfer in the same session if you also want the first-transfer discount

Watch out

US only and invitation-based; Remitly alone decides what counts as a qualifying deposit, and a disguised transfer will not pay. The amount is not fixed in the terms, and the first-transfer discount may overlap with our multi-region $20 Remitly entry, so do not count both. The card is not a bank account.
