Highlight

If you were 15-29 on 2026-04-01, open your first ordinary deposit account in the MUFG Smart Account app or the MDC app, enter the campaign in MUFG Direct by the end of the following month and receive 4,000 giftee Box Select points

What you get

MUFG's "En-tame de Entertainment! Emutoku" campaign: people aged 15-29 on 2026-04-01 who have never held an MUFG ordinary deposit account can open one in the MUFG Smart Account app or the MDC app between 2026-11-09 and 2027-04-30 and enter the campaign in MUFG Direct by the end of the month after opening, which earns 4,000 giftee Box Select points (about 4,000 yen) exchangeable for restaurant e-tickets, streaming coupons and similar. The reward email arrives in the late part of the third month after opening at registered email address 1, which must accept @mufg.jp mail. Account holders can also join the monthly "Ataru Emutoku" lottery from a 20,000 yen month-end balance, rising 10,000 yen a month.

Conditions

Aged 15-29 on 2026-04-01 and living in Japan; first MUFG ordinary deposit account (accounts closed or dormant since 2024-04-01 do not count); must use the designated apps, not a branch, tablet or other app; opening through a points site or similar reward route disqualifies; must complete the entry and keep a unique inbox; one account per person.

Steps

  1. Open an ordinary deposit account in the MUFG Smart Account app or the MDC app (have your residence card and ID ready)
  2. Register MUFG Direct after opening and confirm email address 1 receives mail
  3. Complete the entry under Home > News & Campaigns > Campaign Entry by the end of the month after opening
  4. Around three months later, open the reward email and exchange the giftee Box Select points

Watch out

Japan residents only; the reward arrives about three months after opening; no resend for a missed entry, a shared inbox or a lost email; opening through a points site forfeits the reward; no community receipt data points yet, to be rechecked within 7 days.
