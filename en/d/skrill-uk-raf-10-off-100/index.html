Highlight

New UK customers register through a referral link or code and, after a first cross-border transfer of £100+, both sides get £10 of transfer credit; verify KYC with a passport plus UK proof of address, pay with a UK card, and you can also open a euro-denominated Skrill Prepaid Mastercard (virtual card instantly, works with Apple/Google Pay)

What you get

Skrill Money Transfer's Refer a Friend programme; the official terms state the UK line clearly: users registered in the United Kingdom, minimum transfer GBP 100 (or the equivalent in the send currency), referral credit GBP 10 for both the inviter and the new customer. The flow: the new customer registers through the inviter's link, or enters the code under "Got a referral code?" on the first transfer page (codes are valid for 3 months from registration), completes a cross-border transfer (sender and recipient in different countries, at least £100 excluding fees and taxes, delivered successfully), and once the recipient's funds are confirmed Skrill places £10 of credit under My Account → Refer a Friend, usable on the next qualifying transfer within 3 months, up to £50 per transfer (at most 5 credits). Registration and KYC need an ID plus proof of address (a passport plus UK proof of address is enough, with 4 days to supply it before the transaction is cancelled). Payment must be a UK-issued debit or credit card or a UK bank account — Skrill balance can't be used. The same account can apply for the Skrill Prepaid Mastercard: UK and EEA residents qualify, the virtual card works as soon as it's approved, the physical card arrives within 15 working days and it's denominated in euros. A UK user's hands-on report: paying with a UK card, transfers to Germany, the US, Italy and the Philippines arrived almost instantly, all done on the website (ordering in the app doesn't get this offer).

Conditions

New Skrill customers only (no Skrill account before registering, and one account per person); must register through the inviter's link or enter the code on the first transfer, completing a qualifying transfer within 3 months of registering; the first transfer must be cross-border (different countries), at least £100 excluding fees and taxes and successfully delivered; paying with the Skrill Prepaid Mastercard, wallet top-ups or withdrawals, moving money between your own accounts with your own code, or sending back to the inviter don't count; payment only by card or bank account, never Skrill balance; KYC needs passport/ID plus UK proof of address within 4 days or the transaction is cancelled; the £10 credit is valid 3 months, up to £50 per transfer, can't be withdrawn or transferred and returns if a transfer fails.

Steps

  1. Ask an existing Skrill Money Transfer user for a referral link or code (they must have sent a transfer themselves to generate one)
  2. Register a UK Skrill account through the link, or tap "Got a referral code?" on the first transfer page and enter it
  3. Complete KYC: passport plus UK proof of address with matching name and address
  4. Do everything on skrill.com: choose UK → Germany/US/Italy/Philippines, at least £100, paid with a UK debit or credit card
  5. Once the recipient's funds are confirmed, the £10 appears under My Account → Refer a Friend
  6. Spend the credit on a second cross-border transfer of £100+ within 3 months; for the card go to Skrill Card → Apply now — the virtual card is immediate

Watch out

The terms contradict themselves: 3.4 says the credit applies to "the next transfer" while 3.6 and the marketing page say new customers can use it on the first, so go by the quote at checkout and plan for a second £100+ transfer; it isn't free to run — 1% card top-up plus 3.99% FX (with Skrill's own rate mark-up) makes two £100 transfers cost about £7–8, leaving roughly £5, so it's only worth it if you meant to send money anyway; "instant" and "must use the website, not the app" are user data points, and the official promise is minutes to 2 business days; the card is euro-denominated with a €10 annual fee (some users also pay a €10 issuance fee), 1.75% at ATMs and 3.99% FX, so only open it if you'll really spend; the UK proof of address must genuinely be yours, since borrowing an address is a compliance risk that can freeze the account and make the balance hard to recover; accounts with no transactions for 12 months pay a €5/mo inactivity fee; from 2027-02-01 the UK Skrill business moves from Skrill Limited to Paysafe Financial Services Limited and the terms will change with it.
