Highlight

EUR 2.99 buys an eSIM with a +372 Estonian number, no monthly fee and a balance that never expires; one qualifying use within 365 days renews it. In mainland China it roams on China Unicom: EUR 4.7 per GB and free incoming SMS, but no voice calls there

What you get

eSIM.GG (known in Chinese communities as the turtle card) sells a prepaid eSIM with an Estonian +372 number. It is not a data-only travel plan: once installed it receives SMS, can call and take calls, and data can be bought per use. The number costs EUR 2.99 on the official site, there is no monthly fee, and the account balance does not expire. The keep-alive rule follows the official FAQ: any qualifying activity within 365 days, such as a little data, one SMS, one call, a top-up or a successful registration on a local network, restarts the 365-day window. After a full year without activity the number may be recycled. The vendor has announced a switch to a balance-change rule; until it lands, follow the site. Rates from the official coverage page updated 2026-11-21: data from EUR 0.88 per GB in most of Europe and Asia; a 10GB pack usable in 42 countries for EUR 9, covering China, the US, the UK, the EU, Malaysia and Thailand. In mainland China data is EUR 4.7 per GB, incoming SMS is free, outgoing SMS is EUR 0.24 and voice is unavailable. The site also states that ChatGPT and TikTok work normally, which matters when traffic needs a foreign exit. Community tests in October 2026: total opening cost around CNY 30 (number plus a first balance), installed on an eSTK external card, and after about 24 hours the number received a WhatsApp verification code; Telegram codes also arrived on some number ranges (5405, 5406, 5311, 57, 59, 8103). Receiving SMS does not mean every platform accepts the number: the vendor explicitly does not guarantee third-party codes, and a new line needs about 24 hours of technical provisioning, so silence right after installation is not a setup error. Who it suits: people who want a low-cost foreign number that receives codes for years without a subscription. Who it does not: anyone who needs voice calls inside mainland China, or whose only requirement is one specific platform that must accept the number.

Conditions

Buy online at esim.gg; the number starts at EUR 2.99 (some numbers also need a first balance); no monthly fee and no identity check; the install code is issued right after payment. Keep it alive with any qualifying activity within 365 days (data, SMS, call, top-up or a successful network registration). The phone must be unlocked and eSIM-capable, or paired with an eSTK or eSIM.me card. Third-party verification codes are not guaranteed.

Steps

  1. Open esim.gg and pick a +372 number; numbers marked in red need 24 hours to 3 days before SMS works
  2. After payment take the install code and write it to the phone's native eSIM or an external eUICC
  3. Wait about 24 hours for provisioning, then send a test SMS from the line's diagnostics page
  4. Put the expiry date in a calendar and do one qualifying activity a month early each year

Watch out

The EUR 2.99 does not include a first balance, so real opening cost is about CNY 30, and every year needs a small paid activity. The vendor has announced moving from activity-based to balance-based keep-alive, so the yearly cost must be recalculated once that lands. Third-party codes are not guaranteed and one working platform proves nothing for others. New lines need 24 hours to 3 days of provisioning. Voice is unavailable in mainland China and outgoing SMS costs EUR 0.24. The upstream operator can raise prices or shut the service down, with 90 days' notice at best.
