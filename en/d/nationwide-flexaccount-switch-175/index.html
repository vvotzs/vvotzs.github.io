What you get

Nationwide's official £175 switch bonus (from 2026-05-21 until withdrawn): switch to a new or existing FlexDirect, FlexAccount or FlexPlus with CASS from an account outside Nationwide / Virgin Money, bringing at least 2 active Direct Debits with it, and the switch must complete within 28 days of opening (or of telling the bank you'll switch into an existing account); within 31 days deposit £1,000 or more and make at least one payment with the Nationwide debit card (gambling, crypto, cash withdrawals and transfers don't count; Apple/Google Pay does). £175 is paid within 10 days of qualifying.

Conditions

UK resident; must be a first-time Nationwide switch bonus recipient (none since 2021, one per person and one per joint account); the account switched into can't be Nationwide / Virgin Money; full switch with 2 active Direct Debits within 28 days; £1,000 deposit and 1 card payment within 31 days; at most 3 Nationwide current accounts.

Steps

  1. Apply for FlexAccount (or FlexDirect/FlexPlus) at nationwide.co.uk and choose to switch, agreeing to close the old account
  2. Confirm the 2 Direct Debits on the old account move across within 28 days
  3. Deposit £1,000 into the new account and make at least one debit-card payment within 31 days
  4. Look for the £175 within 10 days of qualifying (it shows as Switching Offer on the statement)

Watch out

The full switch, including closing the old account, must complete within 28 days — partial or manual switches don't count; the 2 Direct Debits must already be active on the old account; anyone who has had a Nationwide switch bonus since 2021 can't get another; the bank can end the offer at any time.
