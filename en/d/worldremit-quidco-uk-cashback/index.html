Highlight

Start at Quidco, click through to WorldRemit, sign up and send a first transfer of £25+: £40 back for Pakistan/Nepal/Philippines/Ghana/Zimbabwe (Premium price; £37.50 for standard members) but only £20/£18.75 for other destinations; add a referral code as a new customer for another £10 transfer voucher

What you get

Quidco's WorldRemit merchant page (captured 2026-11-19) shows "Get up to £40 cashback" in two tiers: a new customer who registers and sends a first transfer of £25+ to Pakistan, Nepal, the Philippines, Ghana or Zimbabwe gets £40 (the page marks this as the Premium member rate; Cashback Wolf recorded £37.50 for standard members the same day), while a first transfer to other destinations gets only £20 (£18.75 standard). Cashback tracks within 24 hours but can take up to 6 months to become withdrawable after the merchant confirms. Adding a WorldRemit referral code in the same signup also earns a £10 transfer voucher after a first transfer of £30+ (the inviter gets a £20 voucher), and the community treats this stack as the most reliable single deal in the UK.

Conditions

New WorldRemit customers only; first transfer of £25+ (or £30+ to also get the £10 referral voucher); you must start from Quidco's Get cashback link and finish signup and payment in the same browser session; the £40 tier needs Premium membership (£1 a month, charged only in months with cashback, 3 months free for new members); Quidco's terms say using external discount codes can void cashback.

Steps

  1. Register or sign in to Quidco (new members can use an invite link for an extra signup bonus); take Premium for the £40 tier (3 months free for new members)
  2. Search WorldRemit on Quidco and click Get cashback
  3. Complete the WorldRemit signup in the same browser and enter the referral code (for the £10 voucher)
  4. Send a first transfer of £30+ to the destination (Philippines/Pakistan/Nepal/Ghana/Zimbabwe for the £40 tier)
  5. Check tracking on Quidco within 24 hours, then use the £10 voucher on a second transfer

Watch out

Some Reddit users report WorldRemit closing accounts for the Quidco + referral code stack, and the official terms reserve the right to cancel rewards for abuse; cashback waits for merchant confirmation and may take 6 months, so don't close the account; Premium's £1 comes out of the cashback; FX spread and transfer fees eat into it (about £1.99 in fees on the Philippines route).
