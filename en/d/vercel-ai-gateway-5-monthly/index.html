Highlight

Included with any Vercel account, refreshed every 30 days; switch between OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others by changing one string. Buying credits ends the monthly free allowance.

What you get

Vercel AI Gateway gives each team account a free tier: $5 of credits every 30 days to try models in the catalogue. The original FAQ said every model including premium ones; the current pricing docs say the free tier covers a subset with lower per-model rate limits, so check the Free Tier model list. Tokens are billed at the upstream provider's list price with no markup, including when you bring your own key. The free credit starts with your first AI Gateway request. Once you purchase AI Gateway Credits the account moves to the paid tier and the monthly $5 free credit no longer applies. Exceeding a rate limit returns a 429 that you can retry after a short wait.

Conditions

A Vercel account is required. The free tier is a subset of models with lower rate limits, resets every 30 days and does not roll over, and it disappears for good once credits are purchased.

Steps

  1. Register a Vercel account and open AI Gateway
  2. Make your first request to any free-tier model, which starts the $5 clock
  3. Change the model string to switch providers without touching your SDK; buy credits only for more usage

Watch out

The free model list and rate limits change; the $5 resets every 30 days and never accumulates; one credit purchase removes the free allowance permanently.
