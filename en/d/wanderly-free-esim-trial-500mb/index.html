What you get

Wanderly (Wanderly Technologies LLC, getwanderly.com) sells travel eSIMs across 200+ countries, and its free trial splits by destination: 500MB for seven days in 24 places including mainland China, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Thailand, Turkey, Australia, Vietnam, the US, the UK and Switzerland; 500MB for one day in 53 including most of Europe, Taiwan and Russia; and 100MB for seven days in 26 including Canada, Brazil, Mexico, India, the UAE and the Philippines, 103 destinations in all. Claiming happens only in the phone app, as the web sells paid plans: install, register and Your free eSIM is waiting appears on the home screen, then pick a destination, confirm the size and validity and finish the eligibility steps. The 500MB tier asks for a card check on Stripe's page without charging or subscribing, with Wanderly keeping only card type, issuing country and a protected token rather than the full number or CVC. Before 13 October 2026 checks ran through Zotlo's $0 trials, which Wanderly says are cancelled, so charges from zotlo.net or Z3P LLP usually date from then.

Conditions

First-time Wanderly users: once per account and device for life, plus a daily overall cap; claim in the app only; the app may require a one-time card check with no charge or subscription, which still does not guarantee approval; one destination per claim; an unlocked eSIM phone; data only with no number; the one-day tier starts on connection at the destination.

Steps

  1. Install the Wanderly app from the App Store or Google Play (the free eSIM cannot be claimed on the website)
  2. Create an account and tap "Your free eSIM is waiting" on the home tab
  3. Pick one destination and check the allowance (500MB or 100MB) and validity (1 or 7 days)
  4. Complete the eligibility steps in the app and, if asked, enter a card on the Stripe page with no charge and no subscription
  5. Install the eSIM over Wi-Fi before you fly, then turn on data roaming for that line after landing

Watch out

No charge and no subscription is the official wording, but Wanderly keeps the card type, issuing country and a protected card identifier for anti-abuse checks; card checks before 13 October 2026 ran as a Zotlo $0 trial, so a statement may show zotlo.net or Z3P LLP (Wanderly says those trials were cancelled, and a third-party list mentions a $0.50 monthly renewal, so write to support@getwanderly.com if you see one); Wanderly states there is no universal 500MB promise and that verification does not guarantee approval, and the allowance and list change from day to day; the offer is tied to your device, so a new phone cannot claim it again; 500MB is emergency data only and a 1-day tier is easy to waste if the time difference catches you out; the plan is data only, so services that need an SMS code will not work.
