Highlight

Enter a 16-digit Visa card number from an Asia-Pacific issuer and get a Travelgoogoo 365 annual eSIM covering 123 countries, with messaging that does not eat data.

What you get

A Visa perk with Travelgoogoo: enter a 16-digit card number from an eligible Asia-Pacific-issued Visa card at travelgoogoo.com/en/visa and receive a free Travelgoogoo 365 annual eSIM. Tiers follow the card (raised from 2026-09-07): Visa Infinite gets 4GB of global data plus 4GB of APAC data and a year of unlimited messaging; Visa Signature gets 2GB global (plus an extra 1GB of APAC per some reports, check the redemption page); Visa Platinum gets 1GB global with the 365 annual plan. "Unlimited messaging" means text and photos in iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, WeChat, Viber and Zalo; voice and video calls still burn data. Redemption puts a $1 authorisation on the card, later reversed. The plan runs a year from first connection, so you can redeem now and activate before a trip.

Conditions

Asia-Pacific-issued Visa Infinite / Signature / Platinum only; one redemption per card number and per mobile number per year, up to two in total until 2027-11-30; eSIM phone required (iPhone XS or later, flagship Androids from 2019); data-only, no local number.

Steps

  1. Open travelgoogoo.com/en/visa, tap Redeem Now and enter the 16-digit Visa number
  2. Sign up and pass the $1 card authorisation (it is reversed)
  3. Install the eSIM from the emailed QR code and activate it when you travel; valid for a year

Watch out

Only APAC-issued cards qualify, so European and US cards are rejected. Signature and Platinum APAC allowances are reported inconsistently (unverified). The data is small for heavy use, and the redemption page intermittently returns 500 from mainland China.
