Highlight

Connect its MCP to Claude or ChatGPT and the agent does the work per action: write and publish an article $3, an AI-visibility check $0.004, a keyword update $0.15, an outreach email found $0.065. Sign-up gives $1, reading your data is free, top-ups start at $10 and roll over.

What you get

RankSpot turns SEO into an execution layer billed per action (No. 2 on the Product Hunt 2026-11-17 daily leaderboard). Model usage stays on the Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or n8n plan you already pay for; RankSpot charges only for the actions it performs and shows the price before each one runs. An account is free, reading your own site data is free, and a one-time $1 welcome credit lets you try paid actions; top-ups start at $10, credit rolls over and nothing renews automatically. Published prices: an article researched, written, published and indexed is $3 (images add the AI cost +30%), an indexing request is $0.01, editing through MCP with your own AI is free; an AI-visibility check — one prompt in one AI, the answer, the brands it names and the pages it cites — is $0.004 and includes forum threads and link opportunities; a keyword update is $0.15, a backlink update $0.07, tracking a competitor is free; an outreach email found is $0.065 (nothing if not found), sending an email from your own mailbox is $0.025, connecting a mailbox and replies are free.

Conditions

$1 is a one-time welcome credit and only useful once an agent is connected. Minimum top-up is $10. Model token costs are not included — they come from your own AI subscription. Writing, publishing and requesting indexing each trigger paid actions in sequence.

Steps

  1. Sign up at rankspot.ai (free, with the $1 welcome credit)
  2. Connect its MCP to Claude or ChatGPT in about two minutes
  3. Do the free work first: read your site data, add competitors, look at the link opportunities inside AI-visibility checks
  4. Ask for a price before writing ($3 an article) and only then let it run; editing with your own AI is free
  5. Top up from $10 when the credit runs out — it rolls over and never auto-renews

Watch out

$1 is a third of one $3 article, so a real run costs far more than the free credit. The Product Hunt copy mentions a free article with sign-up, but the pricing page lists only the $1 welcome credit — unverified. Publishing puts content on your live site and requests indexing, so test on a small scale first. Outreach sends from your own mailbox, so watch your sending-domain reputation.
