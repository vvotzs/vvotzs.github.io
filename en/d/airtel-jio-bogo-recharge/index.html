Highlight

Airtel and Jio are rolling out a buy-one-get-one to selected prepaid users: recharge the 28-day ₹349 or ₹399 plan in the Airtel Thanks or MyJio app and qualifying accounts get a second recharge of the same plan, doubling validity to 56 days and cutting the effective monthly cost to ₹175 or ₹200

What you get

Beebom (8 Nov 2026), Gadgets Now (15 Nov 2026) and PaidFreeDroid (9 Nov 2026) checked the carrier apps: Airtel shows a "1+1 Recharge Free" tag to some accounts, and recharging the ₹349 plan (2GB a day plus unlimited 5G) or ₹399 plan (unlimited 4G/5G) grants a second recharge of the same value. Jio's version appears as a "Buy One Get One Free" banner or a gold badge in recommended plans on MyJio, and support says the bonus is credited as a voucher in MyJio Vouchers within seven days. Eligibility is algorithmic in both cases, so if the tag is not on your account the offer is not available.

Conditions

Indian prepaid users only; the tag must appear in the Airtel Thanks or MyJio app; only the ₹349 and ₹399 28-day plans; Jio's bonus arrives as a voucher to redeem manually within seven days; cannot be stacked with some other promotions.

Steps

  1. Open the Airtel Thanks or MyJio app and sign in with the prepaid number
  2. Check whether the ₹349 or ₹399 plan carries the 1+1 tag
  3. Complete the recharge as prompted
  4. On Jio, collect the bonus voucher under Menu > Vouchers after seven days
  5. Activate the bonus once the first plan expires so nothing is wasted

Watch out

A trial rollout, so most accounts will not see the tag; the carriers have not detailed how the bonus activates and Jio needs a manual voucher redemption; you are locking in 56 days of prepaid service rather than getting cash.
