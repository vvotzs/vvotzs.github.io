Highlight

Foreign passport holders scan U Mobile's official QR code in the arrival halls of KLIA T1/T2, Kota Kinabalu and Langkawi, pass passport and face verification in the MyUMobile app and get a 100GB uncapped 5G eSIM for 24 hours; Malaysia local network only, no mainland China roaming

What you get

An official tourist eSIM for Visit Malaysia 2026: foreign passport holders scan a dedicated QR code in the arrivals area at KLIA T1 or T2, Kota Kinabalu or Langkawi and claim a 100GB, 24-hour ULTRA5G tourist eSIM in the MyUMobile app, with full-speed 5G/4G, no throttling, hotspot sharing and no physical option. The flow is airport Wi-Fi, install MyUMobile (v3.13.11+), scan into ULTRA5G Tourist, accept and Redeem, upload the passport with a face check, Verify and install, with details also emailed. The line stays 14 days after the 24 hours (free incoming calls) and more data costs RM3 for 1GB a day, RM6 for 10GB a day, RM12 for 10GB a week and RM15 for 20GB a week, all full speed with hotspot. The separate Free ULTRA5G Trial (10GB, seven days, MyKad holders) is for locals.

Conditions

Non-Malaysian foreign passport holders, once in a lifetime; scan the official code at one of the four airport arrivals, as the web cannot claim it; passport and face verification; eSIM only on an unlocked compatible phone; Malaysian data only with no mainland China roaming; the campaign runs to 31 December 2026.

Steps

  1. After landing, find the U Mobile QR code in the arrival area of KLIA T1/T2, Kota Kinabalu or Langkawi and connect to airport Wi-Fi
  2. Install and update the MyUMobile app to 3.13.11 or newer, then scan the code to open the ULTRA5G Tourist page
  3. Accept the terms, tap Redeem, upload your passport, complete face verification, fill in your details and tap Verify
  4. Download and install the eSIM in the app, set it as the data line and turn on data roaming

Watch out

Only 24 hours, so use it on landing day; the 100GB is a trial allowance and any further data on the same eSIM must be bought; the code is only available at the airport, so you cannot get it after leaving; face verification fails if your passport details do not match; Malaysia local network only, so it does not roam, including in mainland China.
