Highlight

An AI agent that works inside WordPress: during the sale a new account starts with 15,000 credits instead of 5,000, no card needed, and code FRIDAY50 takes 50% off an upgrade

What you get

WPOS is an AI agent that builds and edits WordPress sites from inside WordPress. Its official Black Friday page says accounts created between 11 and 30 November start with 15,000 credits instead of the usual 5,000, with no card required. By WPOS's own count across 573 real tasks, 15,000 credits buy roughly 27 full page builds, 83 new sections or pages, 214 single edits, or 500 audits and answers. Upgrading in the sale takes code FRIDAY50: 50% off site licences for the first two months for new paying customers, then $25 a site a month ($22 from 10 sites, $20 from 50, $19.50 from 100), and any credit pack adds 25%. The stated deadline is 30 November at 23:59 Pacific time, which is 15:59 on 1 December in Beijing.

Conditions

Create a new WPOS account between 11 and 30 November; existing accounts do not get the extra credits. Bring your own WordPress site on any host and page builder. The free credits need no card; only an upgrade costs money.

Steps

  1. Open wpos.ai/black-friday/, sign up with an email and create a new account
  2. Connect your WordPress site and install the WPOS plugin
  3. Build or redesign pages from inside the site and watch the credit balance in your account; at checkout for an upgrade enter FRIDAY50

Watch out

The countdown at the top of the page does not load fully and shows the static text "The sale has ended", so trust the dates in the body (11 to 30 November); if your dashboard shows only 5,000 credits the offer has closed. Credits are WPOS's own task units, not dollars. FRIDAY50 is for new paying customers, once per customer, and licences renew at $25 a site a month after two months.
