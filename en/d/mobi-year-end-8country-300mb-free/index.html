Highlight

mobi's early year-end gift lists eight destinations - Japan, Thailand, USA, Vietnam, Hong Kong/Macau, Indonesia, China and the Philippines - each with a 300MB/1-day data-only eSIM cut from 1,000 KRW to 0 KRW; ordering needs only a phone number check, no account signup

What you get

mobi, the eSIM roaming brand of KT's MVNO kt M모바일, runs a year-end gift page: in Japan, Thailand, the USA, Vietnam, Hong Kong/Macau, Indonesia, China and the Philippines one 300MB/1-day data-only eSIM each drops from 1,000 KRW to 0 KRW, while other plans on the same page are 31-32% off (Japan unlimited for three days is 2,970 KRW). Conditions

Needs a Korean mobile number to receive the SMS code (whether an overseas number works is not stated); the page presents it as limited stock, first come first served, with no end date published; data only, no number and no calls or SMS; 384Kbps cap after 300MB, no top-up and no refund; the device must be unlocked and the eSIM must not have been bound to another phone.
