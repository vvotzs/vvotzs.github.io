What you get

Exa provides semantic search for AI applications through Search, Contents and Answer endpoints, with Lite search around $4 per 1,000 requests. The official Starter tier gives every team $10 of credits a month, reset on the first of each month without rollover and with no payment method required, and completing onboarding adds a one-time $10, so the first month holds $20. Two creator and partner codes circulating in the community, EXA20FREEBUFF and EXA20FIRESHIP, were tested on 2026-11-03 and stacked on one account, taking the balance from $20 to $60 with no expiry shown in the dashboard; the older EXA50API stopped working and EXA50FIRESHIP hit its redemption limit. A NodeSeek post on 6 November records the same codes with reports that EXA50API had already failed for some.

Conditions

Both codes are creator or partner promotions rather than official pricing-page offers, so they can stop working or hit a limit at any time, once per team each. Some accounts must verify a non-prepaid card before bonus credit is usable. The $10 a month does not roll over. Codes show no expiry in the dashboard, though the official terms reserve the right to change them.

Steps

  1. Sign up at dashboard.exa.ai (no card needed)
  2. Open Billing, redeem EXA20FREEBUFF, then EXA20FIRESHIP
  3. Reload Billing to confirm the balance, then finish onboarding for the extra $10
  4. Make one Search or Contents call; usage beyond the credits bills at list rates

Watch out

Both codes are unofficial and can be withdrawn or capped. Some accounts hit a non-prepaid card check that virtual cards fail. The credits are API usage, not cash, and usage beyond the allowance bills at $4 per 1,000 requests.
