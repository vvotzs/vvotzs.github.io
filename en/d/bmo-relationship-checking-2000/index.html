Highlight

Open BMO Relationship Checking and take $10,000, $15,000 or $20,000 in qualifying direct deposits within 90 days for $600, $800 or $1,000; add a Growth Money Market with $150,000 of new money for another $1,000

What you get

BMO's official Relationship Checking limited-time offer page, read 21 November 2026, runs from 9 September 2026 to 2 March 2027: new personal customers who open BMO Relationship Checking and log in to BMO Digital Banking at least once within 90 days earn $600 for $10,000 to $14,999.99 of qualifying direct deposits, $800 for $15,000 to $19,999.99 and $1,000 for $20,000 or more. Opening a Growth Money Market at the same time and depositing $150,000 or more in new money within 45 days, then holding that balance from day 46 to day 90, adds another $1,000 for a combined maximum of $2,000. Both accounts must be opened at the same time and the unique promo code can only be used once. Relationship Checking carries a $25 monthly fee.

Conditions

New BMO personal checking clients only, including joint owners, and anyone who held or closed a BMO personal checking account in the past 12 months is excluded; the unique promo code from BMO's mailer, email or QR code is mandatory and there is no public universal code; qualifying direct deposits must reach the chosen tier within 90 days plus one digital banking login; the money market bonus needs $150,000 in new money within 45 days held through day 90; opening the accounts at different times forfeits the second bonus.

Steps

  1. Find the unique promo code or QR code on your BMO mailer or email
  2. Open BMO Relationship Checking with that code, adding Growth Money Market at the same time if you want both bonuses
  3. Bring in the qualifying direct deposits for your tier within 90 days and log in to digital banking once
  4. For the money market bonus, deposit $150,000 of new money within 45 days and hold it to day 90

Watch out

Without BMO's mailed code you cannot claim the offer; the $25 monthly fee applies to Relationship Checking; the $1,000 money market bonus needs $150,000, which most people cannot reach; the $20,000 direct deposit tier is steep; community reports mention difficulty getting refunds after closing a BMO account.
