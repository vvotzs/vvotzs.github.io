Highlight

Always-free 2 OCPU/12GB ARM or 2 small AMD instances, plus $300 for 30 days

What you get

Oracle Cloud Always Free includes 1,500 OCPU hours and 9,000 GB hours a month (equivalent to a 2 OCPU / 12GB ARM A1 instance), up to 2 AMD micro instances, 200GB of block storage, object storage and a load balancer. New accounts also get $300 of trial credit for 30 days.

Conditions

Card verification required; Always Free resources can only run in your home region; instances whose 95th-percentile CPU and network use stay below 20% for 7 days (memory too for A1) may be reclaimed; capacity shortages that block new instances are normal.

Steps

  1. Sign up for Oracle Cloud and pick a suitable home region (it sets latency and which shapes you can launch)
  2. Create a VM.Standard.A1.Flex (2 OCPU/12GB) or a micro instance under Compute
  3. Attach a boot volume within the 200GB of block storage
  4. Before the trial ends, keep A1 within 2 OCPU/12GB so it isn't deleted

Watch out

Since 2026-07 the free ARM allowance is 2 OCPU/12GB (was 4 OCPU/24GB); older 4-core instances are stopped and deleted after 30 days, and idle instances can be reclaimed.
