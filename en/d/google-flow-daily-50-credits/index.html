Highlight

Google's own help centre states every account gets 50 Flow credits a day with or without a subscription; Veo 3.1 Lite costs 10 credits for an 8-second 720p clip, so that is five clips and about 40 seconds of footage a day, plus Nano Banana storyboard images, with no card

What you get

Google Flow is the AI film-making tool inside Gemini. The official help page "Manage your Google Flow credits" (updated November 2026) states that every account receives 50 Google Flow credits a day whether or not it has a subscription, refreshed by the first generation of the day, with unused credits not carried over. What the free tier can run: Veo 3.1 Lite (4, 6 or 8 seconds at 720p, 10 credits each) and Omni Flash 1.2 (1 credit per second at 360p, 3 per second at 720p, up to 10 seconds), plus image generation and the Flow agent. That makes the daily 50 worth five 8-second Lite clips, or one 720p 10-second Omni Flash clip (30 credits) plus extras. Free users may be blocked from video generation during the 14:00-17:00 UTC peak; images keep working. Workspace Business Starter/Standard/Plus and Education Standard/Plus plans also include 50 credits a day at no extra charge.

Conditions

Age-verified 18+; the account must be in a supported region; the 50 credits reset daily and unused ones are lost; video generation can be throttled for free users at peak while images are unaffected; extra AI credits cannot be bought in Japan and some other regions.

Steps

  1. Open Google Flow (labs.google/flow) with a Google account
  2. In the prompt box settings confirm the model is Veo 3.1 Lite or Omni Flash 1.2 and check its credit cost
  3. Generate storyboard images first and pick the good frames before spending credits on video
  4. Spend the day's credits before midnight since they do not carry over

Watch out

50 credits a day go fast on serious work; Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni Flash 1.1 retire on 5 December 2026, so older tutorials pick models that will be gone; free users are throttled during the 22:00-01:00 Beijing peak.
