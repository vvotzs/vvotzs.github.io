Highlight

A 3HK prepaid card sold by Fortress: HKD 50 for the card with HKD 50 of credit inside, no monthly fee, and free incoming SMS in the mainland; one HKD 10 top-up a year in the app keeps the +852 number for another 365 days, and only Hong Kong local service needs registration

What you get

A recyclable travel prepaid card from Hutchison (3HK) sold through Fortress: typically HK$50 with HK$50 of credit inside, so the card is effectively free, and it must be first inserted before the last-activation date printed on the pack. The number lasts 365 days, extended another 365 by a top-up of HK$10+ in the haha TRAVEL app or on 3HK's top-up page every 12 months; buying data packs does not keep it alive, the app's minimum is often HK$20, so a HK$10 top-up goes through 3HK's site (which takes WeChat Pay and Alipay from the mainland). Within 14 days of expiry a restart can be requested but is not guaranteed and needs new real-name registration. On mainland roaming incoming texts are free while sending costs HK$3.5, answering HK$8 a minute, calls to Hong Kong HK$8 and to the mainland HK$6, and data comes from mainland day passes (often HK$10 a day, throttled after about 500MB). Real-name registration applies only to Hong Kong local service, not to plain roaming codes.

Conditions

Buy the physical card at Fortress or 3HK (HK$50 with HK$50 credit) and insert it before the last-activation date; top up HK$10+ every 12 months, as data packs do not count; real-name only for Hong Kong service; no eSIM from the operator, so the phone needs an unlocked physical slot; numbers are assigned at random.

Steps

  1. Buy a haha SIM from Fortress and check the last activation date on the box
  2. Turn Wi-Fi off, insert the card in slot one with automatic network selection, and read the +852 number in the activation SMS
  3. Log into haha TRAVEL with the number and ICCID, then screenshot the balance and expiry date
  4. Top up at least HKD 10 before the expiry date each year, using the 3HK page for an exact HKD 10, and confirm the new date

Watch out

Physical SIM only, with no eSIM, and the last activation date on the box is easy to miss. Keeping the number depends on a top-up, data packs do not count, and the app often starts at HKD 20, so overpaying is common. After 14 days past expiry the number cannot be recovered. In the mainland everything runs on roaming and calls and SMS are expensive. Numbers are random and cannot be chosen, and platforms such as Telegram and Google reject them often. Hong Kong local service needs registration, so an unregistered card is roaming-only.
