Highlight

Kiro Free is a perpetual free tier: no card, 50 credits a month, with Claude Sonnet 4.5 and open-weight models such as DeepSeek 3.2 / MiniMax M2.5 / GLM-5 / Qwen3 Coder Next. From 2026-11-01, a first upgrade to Pro is free for the rest of the month (prorated to the second) and adds 500 trial credits (about $20, valid 12 months), unlocking Claude Opus 5.5 / GPT-5.6 Sol / Grok 4.7.

What you get

Kiro is AWS's AI IDE (IDE, CLI, Web, Crew) billed in credits: a simple prompt can cost less than one credit, a full spec task costs more, and models have different multipliers. Free tier (perpetual): 50 credits a month, reset at the start of each month and not rolled over; access to Claude Sonnet 4.5 and open-weight models (DeepSeek 3.2, MiniMax M2.5, GLM-5, MiniMax M2.1, Qwen3 Coder Next) subject to rate limits; Kiro Web needs a paid plan, while the IDE and CLI work on Free. The new free trial, from 2026-11-01 (announced 2026-10-27): the first time you upgrade from Free to Pro, the prorated cost of the rest of the month is covered, the first bill lands on the 1st of the following month, and you get 500 trial credits valid for 12 months that are used only after plan credits run out. Example: upgrading at 00:00 UTC on day 15 of a 30-day month gives a prorated $10.67 covered by the free month, so you pay $0 and receive 533 plan credits plus 500 trial credits. Starting on Pro+, Pro Max or Power still uses the free Pro month — you pay only the difference from Pro's prorated price ($10.67 for Pro+ in the same example). Going back to Free keeps unused trial credits, but they can then only be spent on free-tier models. The trial applies only to your first upgrade.

Conditions

The free tier needs no credit card (sign in with GitHub, Google or AWS Builder ID); upgrading to a paid plan requires a valid card and renews monthly. The 50 monthly credits reset and do not roll over. The 500 trial credits come once, on the first upgrade, expire after 12 months, and after a downgrade can only be used on free-tier models. Model availability depends on your country and each model provider's policy.

Steps

  1. Download Kiro IDE or the CLI at kiro.dev and sign in with GitHub, Google or AWS Builder ID to land on the Free tier (no card)
  2. Use the 50 monthly credits to try Claude Sonnet 4.5 and DeepSeek 3.2 / MiniMax M2.5 / GLM-5 / Qwen3 Coder Next
  3. For Claude Opus 5.5 / GPT-5.6 Sol / Grok 4.7, upgrade to Pro for the first time (card required): the rest of the month is free and you get 500 trial credits
  4. Trial credits kick in after plan credits are used and stay valid for 12 months
  5. To avoid the charge, downgrade to Free before billing on the 1st; unused trial credits remain usable on free-tier models

Watch out

Upgrading needs a card, so forgetting to downgrade before the 1st means $20/month and up. The free month and 500 trial credits are first-upgrade-only; after a downgrade the trial credits are limited to free-tier models. The free tier's 50 credits a month don't accumulate and run out quickly on full spec tasks. Premium models are not available in every country. Kiro has not quantified the credit multiplier for each model, the Sonnet 4.5 rate limit, or the exact draw-down order of trial credits — those are marked unverified.
