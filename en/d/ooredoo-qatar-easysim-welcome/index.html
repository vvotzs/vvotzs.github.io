What you get

Ooredoo Qatar's EasySIM online welcome offer: priced Free, with 300MB of local data and 10 minutes of local calls, valid 3 days. You can choose eSIM or a physical SIM; scan a QR code, pick a plan, scan your ID and take a selfie for AI identity checks, and it activates immediately without queueing at a counter. It's a gift for new lines — once the 300MB is gone, top up a Hala plan in the Ooredoo app.

Conditions

Must open a new Ooredoo Qatar line with real-name registration (Qatari ID or passport, depending on the channel); the 300MB and minutes are Qatar-local only; valid 3 days and not cumulative; an eSIM-capable unlocked phone.

Steps

  1. Open the EasySIM page on ooredoo.qa and click Get it now
  2. Confirm the Welcome Offer and choose eSIM as the SIM type
  3. Scan your ID and take a selfie to pass the identity checks
  4. Install the eSIM QR code you receive and use the 300MB within 3 days

Watch out

300MB is only enough for navigation and a check-in; opening the line needs real-name registration, so tourists use a passport and some channels require a Qatari ID; the free allowance is local only and roaming is charged separately.
