Highlight

A three-year prepaid card on the CSL network: HKD 138 buys a +852 number with 30GB of Hong Kong data at 42Mbps and 2,000 local minutes over 1,095 days, as a physical SIM or an eSIM

What you get

LuckySIM leases the CSL network and sells this as plan code LSPG#1095D_30G42M_2000: HKD 138 brings 30GB of Hong Kong data capped at 42Mbps plus 2,000 minutes of local calls, valid for 1,095 days with the number kept throughout, and local calls beyond the allowance cost HKD 0.1 a minute. A physical SIM or an eSIM both work, but the eSIM installs only once and cannot move to another phone, and sales are final. Hotspot sharing and VoLTE work while IDD international calls do not. Hong Kong services require real-name registration at rnr.luckysim.com.hk, checked within two working days at most, and the line stays blocked until the check clears; the vendor also converts between SIM and eSIM. The plan covers Hong Kong alone, so data abroad needs a separate roaming bundle such as 24GB across 17 countries including China for HKD 150 over two years, while roaming voice deducts local minutes.

Conditions

Buy from the official shop or an authorised agent for HKD 138, with overseas postage extra; real-name registration with a passport or Hong Kong ID is mandatory before use; the eSIM installs once with no refund; numbers are random; roaming data is a separate bundle.

Watch out
