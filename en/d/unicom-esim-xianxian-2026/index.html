Highlight

After setting up a Unicom eSIM (new line, physical SIM to eSIM, or number port-in) claim a benefit within 30 days: carrier-supplied models can take 100GB of domestic data or overseas eSIM data and roaming vouchers; self-bought phones choose one of two

What you get

China Unicom's eSIM launch season 2026: activating eSIM service (new line, physical SIM to eSIM, eSIM to eSIM, or after number port-in) lets you claim a self-selected benefit pack within 30 calendar days, once per phone. For eSIM models supplied by Unicom you pick one of three — overseas eSIM data, an international roaming voucher or domestic data — while self-bought phones that Unicom doesn't supply usually choose between overseas data and a roaming voucher. Domestic data tiers include 100GB (plus a 50GB six-month pack and 200/300/500GB annual packs), with the activation month as the first month, valid for 6 or 12 calendar months and no rollover; the roaming voucher is the ¥300 tier. Benefits can't be cancelled, renewed or given away.

Conditions

Must hold a Unicom number and set up eSIM (the phone must be on Unicom's compatibility list and a mainland-market model); the benefit must be claimed within 30 calendar days through the Unicom app, a store poster QR code or the card in carrier-supplied device packaging; domestic data is only for carrier-supplied models, and numbers on conflicting plans such as "Unicom Cube" or throttled plans can't claim it; an independent eSIM number counts towards the five-lines-per-ID limit, and each device supports at most 2 eSIM numbers across carriers.

Steps

  1. Check in the Unicom app under Services → eSIM zone, or at a nearby own-brand store, that your model qualifies
  2. Set up the eSIM (new line, physical SIM to eSIM, or number port-in)
  3. Within 30 days choose one benefit on the campaign page (domestic data, or overseas data/roaming voucher)
  4. Use it before it expires; domestic data doesn't roll over

Watch out

Domestic data only goes to carrier-supplied models, so self-bought phones usually get overseas data or a roaming voucher; throttled or conflicting plans make the domestic data claim void; overseas data must be installed and activated abroad within 365 days and doesn't support hotspot; in-store processing means visiting an own-brand store.
