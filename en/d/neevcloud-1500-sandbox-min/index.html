Highlight

A free tier added in the November release: every verified account gets 1,500 sandbox minutes a month, fully covering sandboxes up to 2 vCPU / 4 GB with disk included, on templates that ship Claude Code, Codex CLI, opencode and OpenClaw.

What you get

NeevCloud's November 2026 release notes (v0.8.1 to v0.9.0) add a free tier for Agentic Studio sandboxes: 1,500 sandbox minutes free each month, renewing monthly, for every verified account, and explicitly "no credit card is needed; completing account verification is enough". It applies to existing accounts too, and the console shows the remaining balance. Minutes are weighted by sandbox size: up to 2 vCPU / 4 GB is fully covered, smaller sandboxes burn fewer minutes and larger ones proportionally more, and disk inside the free tier is free. The platform also ships templates for OpenClaw, Claude Code, Codex CLI, opencode and Hermes, plus Docker and Playwright + Chromium templates, snapshots and an MCP endpoint.

Conditions

The account must be verified (individual KYC; businesses have a separate flow). The 1,500 minutes reset monthly and do not accumulate. 2 vCPU / 4 GB is the covered size, and larger sandboxes draw down proportionally more. Idle sandboxes pause automatically and wake on the first request, and usage beyond the allowance is billed.

Steps

  1. Sign up at neevcloud.com and complete account verification (no card needed)
  2. Open Agentic Studio, then Sandboxes, and confirm the 1,500-minute monthly allowance
  3. Create a sandbox from a template such as Playwright + Chromium or Claude Code
  4. Watch the remaining minutes in the console before committing to paid usage

Watch out

Minutes are weighted by size, so large sandboxes drain them quickly. KYC means handing over identity details. The allowance resets monthly with no carry-over, and sandboxes that pause still consume minutes while awake. The platform is young, so stability and retention policies deserve a look.
