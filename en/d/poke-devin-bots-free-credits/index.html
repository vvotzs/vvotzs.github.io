Highlight

Text Poke what you want to build and convince it how many free credits you deserve; 10,000 accounts, up to $10,000 each, plus a month of Devin Max

What you get

Cognition launched Devin Bots on 17 November 2026: a lead agent runs several Devin sessions in parallel, each on its own cloud machine. In the same limited-time experiment you message Poke at poke.com/bots, describe the outcome you want it to own (not a single ticket) and argue for how many free credits you deserve. Cognition's Walden Yan said 10,000 accounts are available with up to $10,000 each, and participants also get one month of Devin Max ($200/month). Once you confirm, Poke sends an exclusive link; sign in to Devin and finish the Stripe checkout, no card needed.

Conditions

Needs a phone that can receive Poke messages (iPhone Safari or Mac iMessage); the credit amount is decided by Poke per pitch, so not everyone gets a large grant; 10,000 accounts, first come first served; the offer ends 23 November 2026 at 16:00 Beijing time.

Steps

  1. Open poke.com/bots in Safari on an iPhone (or message via iMessage on a Mac)
  2. Tell Poke the outcome you want it to own, your stack, and what you will handle yourself
  3. Agree on an amount, confirm, and get your exclusive link
  4. Open the link, sign in to Devin and complete the Stripe checkout (no card required) to activate the membership and credits

Watch out

Poke decides the amount, so most people will not get the maximum; this is described as a one-week experiment and can be tightened or ended at any time; Devin quotas refresh daily and weekly, so the advertised credit value is not usable all at once; a vague pitch tends to get the lowest tier.
