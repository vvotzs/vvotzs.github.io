What you get

GLM Coding Plan charges GLM-5.3-Flash, including the visual-understanding MCP, at 50% of its normal multiplier from November 24 to December 24: input 2.3 to 1.15, cached input 0.56 to 0.28, output 8 to 4. Official estimates: at a 95% cache hit rate Lite goes from roughly 146-292 million Flash tokens a week to 292-584 million, Pro to 1.75-3.51 billion and Max to 4.09-8.19 billion. Off-peak calls were already charged at half and stack with this, and peak hours are Monday to Friday 14:00-18:00 UTC+8.

Conditions

An active GLM Coding Plan (Lite, Pro or Max) is required, and calls must go through the officially supported tools to spend plan credits. The discount applies automatically with no code, direct API calls outside those tools do not qualify, and multipliers return to normal after the promotion.
