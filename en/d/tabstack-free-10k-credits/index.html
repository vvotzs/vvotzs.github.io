Highlight

Mozilla's web API for agents gives every new account 10,000 credits with no card, enough for 1,000 page-to-Markdown extractions or 40 cited fast research runs. The trial stops when the credits run out.

What you get

Tabstack's pricing page states every new account includes 10,000 free credits with no credit card. Credits are spent per action: Extract to Markdown 10 credits, Extract to JSON 50, Generate 100, Automate 100, Research Fast 250, Research Balanced 350. So 10,000 credits is roughly 1,000 Markdown extractions, 40 fast research runs, or any mix. The platform runs search, page reading, gap checking, synthesis and citation outside your model and returns a cited Markdown report. It says it is never trained on, private by default and built by Mozilla. Trial accounts have no overage billing: requests stop when the credits run out instead of converting to a paid plan. Paid tiers are Starter at $10/month (100,000 credits), Team at $99 and Pro at $499. It launched on Product Hunt on 2026-11-19 with "Start free with 10,000 credits".

Conditions

Sign up at tabstack.ai; no card. The 10,000 credits are a one-off trial allowance and do not refresh. Balanced research needs Team or above, so trial accounts only get Fast. Rate limit is 15 requests per minute.

Steps

  1. Register at tabstack.ai with no card
  2. Create an API key in the console and confirm the balance shows 10,000 credits
  3. Call /extract (Markdown or JSON) or /research with mode: fast
  4. Check in the console how many credits each action spent; requests stop when they are gone

Watch out

At the published overage rate the 10,000 credits are worth only about $3, so this is a trial rather than a lasting free tier. Research can chain several actions and bills each one, so use the console estimate before running it. Do not point production at a trial account.
