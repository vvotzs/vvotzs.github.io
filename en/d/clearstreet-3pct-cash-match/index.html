Highlight

Open and fund a brokerage account between Nov 9 and Dec 31 for a 3% match on net deposits within 14 days, up to $10,000 — but the matched money has to stay for 5 years

What you get

Clear Street, a New York brokerage, offers new customers who open and fund an individual cash or margin account between 9 November and 31 December 2026 a 3% cash match on net deposits made in the 14 days after the first funding date, with a $5,000 minimum net deposit and a $10,000 cap; ACH, wire and ACATS transfers all count, and the match is credited in one lump sum within 30 days after the window. The catch is that the deposited funds must remain for 5 years after the match is credited, and withdrawing early can trigger a 3% chargeback. Idle cash under $50,000 earns 0%. Note: a third-party report on 16 November described an older $250-for-$5,000-for-90-days version; the official page now shows the 3% match with the 5-year hold, so the official terms control.

Conditions

New Clear Street customers who never received a prior new-account promotion; US resident with US tax ID and address; $5,000 minimum net deposit within the 14-day window; funds kept 5 years; cannot be combined with other Clear Street promotions, though referral rewards can stack.

Steps

  1. Open a Clear Street individual brokerage account through the offer page before 31 December
  2. Within 14 days of your first deposit, top up to your target net deposit (ACH, wire or ACATS)
  3. The match is credited within 30 days after the window and can be used as buying power
  4. Do not withdraw the matched funds for 5 years or you may be charged back

Watch out

The 5-year hold is very long and ties up your money; cash under $50,000 earns 0%, so parking $5,000 for $150 of match has a real opportunity cost; early withdrawals can be clawed back; investments can lose value; terms can change.
