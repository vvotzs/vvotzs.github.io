Highlight

The biggest switch bonus in the UK right now: switch in with CASS and three direct debits plus a £2,000 salary to earn £150, then £30 a month for five months by spending 10 times and keeping the salary coming.

What you get

Barclays' Current Account Switch offer (from 2026-10-01, withdrawable at any time) pays £150 when you open a sole Barclays Bank Account in the app, complete a full CASS switch from another UK bank with at least three active direct debits, request the switch within 30 days and have £2,000 of salary paid in within 30 days of opening. The £150 arrives within 28 working days of qualifying. After that, up to five further full months each pay £30 when you make at least 10 eligible debit card purchases, receive £2,000 of salary in that month and still hold the account, for a maximum of £300. Someone qualifying in October is assessed for the first £30 in November with payment in December, and so on through March 2027 assessed and April paid. Salary must be Bacs from an employer, a state or private pension, or a transfer from a UK business account to your personal account; transfers between personal accounts do not count. Eligible card spending excludes cash withdrawals, ATMs, gambling, refunds, P2P payments and internal transfers, and pending transactions do not count.

Conditions

UK resident, 18+, opening in the app with a mobile number. You are excluded if you held any Barclays current account on or before 2026-10-01, have had a Barclays CASS bonus before, are opening a joint account or only qualify for a Basic Account. The switch must carry three active direct debits, the first £2,000 salary must land within 30 days, and the total is capped at £300.

Steps

  1. Download the Barclays app and open a sole Barclays Bank Account (finish the application within 21 days)
  2. Complete a full CASS switch within 30 days of opening, carrying three active direct debits
  3. Have £2,000 or more of salary paid in by Bacs within 30 days
  4. For the next five months, make 10 eligible debit card purchases and keep the £2,000 salary arriving

Watch out

Barclays says the offer can be withdrawn at any time and publishes no end date. Salary must arrive by Bacs and cannot be a transfer between personal accounts. The 10 card purchases must clear within the month, and every missed month costs £30 of the £300 ceiling.
