Highlight

After booking a TAROM ticket, the confirmation page, My Booking or online check-in shows an eSIM banner that gives 1GB free across 200+ destinations; the same eSIM can be reused on later trips

What you get

A partnership between TAROM, Romania's SkyTeam carrier, and Transatel's Ubigi (live December 2025): after booking, an eSIM banner appears on the confirmation page, My Booking and online check-in, offering each passenger 1GB of free data plus 30% off a first paid plan. Ubigi covers 200+ destinations on one profile, activates on arrival with Smart Start, supports hotspotting on all plans and allows top-ups with no Wi-Fi or balance.

Conditions

A TAROM booking, with entry only on the confirmation page, My Booking or check-in; 1GB per passenger with paid top-ups after; an unlocked eSIM phone; data only with no local number.

Eligibility

Funding
Purchase required

Steps

  1. Book on tarom.ro and stay on the confirmation page after payment
  2. Tap the eSIM banner, or find the same entry in My Booking or online check-in
  3. Install the Ubigi app, claim the free 1GB and install the eSIM
  4. Smart Start activates it on arrival; buy more data in the app with the 30% first-order discount

Watch out

Without a TAROM booking there is no entry point; TAROM does not publish the validity or country list for this gigabyte, so check it in the app after claiming; Ubigi's site is blocked inside Turkey, so install before any Turkish connection.
