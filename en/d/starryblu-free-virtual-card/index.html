Highlight

A sister brand of Panda Remit with a MAS major payment institution licence and OCBC-segregated funds; the free tier issues a Singapore-BIN 228001 Mastercard that community tests use for Claude.

What you get

Starryblu is a multi-currency account from Singapore's Wotransfer Pte. Ltd., a sister brand of Panda Remit holding MAS major payment institution licence PS20200501 with customer funds segregated at OCBC. It covers CNY, SGD, USD, EUR, HKD, GBP and more, with cross-border transfers and a Mastercard debit card. The free tier issues a virtual Mastercard at no cost (Singapore BIN 228001, usable for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other overseas subscriptions); a physical card ships for about US$9.9. Paid plans at $9.99 and $19.99 a month and $999.99 a year add free physical cards and number choice. Funding runs over Hong Kong FPS, Singapore PayNow or a cross-border wire, and withdrawals back to Alipay or WeChat work with small losses. Since 2026-10-29 the company has been releasing cards in batches, so free-tier users who queued for months can now open one; new registrations still queue, and a whitelist or invite code moves you up.

Conditions

A passport, face check and proof of address are required (a mainland ID card works as address proof). New accounts still queue for release. The free card cannot be added to Apple or Google Wallet per community reports. An invite code must be entered at signup and cannot be added later. Physical cards and plans carry extra fees and auto-renewal.

Steps

  1. Download the app from the official site or an app store, pick your country of residence and enter an invite code if you have one
  2. Photograph the passport photo page, complete the face check and upload proof of address
  3. Once approved, open the card page, choose the free plan and apply for the free virtual card; order a physical card separately if needed
  4. Fund a small amount first to test linking and spending before leaving a balance

Watch out

The queue can run for months and cards are released in batches; this is a payment account, not a bank deposit, so segregated custody is not deposit insurance; Alipay and WeChat QR spending carries noticeable loss; wallet and merchant acceptance varies; watch plan auto-renewal.
