Highlight

A document parsing and structured-extraction API for AI agents. The Standard pay-as-you-go tier starts with $150 of free usage, then bills per page ($1 per 1,000 pages on r-1 mini).

What you get

Reducto is a document platform built for agents: Parse (PDFs, images, tables, scans to structured text), Extract (schema-based field extraction), Split, Classify and Edit, with 30+ file types and no page limits. Its pricing page, read on 2026-11-20, states the Standard pay-as-you-go plan includes "Free $150 in free usage". After that, list rates apply: r-1 mini Parse $1 per 1,000 pages, r-1 Parse $10, Extract $20, Deep Extract and Deep Split $40, Classify $7.50. The same page offers a Startup Program (under $15M funding, under $3M revenue, under 50 employees) with migration credits and discounted Growth pricing.

Conditions

The Standard tier includes $150 of free usage at signup and then switches to metered billing rather than a fixed subscription. The pricing page states no end date. The credit's validity period and whether a card is required are not stated on the pricing page, so check the console. The Startup Program requires an application and eligibility review.

Steps

  1. Sign up at reducto.ai/pricing or app.reducto.ai
  2. Create an API key and confirm $150 of usage is available on the Standard tier
  3. Parse one PDF and check that pages are deducted from the balance
  4. Decide before the balance runs out; do not add a card just to keep the trial

Watch out

The $150 is usage credit, not cash, and deep extraction burns it quickly. No validity period or card requirement is published, so the console is the source of truth. Parsing accuracy still needs spot checks, and confidential documents raise data-retention questions.
