Highlight

New UK customers register with an invite code and send a first transfer of £50+; the reward lands in the Reward Wallet and covers the whole next transfer with no fee; the reward dropped from £10 to £5 in November

What you get

Invite a Friend: a new customer enters a code at signup and, after a first qualifying transfer, the reward lands in Reward Wallet and covers the next transfer in full (the terms set a minimum use of 5 in local currency). The UK offer was £10 for a £50 transfer in early November, but around 10 November many users reported £5, and the help centre says only that the amount is shown on the app's Earn page while listing the UK minimum transfer as £100, which conflicts with the £50 seen in community tests and stays unverified.

Conditions

New NALA customers only; the code must be entered at signup and cannot be added after sending; a first transfer of £50+ (£100 per the help centre, unverified); UK IP, timezone, number, card and passport KYC with the card's billing address matching registration; the reward offsets transfers and cannot be withdrawn.

Steps

  1. Set your phone's time zone to the UK, connect through a UK IP and download the NALA app
  2. Enter the invite code at registration and complete KYC with passport, NFC and a face scan
  3. Send a first transfer of £50+ to the Philippines' Maya (arrives in minutes, no fee)

Watch out

The reward fell from £10 to £5 and can close early "because of demand"; the official minimum is £100 while the community reports £50 — the two don't agree; mismatched address, time zone or IP can get the payment refused or the account banned; the reward only offsets a transfer and can't be cashed out.
