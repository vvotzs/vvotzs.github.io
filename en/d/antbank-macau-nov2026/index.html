What you get

Ant Bank Macau's November 2026 new-customer campaign (11/1–11/30): opening a wealth account earns a USD 10 credit card; net-depositing HK$3,000 equivalent into the wealth account earns a redemption card for 2 Alibaba (09988.HK) shares (about HKD 230) plus commission credits of up to HKD 200 in each of three periods from 2026-12-15 to 2027-03-14; keeping the balance for 30 days earns 2 more Xiaomi shares (01810.HK, about HKD 50); buying 1/5/10 times within 15 days of funding accumulates up to HKD 150 of credit cards. Net-deposit HK$1 million, keep it 90 days and complete 300 or 100 trades to choose between an iPhone DUO 256G and an iPhone 18 Pro 256G.

Conditions

Must open in person in Macau (the app checks location) with a mainland ID plus HK/Macau permit or passport and an immigration slip from the last 7 days; the referrer must be linked before funding; the money must go into the wealth account, not just the bank account; rewards are stock redemption cards that must be redeemed in the securities account; new-customer eligibility is decided by the in-app campaign page.

Steps

  1. Register through the official campaign entry and link the referrer before you travel
  2. In Macau, open the account and verify your identity in the app, then enable the wealth account and securities
  3. Net-deposit HK$3,000 or more into the wealth account
  4. Confirm on the task page; keep the balance 30 days if you want the Xiaomi shares

Watch out

To be confirmed: the details live in the app and the web page won't open, so the terms are whatever the campaign page shows on the day you apply; share values move and the redemption cards have scope and expiry limits; HK$3,000 is tied up for 30 days and travelling to Macau is at your own cost; linking the referrer after funding may not count.
