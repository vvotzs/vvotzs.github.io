What you get

Bitel Peru's November eSIM promotion: activate a new eSIM line in the Mi Bitel app on a postpaid plan between 1 and 30 November 2026 for seven free days of unlimited local data, local calls and texts stacked on the plan's 30 days with nothing paid up front. A new eSIM line, whether a new customer or an extra line; apply in the Mi Bitel app on a postpaid plan; once per customer and not transferable; ported lines, eSIM replacements, international roaming and calls are excluded; an eSIM-capable Android or iOS device.
