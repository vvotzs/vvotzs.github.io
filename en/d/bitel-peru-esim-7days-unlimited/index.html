Highlight

Activate a new Bitel eSIM line in the Mi Bitel app with a postpaid plan and get 7 days of unlimited local data, calls and SMS free, ahead of the normal 30-day period

What you get

Bitel Peru's November eSIM promotion runs from 1 to 30 November 2026: customers who activate a new eSIM line through the Mi Bitel app with a postpaid plan get an extra 7 days of service free — unlimited local data, unlimited calls to any national operator and unlimited SMS — added on top of the plan's normal 30-day period, with no payment needed first. From day 8, the line is suspended if the plan is unpaid and resumes after payment.

Conditions

New eSIM lines only (new customers or existing customers opening an extra line); must be done in the Mi Bitel app with a postpaid plan; once per customer, not cumulative or transferable; excludes ported lines, eSIM replacements, roaming and international calls; requires an eSIM-capable Android or iOS device.

Steps

  1. Download the Mi Bitel app and register
  2. Choose to activate a new eSIM line and sign up for any postpaid plan
  3. The 7 free days of unlimited local data, calls and SMS are applied automatically after activation
  4. Pay the plan before day 8 or the line is suspended

Watch out

It is a postpaid sign-up with 7 free days, not pure free data; the line is suspended if you do not pay after day 7; only usable in Peru; the ID and payment requirements for foreign visitors are not stated officially (to be confirmed).
