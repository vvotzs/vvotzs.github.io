Highlight

Hark Labs' (founded by Brett Adcock) personal AI agent: every Hark Pro feature is free forever with no ads, on web, iOS, Android and Mac. It brings Handoff, its own cloud computer — a real browser, file system and terminal, up to six browsers at once — that signs into sites, places orders, fills forms, runs your email and calendar and builds mini apps for you.

What you get

Hark is the personal AI agent Hark Labs, founded by Brett Adcock, launched on 2026-10-06, positioned as an operating system for your life: everything happens in one long-running chat, with topic threads called Projects and a customisable Home. At its centre is Handoff — every request spins up a dedicated virtual computer with its own browser, file system and terminal, which signs into millions of sites like a person, places orders, cancels subscriptions, fills forms, files expenses, books tables and makes slides. Hark says it can juggle six browsers at once; outside the browser it connects to Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 by API and can read files, query external databases and use MCP. Memory keeps your preferences and context, and Action Buttons (pay a bill or cancel in one tap), Panels (mini apps built from a sentence), Scheduled Tasks and Specialties (multi-step routines it memorises) do the rest. Pricing: the full Hark Pro feature set is free forever with no ads, limited only by a base usage cap whose size Hark has not published; Pro² at $20/mo gives 2x the usage and Pro³ at $100/mo gives 10x, with the same features in every plan. The US App Store also lists Pro⁴ ($259.99, 10x Pro², launched 2026-11-11), and its in-app prices run above the website's. Passwords and cards sit in Secured by Hark, an encrypted vault Hark itself cannot see inside, and it checks with you before payments or orders; Hark says it never sells your data or shares it with advertisers.

Conditions

The free plan needs no invite code and no card; a card is needed only to upgrade to Pro² / Pro³ or switch on auto top-up, which then charges automatically within the amount and ceiling you set. Memory is unavailable in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The free plan has a base usage cap whose size, cost rules and reset are not published; when you hit it you need a higher tier or a top-up to continue. Signing in, paying and ordering require your authorisation and confirmation in Hark, and Handoff relies on the accounts you connect (Gmail, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and so on).

Steps

  1. Sign up at hark.com, or install the app from the App Store, Google Play or hark.com/mac (no card needed for the free plan)
  2. Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365 and other accounts in settings so it can read mail and see your calendar
  3. Ask for what you need in Chat, e.g. "Book a table for two at 7 PM Friday and check parking nearby"; anything that needs the web goes to the Handoff cloud computer
  4. Before paying, ordering or cancelling, check the confirmation card (what it will do and what it costs) and approve it
  5. Need more usage: upgrade to Pro² ($20/mo, 2x) or Pro³ ($100/mo, 10x); the free plan keeps working without an upgrade

Watch out

Hark has not published the free plan's usage cap, how usage is consumed, or when it resets, so "free forever" only promises that no feature is paywalled — heavy use may hit the cap quickly (marked unverified). The ≈$10/mo in the value line is an estimate derived from Pro² at $20 for 2x the usage, not an official price. Once a card is bound or auto top-up is on, it charges when credits run low — stay unbound if you only want the free tier. The EEA, the UK and Switzerland get no Memory, so the experience differs from the US. The agent makes mistakes: check the confirmation card and the result before paying, ordering or cancelling, keep passwords and card numbers in Secured by Hark and never type them into the chat. The US App Store also has Pro⁴, and its in-app prices ($26 / $129.99 / $259.99) are higher than the website's, which is the reference.
