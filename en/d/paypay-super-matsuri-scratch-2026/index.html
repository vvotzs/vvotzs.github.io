Highlight

Live today: pay 200 yen or more in one go with PayPay and a scratch card appears — first prize refunds the whole amount, second 5%, third 0.5%; balance payments win one time in four, PayPay Credit or PayPay Card one in two, and PayPay Card through Apple Pay or Google Pay tap-to-pay always wins; referrals pay 500 points each

What you get

PayPay's official release of 12 November 2026 confirms the Super PayPay Festival starts on 20 November, with PayPay Scratch Cards running to 27 December 23:59. Pay 200 yen or more including tax at a participating store with PayPay or PayPay Card and a scratch card appears in the app: first prize refunds 100% of the payment, second 5%, third 0.5%, capped at 100,000 points per payment and across the whole period. Odds depend on the payment method: PayPay balance (including the balance card and point part-payments) wins one time in four; PayPay Credit or PayPay Card one in two; PayPay balance with PayPay Bank's balance link set up also rises to one in two; and PayPay Card through Apple Pay or Google Pay tap-to-pay at a store always wins (the plastic card's tap does not count). Scratch cards expire 30 days after issue. The festival also distributes PayPay Coupons and runs a PayPay Credit referral programme: the referred person opens a PayPay Card, sets up PayPay Credit and pays once during the period, and both sides get 500 points (referrers up to 10 people, 5,000 points).

Conditions

Completed PayPay eKYC; PayPay Card payments require PayPay Credit to be set up first; payments must be 200 yen or more including tax, points-only payments do not count, and PayPay gift vouchers, PayPay Debit and other-card vouchers are excluded; wins are capped at 100,000 points per payment and per period; unused scratch cards expire after 30 days.

Steps

  1. Complete PayPay eKYC in the app (a My Number card is fastest, often same day)
  2. Set up PayPay Credit, or add your PayPay Card to Apple Pay or Google Pay in the app
  3. Pay 200 yen or more in one go at a store with the campaign poster and scratch the card on the spot
  4. Use the points within 30 days before they expire

Watch out

The draw is not guaranteed and odds apply per scratch; raising your odds means opening a card and setting up credit, so do not spend more just to scratch; points cannot be withdrawn or transferred; overseas physical stores are excluded from this card.
