Highlight

All desktop IDEs and Ultimate subscriptions free, renewed yearly, then 40% off for two years after graduating

What you get

Students and teachers can apply for a JetBrains educational licence and use IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, PyCharm, WebStorm, CLion, GoLand and every other desktop IDE plus Ultimate subscriptions for free while enrolled, renewed each year; after graduating you keep 40% off for two years.

Conditions

Must be enrolled at an accredited school (university/college); verify with a school email, an ISIC/ITIC student card or the GitHub Student Pack; renew once a year.

Steps

  1. Open the JetBrains student application page and verify with your school email or the GitHub Student Pack
  2. Click the confirmation link in the email
  3. Download the IDEs and sign in with your JetBrains Account

Watch out

The licence runs for a year: forget to renew and it lapses; commercial use must follow the educational licence terms.
