Highlight

The help centre states the Always Free plan has no trial, no expiry and no card: 6 GB RAM, 1.5 cores, 10 GB disk and up to 4 servers, plus 10 GB of U3 object storage and 1 GB of KV; servers do not sleep when idle and can run Node, Bun, Python or Docker images

What you get

Altare hosts game servers, bots and small apps. Its help centre and Chinese site both state that every team starts on Always Free: 6 GB (6,144 MiB) of RAM, 150% CPU (1.5 cores), 10 GB of disk and 4 server slots, shared as one pool you split as you like, plus 10 GB of U3 object storage (5 buckets, 10 keys) and 1 GB of KV (3 million reads and 300,000 writes a month). There is no trial, no expiry and no payment details, and servers do not sleep when nobody is online. Templates cover Minecraft (Paper, Forge, Vanilla, Sponge, BungeeCord), Rust, CS:GO, TF2 and ARK, plus Node.js, Bun and Python runtimes for bots and apps. More resources can be bought with bolts earned from daily rewards, AFK time and referrals, which is optional.

Conditions

One 6 GB/10 GB pool per team and multi-accounting is not allowed (each user can join at most 2 teams); long-running probes or mining have been banned in reported cases; containers only, so host mounts, privileged images and some port mappings are off-limits.

Steps

  1. Sign up at console.altare.gg with an email (no card needed)
  2. Link Discord to collect bolts and daily rewards
  3. Create one server and give it the full 6 GB and 10 GB, which is steadier than splitting
  4. Pick a Node, Python or Minecraft template and avoid privileged containers

Watch out

The host's disk is tight and free containers can be reclaimed; running probes or staying at full load has led to bans; it is not a full VPS, so privileged images and host mounts fail; registration sometimes returns 522 and needs retrying later.
