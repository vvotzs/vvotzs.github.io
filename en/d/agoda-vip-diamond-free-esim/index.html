Highlight

Agoda VIP Diamond members activate a free 1GB, 7-day eSIM inside the Agoda app for eligible destinations, so the phone is online the moment they land

What you get

Agoda's press release of 11 February 2026 launched a free eSIM for VIP Diamond members: 1GB for seven days, activated in the Agoda app on iOS or Android before departure at several eligible destinations, covering the gap before a local SIM. Diamond is the top AgodaVIP tier, which third parties put at 15 bookings and US$1,500 within 24 months (Agoda does not publish the bar), with stays, flights and activities all counting toward status.

Conditions

AgodaVIP Diamond membership based on bookings and spend, with the bar unpublished; claim and activate only in the Agoda app; coverage is limited to the eligible list at checkout; 1GB lasts seven days with paid top-ups after; data only with no number.

Steps

  1. Sign in to the Agoda app and confirm VIP Diamond status
  2. Open the VIP benefits page and find the free eSIM entry
  3. Choose a destination and activate, installing the eSIM on Wi-Fi before departure
  4. Enable data roaming for that eSIM after landing and use it within the seven days

Watch out

The threshold is high and non-Diamond accounts see no entry; Agoda has not published how many eSIMs a member may claim or the full destination list; 1GB only covers light use over a week; the feature may roll out market by market.
