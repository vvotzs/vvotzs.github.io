Users who have never used Gate Convert can register and make one Convert trade of at least 1 USDT to get 20 USDT (first 500 only). Trade 20 USDT a day to check in for 10 WLFI, out of an 80,000 WLFI pool.

What you get

Gate's official announcement dated 2026-11-19 09:00 UTC runs the Convert new-user campaign until 2026-11-26 09:00 UTC. Event 1, New User Convert Gift: after registering on the event page and passing identity verification, a user who has never completed a Convert trade on Gate gets 20 USDT for their first Convert trade of at least 1 USDT. Conditions

Click Join Now on the event page and complete KYC first. A Convert new user means no Convert trade completed before registering for the event. Event 1 is capped at the first 500 qualifying users. Event 2 counts UTC calendar days with at least 20 USDT of Convert volume, and missed days cannot be made up. Rewards are paid as Dual Investment trial funds (event 1) and WLFI spot (event 2), not withdrawable USDT.

Steps

  1. Sign in to Gate, open the event page, click Join Now and complete KYC

Watch out

The 20 USDT is Dual Investment trial funds rather than withdrawable cash. The first 500 places can run out. Check-ins follow UTC days with no catch-up. KYC is required and some regions are excluded. WLFI is volatile, so its real value is below face value.
