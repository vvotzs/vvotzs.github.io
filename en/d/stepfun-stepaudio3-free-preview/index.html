Highlight

Realtime duplex voice, TTS with instant voice cloning, sound-effect generation, AI music and ASR all at zero cost until noon on Nov 23; no card and no prepayment.

What you get

StepFun's open platform has extended the free window for its StepAudio 3 voice family to 11:59:59 Beijing time on 2026-11-23; from 12:00:00 the new prices apply and the preview builds give way to paid releases. The free `-preview` model IDs are `stepaudio-3-realtime-preview` (end-to-end duplex realtime voice with barge-in), `stepaudio-3-chat-preview` (voice understanding over Chat Completions), `stepaudio-3-gen-preview` (text to sound effects and ambience) and `stepaudio-3-music-preview` (songs and instrumentals); `stepaudio-3-tts` (emotional TTS, seconds-long cloning, text-designed voices) and `stepaudio-3-asr-max` (long-form transcription with timestamps) are described as covered too. List prices for reference: realtime voice ¥0.65 per session minute (¥39 per hour), TTS and audio generation ¥95 per million billed characters, cloning or voice design ¥10 each, music ¥16 (no audio input) or ¥32 (with audio input) per output hour, ASR ¥7.50 per audio hour.

Conditions

Register on the platform and create an API key; personal accounts generally need real-name verification for voice models. No card, no prepayment. Billing resumes after 11:59:59 on Nov 23. Whether TTS and standalone ASR are fully inside the free window differs between sources, so check the console (unverified).

Steps

  1. Register at platform.stepfun.com, complete verification and create an API key
  2. Call the model names carrying the -preview suffix; try voices first in the playground
  3. Stop any scripts or background calls before 12:00 on Nov 23 so the new prices do not bite

Watch out

New prices apply automatically from noon on Nov 23, so a call left running will be charged. The exact set of free models should be confirmed in the console. Verification data falls under the platform's terms.
