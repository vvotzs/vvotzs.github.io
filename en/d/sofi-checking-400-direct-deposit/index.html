Highlight

Take $1,000–$4,999.99 in qualifying direct deposits within 25 days of your first one for $50, or $5,000+ for $400; no monthly fee, no minimum balance

What you get

SoFi Checking and Savings direct-deposit bonus: between 2026-05-15 and 2026-12-31, once your first qualifying direct deposit of $1 or more lands, SoFi adds up qualifying direct deposits over the following 25 calendar days — $1,000–$4,999.99 pays $50 and $5,000 or more pays $400, paid within 7 business days of qualifying. The account has no monthly fee and no minimum balance, and pays two days early.

Conditions

New and existing SoFi members qualify as long as they haven't set up a direct deposit with SoFi before; one per person; a qualifying direct deposit must be recurring ACH from an employer, payroll provider or government agency (salary, pension, Social Security); cheque deposits, P2P such as Zelle/PayPal/Venmo/Wise, merchant transfers, ACH transfers from other banks, wires and tax refunds don't count; the account must be in good standing at payout.

Steps

  1. Open SoFi Checking and Savings through the invite link or the website
  2. Switch your direct deposit to SoFi in your employer's payroll system (or set up a government benefit deposit)
  3. Confirm the first direct deposit of $1 or more has landed, then accumulate $5,000 within 25 days
  4. Check the bonus in the checking account within 7 business days

Watch out

Reaching the $400 tier means moving $5,000 in within 25 days, which really means relocating your salary; SoFi decides what counts as a direct deposit and some payroll providers need a manual appeal; the bonus is taxable.
