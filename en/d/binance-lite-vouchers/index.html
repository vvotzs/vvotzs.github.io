Highlight

A first $50 fiat/card/P2P buy plus $100 of spot volume unlock two fee-rebate vouchers

What you get

Register through a referral link (Lite mode) and new users get two fee-rebate vouchers: one of up to $100 for a first fiat, card or P2P purchase of $50+, and one of up to $1,000 for $100 in combined spot or Convert volume. Binance sets each voucher's face value when it is issued.

Conditions

New accounts registered through a referral only; fiat, card and P2P purchases count toward the first task but on-chain deposits do not; activate each voucher within seven days and spend it within 14 days of activation.

Steps

  1. Sign up with the referral link and complete KYC
  2. Buy $50 or more of crypto with a credit/debit card or P2P
  3. Complete $100 of spot or Convert volume
  4. Activate the fee vouchers under Rewards and use them within 14 days

Watch out

The vouchers only offset spot trading fees, so they are worth $0 if you don't trade; the "up to" amounts aren't guaranteed — what the issued voucher says is all you get.
