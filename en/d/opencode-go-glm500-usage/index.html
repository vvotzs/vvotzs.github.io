Highlight

Operation GLM phase two: the Go plan's monthly GLM-5.3 allowance is temporarily raised to $500 (from $200) and Go Plus to $1,000, for new and existing subscribers, with the official docs stating it runs only through 2026-11-22.

What you get

OpenCode announced Operation GLM on 2026-11-17 with a phase one offer of $100 of GLM-5.3 usage for $10 on Go, then moved to phase two about eleven hours later: the same $10 covers $500 of GLM-5.3 usage, and Go Plus at $40 covers $1,000. The official Go docs, read on 2026-11-20, state "GLM-5.3: Higher usage limits apply through November 22, 2026". The $500 is the monthly ceiling the plan covers if you use only that model, not account balance; every paid model on Go draws on the same allowance, with 20% of the monthly allowance available per 5-hour window and 50% per week, resetting Mondays at 00:00 UTC. Subscribe on the OpenCode Console, then run /connect in OpenCode, choose OpenCode Go, paste the key and pick GLM-5.3 with /models, or call https://opencode.ai/inference/go directly.

Conditions

Requires an OpenCode Go ($10/month) or Go Plus ($40/month) subscription with a payment method. The raised allowance ends 2026-11-22 and then reverts. The $500 is a usage ceiling, not withdrawable balance. Limits are 20% per 5 hours and 50% per week, shared with other paid models. Users in China must set Regions to Global under Settings › Privacy to reach GLM-5.3.

Steps

  1. Sign in at opencode.ai/console, subscribe to Go, add billing details and copy the API key
  2. Run /connect in OpenCode, choose OpenCode Go and paste the key
  3. Select GLM-5.3 with /models and work normally
  4. Use the allowance before 11/22 and cancel the subscription once it reverts if you do not need it

Watch out

The $500 is a usage cap, not cash, and cannot be read as a 20% discount on real money. The 5-hour and weekly limits are real and heavy users will hit them, users report 529 errors at peak times, and the allowance drops after 11/22, so do not budget against it.
