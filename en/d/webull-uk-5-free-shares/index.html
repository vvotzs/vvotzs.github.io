Highlight

New Webull UK customers who deposit £500 in one go and hold it for 60 days can claim five US fractional shares in the Rewards centre, each worth $3 to $300, plus 12 months of Nasdaq TotalView Level 2 and a 90-day commission-free card.

What you get

BeerMoneyUK, read on 2026-11-20 (listed three weeks ago), records Webull UK's new-customer offer. After signing up and verifying identity you must opt in manually under Menu → Promotion Centre, then make a single deposit of at least £500 (splitting it does not count, and transferring shares in does), hold it for 60 consecutive days, and claim five US fractional shares from My Rewards within 30 days. Each share is worth $3 to $300, but community reports say most people get roughly $3 Apple shares, about £11 in total. The offer also includes 12 months of free Nasdaq TotalView Level 2 data, a 90-day UK shares and ETFs commission-free card for a GIA or ISA, and, with options enabled, 90 days of commission-free contracts up to 100 plus 30 days of OPRA real-time data. The campaign asks for the deposit by 2027-03-31.

Conditions

New Webull UK customers only, and opting in under Promotion Centre is mandatory. A single deposit of at least £500 must stay for 60 consecutive days, with withdrawals allowed as long as they do not exceed what was deposited. Rewards must be claimed within 30 days of becoming available or they expire, and each share's value is random with most at the low end.

Steps

  1. Sign up through the offer page and verify identity in the Webull app
  2. Opt in under Menu → Promotion Centre before depositing, or there is no reward
  3. Deposit at least £500 in one go and leave it for 60 consecutive days
  4. Claim the five shares in My Rewards within 30 days of them appearing

Watch out

The five shares are random and most people get about $3 each, so do not expect anything near the $1,500 headline. Forgetting to opt in or to claim within 30 days wastes the 60-day hold. The £500 is locked for 60 days, investing carries risk, and a throwaway account brings idle-fee and tax-reporting chores.
