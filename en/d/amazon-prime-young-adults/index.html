Highlight

18–24s or enrolled students get 6 months of Prime for $0, with Prime Video, free delivery and exclusive discounts; $7.49/mo after

What you get

Amazon's membership for younger customers: 18–24s or enrolled college students get 6 months for $0, then $7.49/mo or $69/yr (about half of regular Prime), including free delivery, Prime Video, Amazon Music Prime, Prime Gaming and unlimited photo storage, plus 5% back in some categories. Age is verified with a driving licence, passport or ID, or a .edu email for students; at 25 or after study ends it rolls into regular Prime.

Conditions

New US-site members only; 18–24 or enrolled at a two- or four-year college; age or student verification required; after 6 months it charges $7.49/mo automatically, so cancel in advance if you don't want it.

Steps

  1. Open amazon.com/joinstudent (or amazon.com/youngadult)
  2. Choose Prime for Young Adults and complete the age or .edu verification
  3. Confirm the 6 months at $0 and add a payment method
  4. Cancel before it ends if you don't want to continue

Watch out

It auto-renews at $7.49/mo after 6 months, so set a reminder to cancel; US site only, needing a US payment method and address; when student status ends it becomes regular Prime.
