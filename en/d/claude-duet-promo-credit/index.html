Highlight

Accounts that already had both Claude Duet and a paid US plan before Nov 23 get the credit added in stages: $500 a week, up to $3,000 in total, with nothing to claim and unused weekly credit reset to zero

What you get

From Nov 23 to Dec 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT, Anthropic adds promotional credit to some Claude Duet accounts: $500 a week, capped at $3,000, following each account's weekly reset. The credit shows up as Claude Duet credit under Settings > Usage on the web, with nothing to apply for or buy. It is spent inside Claude Duet at standard API rates before your plan limits, but it does not reset those limits and does not cover Cowork sessions, Claude Code sessions started by Duet, fast mode, Claude Fable, or long-context work that needs usage credits. Unused weekly credit expires at each reset; the final week's credit lasts until Dec 31.

Conditions

The eligibility list closed at 00:00 PT on Nov 23, 2026: it covers accounts that already had Claude Duet and a paid US plan (personal Pro/Max/Ultra billed in the US or a US territory with no plan billed elsewhere; Team uses its billing country on file) and had actually used Duet. New subscriptions, new Team seats and turning Duet on later do not qualify. One credit per person, and accounts that fit both a personal plan and Team get it on the personal plan.

Steps

  1. Open Settings > Usage on the Claude web app and look for Claude Duet credit
  2. Use Claude Duet normally; the credit is deducted first automatically
  3. Check each account or Team seat you have, one at a time
  4. Spend the week's $500 before the reset, since unused credit expires

Watch out

The list is closed, so new and newly enabled Duet accounts cannot take part; it does not cover Cowork, Claude Code sessions Duet starts, fast mode, Fable or long context; weekly credit expires; cancelling, going past due, pausing or moving to the free plan ends it, and Team credit does not follow a moved seat.
