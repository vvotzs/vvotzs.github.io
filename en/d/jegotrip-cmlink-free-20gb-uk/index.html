What you get

China Mobile International keeps using its JegoTrip-CMLink app for acquisitions. A new user who signs in with a UK number can claim a CMLink UK eSIM for zero under Profile: one +44 number and 20GB of local high-speed data worth GBP 9. Batches of roughly 1,000 were released in May, June and July 2026 and sold out the same day; signing up with a Japanese, Thai or Singapore number only yields a 1GB China/Hong Kong travel pack. The number has no monthly fee and receives SMS for free but cannot make calls; activate within a year of claiming and the 20GB lasts 28 days. Activation must happen outside mainland China, on a UK or Hong Kong cell or over Wi-Fi Calling, and community reports say the Wi-Fi Calling route tightened in late 2026. Conditions
