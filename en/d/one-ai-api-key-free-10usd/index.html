What you get

Signing up gives $10 of credit and one gateway key. The endpoint speaks both OpenAI and Anthropic formats, using https://api.oneaiapikey.com/v1 and the bare domain respectively. The free lane is shared by six model ids with a :free suffix: 100 requests and 5 million tokens a day, resetting at 05:30 IST (00:00 UTC). The $10 is spent first at each model's discounted rate, and the six that bill $0 per token keep working after it runs out; when a model is busy, requests fall back to Qwen3.8 Max, Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Opus 5.5. The site lists 30 models and also sells a flat $25 a month unlimited single-model plan, with setup guides for Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor, Cline and Aider.

Conditions

Register with Google or an email, no card. Each account gets 100 requests and 5 million tokens a day shared across the six ids. Keys can carry spend caps, model allow-lists and expiry. Your client must accept a custom base URL.

Steps

  1. Open oneaiapikey.com and sign in with Google or an email
  2. Copy the gateway API key from the console
  3. Point your client's base URL at https://api.oneaiapikey.com/v1 (the bare domain for Anthropic format)
  4. Send a test request to a model id with the :free suffix and check the usage page

Watch out

This is a third-party gateway, not an official Anthropic, OpenAI or Google channel, so upstream compliance and uptime are unguaranteed and the service can change or close at any time. Requests pass through its servers, so keep sensitive data and your own upstream keys out of it. Model names and backends are not transparent, the free pool is shared and queues or model fallbacks are normal at peak, and the site is new with no SLA.
