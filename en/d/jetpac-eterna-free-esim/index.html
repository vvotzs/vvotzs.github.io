Highlight

Claim once and it lasts for life: 100MB per trip, up to 4 times a year (2 months apart); WhatsApp / Google Maps / Uber / Grab use zero data (throttled to 1Mbps)

What you get

Jetpac Eterna is a "lifetime free" travel eSIM: once registered and claimed, the eSIM stays valid and arrives with 100MB, and every 2 months you can claim another 100MB in the app, up to 4 times a year (about 400MB in total). Even after the 100MB is gone, WhatsApp, Google Maps, Uber and Grab keep working without limit (throttled to about 1Mbps) and don't use the free allowance. It's data only, with no number and no calls or texts, and works only while roaming abroad, not at home. NodeSeek threads of 2026-10-07 and 11-04, the Naixi forum on 10-07 and the Shaobing forum on 11-04 all report "restocked again".

Conditions

Only for eligible users in the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, New Zealand and France, and registration needs a phone number from one of them for the code (forums report Google Voice passes); must be installed in the Jetpac app; can't be used as domestic data; one account per person; anyone with a paid Jetpac plan has to wait until it ends; contact support before changing phones or reinstalling.

Steps

  1. Open the Jetpac Eterna claim page, tap redeem and fill in name, mobile and email
  2. Choose the US or Canada as the country (it goes through more smoothly) and verify with a number that receives codes
  3. Download the Jetpac app, sign in with the same email and install the eSIM
  4. Enable the line abroad; after that you can claim another 100MB in the app every 2 months

Watch out

100MB a time is very little and only an arrival stopgap; the official terms limit it to eligible international roaming and exclude domestic use; the zero-data apps are only those four and throttled to 1Mbps, so it's not main data.
