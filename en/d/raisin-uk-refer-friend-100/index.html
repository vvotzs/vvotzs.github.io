What you get

Register through a referral link, then make your first Raisin savings account a fixed rate bond of one year or more funded with at least £10,000, and you and the referrer each get £100 into your Transaction Accounts, usually within 28 days of opening and funding. The £10,000 must all go into that one bond rather than being split across accounts, and the bond must be your first savings account; open and fund within 365 days of registering, with no other new-customer codes stacked on top.

Conditions

UK resident, 18+, new to Raisin UK; register through the referral link; the first savings account must be a one-year-plus fixed bond with a single deposit of £10,000+ (not split); open and fund within 365 days of registering; no stacking with other new-customer codes.

Steps

  1. Register through a friend's Raisin UK referral link and complete full identity verification
