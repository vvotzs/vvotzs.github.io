What you get

Openference's official free-model page lists GLM-5.3-Flash-Free (free through Dec 4, 2026 UTC) and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017-Free (free through Dec 31, 2026 UTC) at $0 per million tokens, with 1M context, image and video input and tool calling, and calls that draw on neither the plan allowance nor the balance. Free models answer only with streaming (send stream:true, otherwise 400 streaming_required); an account without a plan needs at least $1 in credits, otherwise 402 free_model_requires_credits. Free usage has its own fair-use limits: too many in-flight requests return 429, and an exhausted daily allowance returns 429 with resets_at.
