Highlight

AED 0 including a new number, self-activated with a face scan, usable straight after landing at Abu Dhabi or Sharjah airport

What you get

e& UAE's Free Visitor Line: priced AED 0 (+5% VAT), with 10GB of UAE local data, 24 hours of validity and a new number. Tourists scan the on-site QR code after immigration, upload their passport and pass a face check to self-activate the eSIM; the eSIM can also be chosen on e&'s Visitor Line page. Since November 2025 Abu Dhabi airport and e& have given the same 10GB one-day card to all international arrivals, and Sharjah airport followed in October of that year. After 24 hours you need a Visitor Line plan to continue (4GB/28 days AED 46.66, 10GB/28 days AED 94.28, 7 days unlimited AED 190.47 and so on, all plus 5% VAT).

Conditions

Inbound tourists to the UAE only, 18+, with a valid passport and visa or entry record; at most 2 Visitor Lines per passport; UAE local data only; the eSIM must be requested inside the UAE (not while roaming); an eSIM-compatible unlocked phone.

Steps

  1. After immigration, scan the airport QR code or choose Free Visitor Line (AED 0) on eand.ae's Visitor Line page
  2. Select eSIM as the SIM type, upload your passport and finish the face check
  3. Install the eSIM QR code you receive on your phone
  4. Turn on data roaming on arrival and use the 10GB within 24 hours

Watch out

Only 24 hours; after that, out-of-plan data is charged by usage (third-party guides say about AED 1.05/20MB capped at AED 9.45 a day, to be confirmed), so turn data off or buy a plan before it ends; the eSIM must be requested inside the UAE.
