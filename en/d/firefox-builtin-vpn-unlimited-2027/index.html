Highlight

Sign in with a Mozilla account and the built-in VPN in Firefox for desktop and Android drops its 50GB monthly cap until Feb 1, 2027

What you get

Mozilla announced that from 10 November 2026 through 31 January 2027 the free built-in VPN in Firefox has no monthly data cap, replacing the usual 50GB. It applies automatically once you are signed in to Firefox with a Mozilla account, and the VPN panel shows unlimited usage. The built-in VPN only routes Firefox browsing traffic through an encrypted proxy to hide your IP; it does not cover other apps. The 50GB cap returns at 00:00 UTC on 1 February 2027.

Conditions

Must be signed in with a Mozilla account; only in regions where the built-in VPN is available; covers Firefox traffic only, not the rest of the device; does not hide local browsing history.

Steps

  1. Update Firefox on desktop or Android
  2. Sign in to or create a Mozilla account
  3. Turn on the VPN icon in the toolbar (desktop) or Settings → VPN (Android)
  4. The panel shows unlimited; you can switch among 28 locations

Watch out

Only Firefox traffic is protected, so downloads and other apps still use your real connection; some sites add checks or slow down behind a VPN; the 50GB cap returns automatically on 1 February 2027.
