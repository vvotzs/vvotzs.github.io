Highlight

New customers click through from a cashback site, register via the link and send a first transfer of £100+: TopCashback £8, Quidco £6.40 (Premium rate), KidStart up to £15; Remitly's own first-transfer fee waiver or rate boost applies at the same time, but don't add third-party promo codes

What you get

The cashback route for new UK Remitly customers (checked 2026-11-19): click "Get cashback" on a UK cashback site's Remitly page, register a UK Remitly account in the same browser session and complete a first international transfer, and the site pays a fixed amount. TopCashback UK currently pays £8 (new customers, first transfer of £100+); Quidco shows £6.40 but marks it as the Premium rate, with standard members getting about £6; KidStart pays up to £15 by tier, though the money goes into a linked child's savings account and can't be withdrawn directly; ShopBuddies up to £8 and Rakuten up to £7. Cashback usually shows as pending within 24 hours and becomes withdrawable only once Remitly confirms the transfer qualifies (Quidco states payment within 6 months). In the same signup, Remitly's own first-transfer offers (fee waiver, rate boost, first-transfer discount — whatever the registration page finally quotes) usually apply automatically and stack with the cashback; but cashback sites generally void it for "using promo codes not approved by us", so entering a third-party referral code or coupon risks losing the cashback.

Conditions

New Remitly customers only (never registered or sent); first transfer of at least £100 (both TopCashback and Quidco terms say £100, though some comparison sites still show £200 — the order page decides); you must arrive through the cashback site's link and finish registration and payment in the same browser session with ad blockers and coupon extensions off; TopCashback excludes unapproved promo codes and Quidco excludes extra discount codes that reduce the transaction value, so stacking a friend's referral code can void it; Quidco's £6.40 needs Premium (£1 a month taken from cashback, often free to try for new members).

Steps

  1. Register or sign in to TopCashback UK or Quidco (or check GotCashback first to see who pays most that day)
  2. Search Remitly on the cashback site and click Get cashback, with ad blockers and coupon extensions off
  3. Register a new UK Remitly account in the same browser session, complete KYC and confirm the first-transfer offer has applied
  4. Send a first transfer of £100+ to a third-party recipient (not yourself and not the inviter) and let it be delivered
  5. Check the pending record on the cashback site within 24 hours; it becomes withdrawable after the merchant confirms (Quidco up to 6 months)

Watch out

The "$25 off Remitly" links circulating online are almost all US-only codes and do nothing in the UK; cashback is confirmed after the fact, so a cancelled transfer, closed account or merchant rejection means nothing at all — don't budget it into the first transfer; KidStart's £15 is a tier cap and only goes into a child's savings account; "fee-free" usually just moves the cost into the FX spread, so compare what the recipient actually receives.
