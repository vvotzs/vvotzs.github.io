Highlight

Since Foxl 0.10.7 every account can chat with Foxl's own models on Foxl credits: a new account starts with 200 and needs neither an API key nor a subscription; a friend who signs up through your link and uses Foxl credits on two different UTC days adds 100 more; the old 200-requests-a-day cap on free accounts is gone, replaced by a 1,500-a-day safety limit

What you get

Foxl's official v0.10.7 release notes (13 Nov 2026) confirm: a new account starts with 200 credits however you sign up; each friend invited who uses Foxl credits on two different UTC days adds 100, and bursts of invites are checked and added over several days. At most 5 new accounts a day can be credited per network, and on busy days they arrive from 00:00 UTC oldest first. With no computer connected the cloud answers on Claude Haiku 5.5 using your credits, with hourly and daily caps on replies; when credits run out Foxl points you to inviting friends.

Conditions

Install Foxl 0.10.7 or later (desktop or mobile) or use app.foxl.ai; 200 credits per new account; at most 5 new accounts a day per network; referral credits require the friend to use Foxl credits on two different UTC days; top up by inviting or paying once credits run out.

Steps

  1. Install Foxl on desktop or mobile, or open app.foxl.ai and sign up
  2. Confirm the account shows 200 credits
  3. Get your invite link under Settings > Account > Invite friends
  4. Have a friend sign up through it and use Foxl credits on two days
  5. Check the referral credits, which may arrive over several days

Watch out

200 credits are worth little and heavy use drains them quickly; anti-abuse checks delay or block rewards from multi-accounting on one network; cloud answers without a connected computer run on Haiku 5.5 with rate limits.
