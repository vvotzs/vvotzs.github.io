Highlight

Brand-new customers who open with referral code NTB2026 and place a 1-month HKD time deposit get 20% p.a. on the first HK$80,000 (about HK$1,315 of interest in month one); deposit another HK$20,000 by 30 November for an extra HK$200

What you get

Ping An Digital Bank, formerly PAObank, runs a November welcome: from 1 to 30 November 2026 new customers enter NTB2026 when opening a personal savings account and place a one-month HKD time deposit, with the first HK$80,000 earning 20% a year — the bank's example puts the first month's interest on HK$80,000 at about HK$1,315 — and depositing HK$20,000 into the savings account by 30 November adds HK$200 cash, about HK$1,515 in all. Referrers earn HK$300 each, with the regular referral cap raised to HK$12,000 and a HK$26,888 top-referrer prize.

Conditions

New customers with no prior account at the bank; the code must be entered during opening and cannot be added later; the 20% deposit covers HK$100 to HK$80,000 for one month only, opened in the promotional section under Deposits then Time Deposits; the HK$200 needs HK$20,000 in the savings account by 30 November; Hong Kong residents or visitors in the city; limited places first come.

Steps

  1. Download the Ping An Digital Bank app and choose new-customer onboarding (have your ID or passport, HK/Macau permit or visa and proof of presence in Hong Kong ready)
  2. Enter NTB2026 in the referral code field during the application (without it there's no 20% deposit and no HK$200)
  3. After opening, go to Deposit > Time Deposit and open a 1-month HKD time deposit of HK$100–80,000 in the promotional slot
  4. Deposit another HK$20,000 into the personal savings account by 30 November and wait for the HK$200
  5. When the deposit matures it rolls to the base rate — decide whether to renew or move it

Watch out

The 20% is an annual rate on a 1-month deposit, not free cash: the HK$1,315 comes from tying up HK$80,000 for a month; non-residents must travel to Hong Kong at their own cost; the code and terms are whatever the in-app page shows that day, and places are limited; the bank has no physical or virtual debit card.
