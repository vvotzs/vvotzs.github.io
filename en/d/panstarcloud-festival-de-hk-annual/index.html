Highlight

On sale on the official product pages: Frankfurt DEPro Festival with 1 vCPU, 1GB RAM, 10GB disk, a 1Gbps port, 2TiB (2048GiB) of two-way monthly traffic and IPv4 plus IPv6 at $47.88 a year; Hong Kong HKPro Festival with 1 vCPU, 512MB RAM, 5GB disk, a 100Mbps port, 384GiB a month and IPv4 only at $99 a year. No coupon needed, stock shown as Available

What you get

Official product pages read on 28 November 2026: Frankfurt DEPro Festival is $47.88 a year for 1 vCPU, 1GB RAM, 10GB disk, a 1Gbps port, 2048GiB of two-way traffic a month and IPv4 plus IPv6; Hong Kong HKPro Festival is $99 a year for 1 vCPU, 512MB RAM, 5GB disk, a 100Mbps port, 384GiB of two-way traffic and IPv4 only. Both bill on a 12-month term, need no coupon and show stock as Available. The matching monthly plans are DEPro Mini at $6.99 a month and HKPro Mini at $23.99 a month, so the annual plans work out to about $3.99 and $8.25 a month. Germany returns over AS9929 on all three Chinese carriers, Hong Kong over China Telecom 163PP, China Unicom 4837/10099 and China Mobile CMI. When traffic runs out you can buy a reset: $10.99 per use in Germany, $12.99 in Hong Kong, once per billing cycle.

Conditions

Annual only, paid up front for 12 months; no coupon needed and stock is limited; a new standard cloud order can be self-cancelled for a full refund within 6 hours of delivery if combined traffic stays at or under 10GiB and you have used the allowance at most 3 times in 30 days; the Hong Kong plan has IPv4 only, the German plan includes IPv6.

Steps

  1. Open Cloud Servers on panstar.ai and pick the Frankfurt (EU Central) region
  2. Choose the DE Pro Premium product, then the DEPro Festival plan on a 12-month billing cycle
  3. For Hong Kong, switch to the HongKong (AP East) region and pick HKPro Festival, comparing memory and traffic
  4. Register, log in and order, checking the annual total and any tax at checkout; if it disappoints you, self-cancel within 6 hours of delivery under the refund policy

Watch out

This is a merchant that opened in 2026, and public reviews describe the routes as sometimes fast and sometimes slow with high retransmission, so keep stability-critical production databases elsewhere. The renewal price and the claim that traffic over the cap drops you to 1Mbps instead of suspending the server both come from third parties; the official pages only list the traffic reset price, so renewal and throttling terms are unverified. The official pages name no campaign or end date, so prices can revert at any time. A year is paid up front, so a shutdown or exit costs you the rest of the term. The 512MB plan is only enough for networking; panels and databases need a larger plan.
