What you get

GigSky's long-running partnership with Visa: download the GigSky app and verify your own Visa card for up to 3GB of global data covering 175+ countries; selected Visa destinations also get up to 7 days unlimited; all GigSky plans give Visa cardholders up to 30% off. The allowance differs by card issuer — some Visa Infinite / Signature cards get 1GB and certain US banks have 3GB or 5GB tiers — so what the app shows after verification is what counts.

Conditions

Must hold a Visa card (debit or credit depending on the issuer) and verify with the card number in the app; new GigSky users only; data activates automatically on connecting in a covered country and must be used within its validity; one claim per card/user; the benefit can be withdrawn if the card is closed or no longer valid; excludes your home country, cruises, in-flight and maritime networks.

Steps

  1. Download the GigSky app and register
  2. Open the Visa benefits page and enter your Visa card number to check eligibility
  3. Choose the free allowance tier and destination
  4. Install the eSIM; it activates on connecting at the destination — set the GigSky line as the data line as instructed

Watch out

The allowance depends on card type and issuer, and an ordinary card may get only 1GB or nothing; you must enter the card number in the app, which raises privacy questions; the free data has an expiry and can lapse if the eSIM is never activated; GigSky can change or end the partnership.
