Highlight

A 3HK prepaid card for HK$33, bought as an eSIM and scanned in about ten minutes, with an extra 50GB for new cards (100GB in total) and a free SIM-to-eSIM coupon.

What you get

SoSIM is Hutchison Telecom Hong Kong's (3HK) prepaid brand. The new-customer offer runs from 2026-09-01 to 11-30: activate a new card in the window and pick the 50GB Hong Kong package as the default, and the first 30 days give 100GB of Hong Kong data (the 50GB package plus 50GB extra), with 1,500 local minutes included. The card costs HK$33 and stays valid for 120 days; activate it within 60 days of purchase. The eSIM can be bought online: the QR code goes to your verified email after payment and the fastest activation is about ten minutes, with no plastic. New cards also get the 'two-way privilege': a free SIM-to-eSIM conversion coupon (normally HK$28) and a HK$8 number change, delivered to Rewards > My Coupons within five working days and valid for seven days. For travel, the same HK$33 can buy five days of Asia-Pacific roaming data if you switch within 48 hours of activation.

Conditions

Real-name registration is required for use in Hong Kong. Activate within 60 days of purchase and the card lasts 120 days from activation. The extra 50GB only applies to cards activated before Nov 30 with the 50GB Hong Kong package as default. The device must support eSIM, and travel data has to be switched within 48 hours.

Steps

  1. Buy the eSIM on the SoSIM online shop for HK$33 (Visa/Mastercard, Alipay, WeChat Pay accepted)
  2. Scan the QR code sent to your email and activate, which takes about ten minutes
  3. Complete real-name registration in the app and set the default package to 50GB Hong Kong data to get the extra 50GB
  4. Collect the free conversion and number-change coupons under Rewards > My Coupons and use them within seven days

Watch out

This is not free: HK$33 is a real payment, local data needs real-name registration, the package must be renewed after 120 days, roaming calls and texts are expensive (HK$8 a minute to receive, HK$3.5 a text), and travel data must be switched within 48 hours.
