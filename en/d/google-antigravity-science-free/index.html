Highlight

At the Gemini 4 Argon launch Google offered 10,000 scientists six months of Antigravity's highest usage tier plus Gemini 4 Argon at no cost. The Google account used to submit the interest form is the one upgraded, personal Gmail or university Workspace alike, with rolling weekly review since 13 October.

What you get

Up to 10,000 researchers get six months of Antigravity's highest usage tier plus Gemini 4 Argon, free; Argon normally needs Google AI Ultra at $249.99 a month, so the grant is worth half a year of Ultra-level agent usage. Apply through the interest form at antigravity.google/use-cases/science while signed in to the Google account you want upgraded, giving your institution, institutional email, a public lab or personal page, an ORCID or Google Scholar profile, and a 6-12 month research summary that must not be confidential or proprietary. Review is weekly and capacity-limited: from 13 October 2026 more than 27,000 applications arrived, about 3,000 were granted first and unsuccessful applicants are not told. Once in, install the Science plugin from the Customizations market to query AlphaGenome, AlphaFold Database, UniProt, PubChem, ChEMBL and 30+ life-science databases; fields run beyond biology to medicine, chemistry and materials, physics and fusion, quantum, climate, neuroscience, maths, computer science, engineering, social science, agriculture and economics, as long as the work is non-commercial.

Conditions

Non-commercial research only; applicants must be staff or students at a university, degree-granting institute, non-profit research institute or national lab (faculty, postdocs, researchers, PhD and master's students; no option for undergraduates); an ORCID or Google Scholar profile is required; the summary must not contain confidential or unpublished data; capped at 10,000 places, reviewed on a rolling basis with only successful applicants contacted; only the Google account used on the form is upgraded.

Steps

  1. Sign in with the Google account you want upgraded at antigravity.google/use-cases/science
  2. Click Apply for free access and fill in institution, institutional email and a public page
  3. Add an ORCID or Google Scholar link and a 6-12 month research summary
  4. Submit and wait for rolling review; selected applicants are notified and the account is upgraded
  5. Install the Science plugin from Antigravity's Customizations marketplace

Watch out

The 10,000 places are a ceiling, not a promise: Google reported about 3,000 admitted from over 27,000 applications by 10 November, fewer than one in nine; the top tier has no published quota, so heavy multi-agent work may still hit limits; the programme says nothing about what happens after six months, so do not rest a project on it; the research summary is stored by Google, so keep it non-confidential; the upgrade belongs to the individual account, not an institution.
