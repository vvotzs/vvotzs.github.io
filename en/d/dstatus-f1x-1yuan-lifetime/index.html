Highlight

After the panel was cracked the developer put out a celebration code: 1 yuan instead of 69 for a permanent 10-node licence, limited to 200; a free tier with five nodes includes the full probing feature set

What you get

The DStatus author posted on NodeSeek on 21 November 2026 that the panel had been cracked, and marked the occasion with a code: buy the personal edition in the user centre and enter F1x, which drops the 69 yuan price to 1 yuan for a permanent 10-node licence, limited to 200 codes. Anyone who does not want to pay can register for a free permanent five-node licence; the author says the recent feature drop moved the full probing set, billing, traffic and network quality, the built-in AI operations assistant and MCP access for Claude and Codex into the free tier, leaving only cloud services and node count as the difference. There is also a draw for three permanent 100-node licences, opened on 25 November 2026 at 09:00. The official pricing page, read on 21 November 2026, matches: registering grants a free licence for five nodes with the full probing feature set and a small AI allowance; the personal edition is a one-off payment for 10, 20 or 50 nodes; the professional edition is annual; the permanent edition is one-off. The page also states that a one-off payment covers the software and all future updates, while the built-in AI allowance and hosted status pages are cloud services currently included as a limited-time gift and may be sold separately later. The price table itself is rendered dynamically, so the 69 yuan tier and the 1 yuan redemption were not readable on the page and are recorded from the author's post.

Conditions

An account at dstatus.sh is needed for the free licence or a purchase; code F1x applies to the 10-node personal edition and is limited to 200 uses; a one-off payment does not permanently include the built-in AI allowance or hosted status pages; self-hosting needs your own machine and command line; do not activate one licence on several instances at once or the anti-abuse check will trip.

Steps

  1. Register at dstatus.sh and claim the free five-node licence first
  2. For 10 nodes, buy the personal edition in the user centre, enter F1x and confirm the price shows 1 yuan before paying
  3. Deploy the panel per the official docs and paste the licence key in the admin panel
  4. Reply in the giveaway thread for a shot at the 100-node licence on 25 November

Watch out

A one-person or very small project with long-term maintenance resting on one developer; the built-in AI allowance and hosted pages are gifts that may later be charged; the code is limited and may already be gone; the pricing page is dynamic, so confirm the 1 yuan at checkout; the panel agent runs on the monitored machines, so review its permissions.
