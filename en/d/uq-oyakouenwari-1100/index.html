Highlight

New UQ mobile lines on Komikomi Plan Value or Tokutoku Plan 2 for a household with someone aged 5–18 get ¥1,100 off monthly for a year; Komikomi also adds 300GB valid for 3 years

What you get

KDDI and Okinawa Cellular began accepting applications for the UQ Parent-Child Support Discount on 19 November 2026. New UQ mobile lines on Komikomi Plan Value (¥3,828/month) or Tokutoku Plan 2 (¥4,048/month), with a member aged 5–18 in the same home-set or family-set discount group, get ¥1,100 off per month for 12 months starting the following month. Komikomi Plan Value also gets the Parent-Child Support Plan with an extra 300GB valid for three years, and the UQ Komikomi discount takes another ¥660 off for up to 13 months (from month 14 it needs an au PAY Gold Card).

Conditions

Applications open from 19 November 2026 with no announced end date; must be a new line, MNP or plan change to an eligible plan; requires a 5–18 year old in the group; one per line; does not stack with the under-5GB discount; applications late in the month may be applied later.

Steps

  1. Confirm you have a 5–18 year old in the same home-set or family-set discount group
  2. Order or port a UQ mobile line on Komikomi Plan Value or Tokutoku Plan 2 (SIM or eSIM)
  3. Complete the application during the acceptance period; the ¥1,100 discount starts the next month for 12 months
  4. For Komikomi, the 300GB arrives around the 10th of the following month and must be claimed in My UQ mobile

Watch out

You need a new line, port or plan change, which can end existing discounts; if the under-18 line leaves or the family group breaks up, the whole family's discount stops that month; the 300GB must be claimed in the app or it expires; the au PAY Gold Card costs ¥11,000 a year.
