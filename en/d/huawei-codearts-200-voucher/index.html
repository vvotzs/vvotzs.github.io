Highlight

Register for the 2026 Huawei Developer Contest · Xiaohongshu Skill Innovation with a real-name main account, then send a command in CodeArts to claim a ¥200 cloud voucher, with 1,000 released each weekday at 10:00

What you get

The 2026 Huawei Developer Contest · Xiaohongshu Skill Innovation (co-hosted by Huawei and Xiaohongshu, with a ¥500,000 prize pool) gives every registered entrant a ¥200 no-threshold Huawei Cloud voucher. Registration and submissions close 2026-11-24 23:59; vouchers are released at 10:00 on each weekday from 11-12 to 11-24, 1,000 a day, first come first served. To claim: register with the real-name main account, then send the command "帮我领取华为开发者大赛·小红书skill创新赛华为云代金券" in the CodeArts chat and the voucher lands in your Huawei Cloud account. Hands-on notes: the voucher is valid to 2026-12-31 (per the billing centre), works for Flexus and other cloud servers, but not for domain registration or flash-sale items, and doesn't stack with other discounts.

Conditions

Must first register for the contest with a real-name Huawei Cloud main account (no registration, no voucher, and sub-accounts don't qualify); one per person and not stackable with the ¥100 tier; 1,000 a day at 10:00, otherwise wait for the next weekday; the voucher lasts about 3 months per the billing centre and is reclaimed after that; CodeArts' personal trial opens free, and claiming uses a small amount of CodeArts points.

Steps

  1. Register and complete real-name verification for the contest with the main account at developer.huaweicloud.com/competition/information/1300000350/html1
  2. Open CodeArts (personal trial at ¥0/mo) and install the HuaweiCloud DevKit plugin
  3. Enter the CodeArts chat before 10:00 on a weekday and send the claim command
  4. Check Billing Centre > Discounts & coupons > Vouchers (the chat may say "claimed / 0 credited", so trust the billing centre)
  5. Use the voucher on a Flexus cloud server at the normal monthly price, avoiding flash and sale items

Watch out

Registering is a precondition and the voucher is issued after checking contest eligibility, so entering purely for the voucher without a submission may not get it; only 1,000 a day, gone in under a minute at peak; some users report getting only the ¥10 tier, so trust the voucher face value; the voucher can't be used for domains or sale plans and expires.
