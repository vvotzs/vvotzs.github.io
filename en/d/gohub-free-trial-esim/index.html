Highlight

New users get 300MB of high-speed data in the app, valid 1 day, across 12 Asian destinations including China, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Malaysia and Thailand, with no card needed

What you get

GoHub's one-off free trial eSIM for new users: 300MB of high-speed data valid 1 day, for 12 Asian destinations (mainland China included). Everything happens in the GoHub app with no credit card; the clock starts on first connection at the destination, and the data stops when it runs out without renewing itself. Afterwards you can buy a paid plan for the same destination in the same app without reinstalling the eSIM. It's data only, with no number and no calls or texts.

Conditions

GoHub new users only, one per person; an eSIM-capable unlocked phone (most mainland-China iPhones don't support it and need a blank eUICC card); claim in the app only; install and activate within 30 days of claiming; the 1-day validity starts on the first connection at the destination, not at install; data only, no number.

Steps

  1. Download the GoHub app from the App Store or Google Play and register
  2. Find "Free Trial eSIM" in the app and choose your Asian destination
  3. Install the eSIM as prompted (you can install it at home on Wi-Fi without using the trial data)
  4. On arrival set GoHub as the mobile data line and turn on data roaming — the clock starts on first connection

Watch out

Only 300MB / 1 day, enough for a ride, navigation and a few messages but not a whole day; it stops when the data runs out with no charge and no auto-renewal; don't treat a 1-day trial as main data for a whole trip.
