Highlight

Move a full external account into Co-op Bank's Standard Current Account or Everyday Extra with CASS, deposit £1,500, keep 2 direct debits, make 5 card payments and register for online banking within 30 days to get £125 within 7 days; keep the same activity for 3 more months for another £25 each month, up to £200

What you get

Be Clever With Your Cash and Moneyfactscompare (checked 18 Nov 2026) confirm the new Co-op Bank switch offer: new customers opening a Standard Current Account or Everyday Extra, or existing holders of a qualifying account, complete a full CASS switch and get £125 within 7 days, in time for Christmas. Then, for three more months, keep depositing at least £1,500 a month, keep at least 2 active direct debits and make at least 5 debit card payments to receive another £25 per month, capped at a further £75 and £200 in total.

Conditions

UK residents only; a full CASS switch (old account closed); deposit at least £1,500 within 30 days, set up 2 active direct debits, make 5+ debit card payments and register for online banking; repeat the same activity for 3 further months; not valid if you took a Co-op switching bonus since 1 Nov 2022.

Steps

  1. Apply for the Standard Current Account or Everyday Extra on Co-op Bank's site
  2. Move your main external account across with CASS
  3. Within 30 days deposit £1,500, set 2 direct debits, make 5 card payments, register for online banking
  4. After £125 lands, keep the activity for 3 more months to collect £25 each month

Watch out

The £125 pays in one go but the three £25 payments depend on keeping up the activity, so stopping the card spend or direct debits loses them; CASS closes your old account, so check salary and subscriptions move cleanly; banks may refuse serial switchers.
