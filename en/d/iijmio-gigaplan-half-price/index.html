Highlight

On a 5/10/15/25/35 GB voice SIM or voice eSIM the monthly fee drops by ¥475/700/800/1,000/1,200 for months 1–3; the 15 GB plan also gets 5 GB extra for 3 months, and stacked with the call-option discount that is 20 GB with unlimited 5-minute calls for about ¥800 a month.

What you get

IIJ started the IIJmio Giga Plan half-price campaign on 2026-11-05 with no announced end date. It applies to the voice SIM and voice eSIM versions of the Giga Plan at 5, 10, 15, 25 and 35 GB, whether you are a new customer, transferring in with MNP, or moving from an old plan (Minimum Start, Light Start, Family Share, metered). For months 1–3 from activation the monthly fee is cut by ¥475 (5 GB), ¥700 (10 GB), ¥800 (15 GB), ¥1,000 (25 GB) or ¥1,200 (35 GB), tax included. Choosing 15 GB also adds 5 GB for up to three months, so you use 20 GB. The simultaneous call-option discount campaign makes the 通話定額 5-minute+, 10-minute+ and unlimited options free for up to three months, so 15 GB (20 GB) plus the 20 GB unlimited-call bundle comes to about ¥800 a month (¥700 with the family discount). The first month is prorated by days, and the discount never exceeds that month's bill or rolls over.

Conditions

Order a 5/10/15/25/35 GB voice SIM or voice eSIM during the campaign and activate it. Keep the same data tier until the benefit ends: changing capacity, switching to a data/SMS-only SIM or cancelling ends the discount (switching between a physical SIM and eSIM keeps it). Taking this campaign on 25 GB disqualifies the separate 25 GB trial discount. Not valid through BIC SIM or JAL Mobile, and not for capacity changes by existing Giga Plan customers. In a prorated first month the discount is capped at the amount billed.

Steps

  1. On iijmio.jp pick the Giga Plan, choose voice eSIM or voice SIM, and a 5/10/15/25/35 GB tier
  2. Order as a new line or MNP transfer (or switch from an old plan in My IIJmio) and finish identity verification and payment setup
  3. Activate the eSIM profile; the discount starts automatically from the activation month, with no separate application
  4. Add the call-option (通話定額オプション) while the campaign runs to get up to three months of it free
  5. Keep the same tier through month three; after that the normal fees apply (5 GB ¥950 / 10 GB ¥1,400 / 15 GB ¥1,600 / 25 GB ¥2,000 / 35 GB ¥2,400)

Watch out

You need a Japanese address, Japanese payment method and identity verification, so it is effectively closed to overseas users. The end date is unannounced and can come at any time. Only three months are discounted — from month four you pay full price, so work out the long-run cost. Changing capacity or SIM type voids the discount, and a prorated first month can make the discount look smaller than advertised.
