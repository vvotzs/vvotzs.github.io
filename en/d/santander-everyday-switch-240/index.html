Highlight

Full CASS switch into Santander Everyday: complete the switch, two household direct debits, a £1,500 deposit and £200 into the Regular Saver within 60 days to get £240 about 90 days later. Apply through the MSE link before 15:00 on 2026-11-25 and add a £25 Amazon gift card.

What you get

Santander's official £240 Switch & Save: apply from 27 October 2026 (the bank can withdraw the offer at any time). Within 60 days you must complete a full CASS switch (old account closed), have at least 2 "household" Direct Debits on the account (council tax, mobile/home phone/broadband/pay TV, utilities and other specified billers), deposit a total of at least £1,500 into the Everyday Current Account and put at least £200 into your own Santander Regular Saver. Eligibility is checked on day 60 after the switch, and £240 is paid into the account within the next 30 days, so about 90 days in; you must still hold both accounts and the 2 active household Direct Debits on the payout day. Addition (MSE round-up 2026-11-19): applying for the Everyday account through the designated MoneySavingExpert link and meeting the same £240 conditions also earns a £25 Amazon.co.uk gift card, £265 in total. Applications must be submitted by 15:00 on 2026-11-25; the claim instructions arrive by email within 120 days and must be used within 30 days.

Conditions

UK resident; held no Santander current account on 2026-01-01 and neither you nor any joint holder has ever had a Santander switch bonus; the switch must be a full CASS switch that closes the old account; within 60 days: 2 specified household Direct Debits, a £1,500 deposit and £200 into a Regular Saver; one payout per joint account. The extra £25 gift card requires applying through the MSE promotional link before 15:00 on 2026-11-25.

Steps

  1. Open an Everyday Current Account at santander.co.uk (or use the one you have)
  2. Choose a full switch through the Current Account Switch Service and enter the old account details correctly
  3. Move at least 2 household Direct Debits (mobile, broadband, utilities, council tax) across within 60 days
  4. Deposit a total of £1,500 into the new account and £200 into your Regular Saver within 60 days
  5. Check the £240 around 90 days later; if it's missing call 0330 9123123

Watch out

The headline £240 needs the switch, 2 household Direct Debits, £1,500 of deposits and £200 into a Regular Saver — miss one and there's nothing; the £1,500 and £200 are your own money (you can take them back out, but they must still be there on payout day); the switch closes your old account, so check salary and subscriptions can move; the bank can withdraw the offer at any time (applications already submitted are still paid if eligible).
