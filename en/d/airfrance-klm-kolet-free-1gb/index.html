Highlight

KLM's official partner page states first-time Kolet users get 1GB free for 2 days across 190+ countries, with no flight booking needed, plus 10% off a first plan and 10 Flying Blue Miles per euro

What you get

A partnership between Air France, KLM and French eSIM provider Kolet (live since 25 November 2024 and still running in 2026): enter FBDEAL in KLM's Flying Blue Kolet portal or the Kolet app (Air France also gives AFKLDATA) and a first-time customer gets 1GB free for two days across 190+ countries, plus 10% off every data plan, 10 Flying Blue miles per euro spent, 250 miles each when a friend buys their first plan and, in France and the Netherlands, mileage payment through Flying Blue+. Transavia France, in the same group, uses the same template from May 2026 with up to 1GB for two days on arrival.

Conditions

New Kolet customers only; install the Kolet app or go through KLM's Flying Blue portal and register by email; link the Flying Blue number before paying to earn miles; the free 1GB lasts two days; data only with no number; the 10% excludes monthly plans and stacks with nothing else.

Eligibility

Phone number
Any number, +86 included

Steps

  1. Open KLM's Flying Blue Kolet page and choose Explore with Kolet, or sign up in the Kolet app
  2. Enter FBDEAL (or AFKLDATA for Air France), add your email and claim the free 1GB
  3. Choose the destination and travel date, then install the eSIM on stable Wi-Fi before flying
  4. Turn the eSIM on after landing; add the Flying Blue number under Profile to earn Miles

Watch out

The free gigabyte is once per person and device, and reinstalling does not reset it; it stops after 2 days or 1GB; the airlines do not promise mainland-China roaming (the Virgin Atlantic version is reported working there, this one is untested); the discount and Miles apply to paid plans only.
