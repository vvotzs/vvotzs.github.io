Highlight

Apply online, get approved and spend A$5,000 on the new card within three months to earn 75,000 Qantas Points and 50 Status Credits; another A$5,000 in the first three months of year two adds 25,000 points, against a A$450 annual fee and a A$450 yearly Qantas Travel Credit.

What you get

Spend A$5,000 on eligible purchases within three months of approval for 75,000 Qantas Points and 50 Status Credits; spend another A$5,000 in the first three months of year two for 25,000 more, up to 100,000 points and 50 Status Credits. The A$450 annual fee includes a yearly A$450 Qantas Travel Credit (Qantas flights booked through American Express Travel, at least A$450 per booking), two Qantas Club lounge invitations after first spend, travel insurance and up to four free supplementary cards. Earn 1.25 points per A$1, 0.5 with Australian government merchants, dropping to 1 point after 100,000 points in a calendar year. At the common 1.5c-per-point valuation, 75,000 points are worth about A$1,125.

Conditions

New card members who have not held any American Express card in the past 18 months; 18+, an Australian citizen, permanent resident or long-term visa holder with local income and good credit; the application asks for a driver's licence, income, expenses and debts plus proof of income; the A$5,000 must be spent on eligible purchases within three months of approval; the A$450 fee is not waived; points need a Qantas Frequent Flyer membership.

Steps

  1. Confirm you are a new Amex customer (no Amex card in the past 18 months) and hold a Qantas Frequent Flyer account
  2. Apply online from the Qantas Ultimate product page on the Amex Australia site with your licence number, income, expenses and proof of income ready
  3. Spend A$5,000 on eligible purchases within three months of approval and check the points land on a statement
  4. After the first eligible Qantas spend, use the A$450 travel credit through Amex Travel and collect the lounge invitations
  5. Spend another A$5,000 in the first three months of year two for the extra 25,000 points, and consider downgrading before the second annual fee if you do not plan to keep the card

Watch out

A credit card application leaves a hard inquiry that affects later borrowing and mortgage approval; the A$5,000 spend is a hard threshold, so falling short means paying the A$450 fee for nothing; the valuation assumes 1.5 Australian cents per point and actual value depends on redemptions, with Classic Flight Reward seats limited; the A$450 travel credit only applies to Qantas flights booked through Amex Travel at A$450 or more; avoid overspending just for Status Credits, and foreign-currency transactions carry a conversion fee.
