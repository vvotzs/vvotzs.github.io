Highlight

A local-first workspace for docs, whiteboards and a knowledge base: MIT editor with a self-hostable backend, unlimited workspaces and members when you host it; cloud Basic is free with 100GB, 100MB files and 10 collaborators

What you get

AFFiNE is an open-source alternative to Notion plus Miro: the same content flips between a document view and an infinite whiteboard (Edgeless), local-first and offline-capable, with a backend you can self-host to keep the data. The GitHub repo has 78,400 stars and 5,719 forks and was still being pushed on 2026-11-26. The pricing page states: self-hosted FOSS is free with unlimited workspaces and members; cloud Basic is free with 100GB of storage, 100MB per file, up to 10 members per workspace and 30 days of version history; Team is $10 per seat per month and the AFFiNE AI add-on starts at $8.9 per month.

Conditions

Self-hosting means deploying it yourself (Docker and similar) and running the server; cloud Basic is free with no card; AFFiNE AI and team add-ons cost extra; the editor is MIT but the backend is source-available, not pure MIT.

Eligibility

Identity
No identity documents

Steps

  1. Download the desktop or mobile app from affine.pro, or sign up for the free cloud Basic
  2. To self-host, bring up the backend with Docker per the docs and point the client at your server
  3. Keep docs, whiteboards and tasks in one workspace and invite members as needed
  4. Add the AFFiNE AI add-on only if you want the AI features

Watch out

The backend is source-available rather than pure MIT, so read the licence before commercial use or redistribution; the free cloud tier caps members and storage; self-hosting means maintaining backups and upgrades yourself; AI features are billed separately.
