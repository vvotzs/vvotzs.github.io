Highlight

Self-custody Visa Platinum issued on a Puerto Rico BIN, no account or monthly fee, free virtual card, up to $100 of AVAX cashback a month on the Standard tier.

What you get

Ethena Pay is Ethena's self-custody Visa card (issued by Third National, programme managed by Rain, BIN registered in Puerto Rico). The account is free: no opening fee, no monthly fee, no minimum balance, free virtual card issuance, and it works with Apple Pay and Google Pay. The value is the cashback. Standard tier pays 4% on the first $2,500 of card spend each month, 1% from $2,500 to $4,000 and nothing above. Pro (unlocked with 10 referrals) pays 4.5% to $8,000 and VIP (invite only) 5% to $20,000. Cashback is calculated in dollars, paid in AVAX after settlement, roughly daily, with no monthly or lifetime cap — so a Standard user tops out near $100 of AVAX a month. The FAQ says it is live in 47 countries and open to non-US persons only. KYC accepts passports, residence permits and driving licences, with the address typed into a form and no proof of address required. It used to need an invite code; this round's submission says the whitelist is gone (unverified).

Conditions

Non-US persons only, KYC required. Excluded spend includes cash advances, ATM withdrawals, crypto purchases, gift cards, P2P transfers, account funding, taxes and fees; transactions under $1 earn nothing; Standard earns nothing above $4,000 a month.

Steps

  1. Download the app at pay.ethena.fi and finish KYC with a passport as a resident of one of the 47 countries
  2. Issue the free virtual Visa and add it to Apple Pay / Google Pay
  3. Spend on the card; AVAX cashback lands a few days after each transaction settles

Watch out

Cashback is paid in AVAX, so price moves can eat the gain. Rewards are discretionary and can be cut at any time. The whitelist removal is only the submitter's word, so check before signing up. Whether mainland Chinese residency passes KYC is unverified.
