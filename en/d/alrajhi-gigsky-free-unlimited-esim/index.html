Highlight

Eligible Al Rajhi credit and prepaid cardholders get a free GigSky unlimited-data eSIM: 7 days on Infinite cards and 3 days on Signature, Platinum and prepaid cards, plus 20-30% off add-on plans; whether mainland China is in the Visa Destinations list needs checking

What you get

A Visa partnership between Al Rajhi Bank and GigSky: qualifying Al Rajhi credit or prepaid cards get a free GigSky eSIM with unlimited data at designated destinations on arrival. Card tier sets the length — Infinite credit cards get seven days of unlimited data and 30% off every add-on, while Signature, Platinum and prepaid get three days and 20% off. The offer runs to 13 April 2027, the free data starts at activation, an eSIM unactivated within a year activates automatically, and each cardholder can use it once every 365 days.

Conditions

Saudi Al Rajhi qualifying credit cards (Infinite, Signature, Platinum) and prepaid cards only; once every 365 days; free data runs from activation and auto-activates after a year; destinations are limited to the Visa Destinations list; GigSky itself covers mainland China via China Unicom, but whether this tier includes it awaits the official list.

Steps

  1. Check the tier of your Al Rajhi credit or prepaid card (Infinite for 7 days, Signature, Platinum or prepaid for 3 days)
  2. Add the card in the GigSky app and claim the free plan from the Visa Destinations section
  3. Open Regions, View Details, Countries in the app to confirm the destination list, including whether mainland China is covered
  4. Install and activate the eSIM on arrival, then turn on data roaming

Watch out

Requires a Saudi bank account and a qualifying card, so an overseas user can barely join; once every 365 days; claiming without activating still auto-activates after a year, which burns the allowance; the destination list depends on Visa's programme and mainland China is not enumerated, so it stays unverified; unlimited data is still subject to fair use and local speeds.
