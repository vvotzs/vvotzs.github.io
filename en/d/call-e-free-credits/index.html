Highlight

Hand the "I'll have to call them" errands to ChatGPT / Claude / Muse / Claude Code: it plans the call, you confirm, it dials a real US or Canadian number, gets through phone menus and hold, then brings back a summary, the transcript and a structured result.

What you get

CALL-E is a phone-call agent from AI Rudder (Singapore), launched in November 2026. It only makes outbound calls. Install the official calle skill in the AI assistant you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Muse, OpenClaw, Hermes) or in a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor), or call it through MCP, the TypeScript and Python SDKs, the REST API or WhatsApp. Say what you need and it plans the call — who to call, when, in which language, and what counts as done. It asks for anything missing, shows you the plan, and only dials after you confirm. During the call it handles live pickup, voicemail, hold, transfers, silence and interruptions, and can navigate phone menus (IVR) with keypad input. Afterwards you get a summary, the full transcript and structured data validated against a schema you define, with quoted lines as evidence and a completion-confidence label. Calls can be scheduled or sent as a batch. Typical errands: booking a table, moving an appointment, calling around for a quote, checking stock, cancelling, reaching a human in support. New accounts get 100 credits (the site says about US$1); credits are a usage allowance, not a fixed number of calls or minutes.

Conditions

100 credits once per new account. Billed per second: a US/CA outbound call in Goal mode is about $0.0148/10s model fee plus $0.02/min carrier fee (about $0.06 for 30 seconds); a one-shot call is about $0.0296/10s plus $0.04/min (about $0.13 for 30 seconds). Preparation can consume model credits even if the call never connects. Outbound only — it does not answer or screen incoming calls. International numbers outside the US/CA are mainly for development and testing; other destinations need your own SIP trunk.

Steps

  1. Install the official calle skill in an MCP-capable assistant or coding agent (or use the API/SDK) and sign in — a CALL-E account with 100 credits is created automatically
  2. Ask in your own words: "Book a table for two at 7 PM Friday at Bella's Bistro and ask about parking"
  3. It fills the gaps (who to call, the name for the booking, what counts as done) and shows you the call plan
  4. Confirm, and it dials, presses through the phone menu and waits on hold; you can take over or add instructions mid-call
  5. Read the summary, transcript and structured result afterwards; schedule or batch calls when you need many

Watch out

100 credits buy only a few trial calls — a real test needs a top-up. Billing combines model and carrier fees, and planning can burn credits even when no call happens. The AI tells the person on the line it is an assistant, and some businesses refuse such calls. Before letting it book, cancel or pay, read the call plan it shows you. Whether you can sign up from mainland China and similar regions is not documented — check the site or login page.
