Highlight

Enrol in any Google Career Certificate on Coursera and redeem 3 months of Google AI Pro from inside the course: Gemini 4, Flow and NotebookLM; the 7-day Coursera trial is enough to claim it

What you get

Coursera's Google Career Certificates, including the Google AI Professional Certificate, come with a limited-time 3-month Google AI Pro subscription: after enrolling, open AI Fundamentals, unit 2, and use Redeem your Google AI Pro trial to generate a Google One redemption link. Coursera offers a 7-day free trial, enough to claim the code; redemption needs a Google Payments method, and AI Pro renews at $19.99 a month when the three months end.

Conditions

A paid Coursera subscription is needed (claim inside the 7-day trial, then $49/month); use a personal Google account that has never had an AI Pro trial; one per person, and redeeming replaces an existing AI Pro plan.

Steps

  1. Enrol in the Google AI Professional Certificate on Coursera
  2. Open AI Fundamentals, unit 2, and click Redeem your Google AI Pro trial
  3. Sign in with a personal Google account and add Google Payments
  4. Confirm the 3 free months and subscribe
  5. Cancel the Coursera trial from my-purchases

Watch out

The Coursera trial bills monthly unless cancelled; AI Pro renews at $19.99 a month after three months; family plans, higher tiers and discounted Pixel bundles are ineligible; redeeming replaces any current AI Pro subscription.
