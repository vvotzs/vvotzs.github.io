Highlight

One app covers HTTP, magnet/BT, FTP, M3U8, MPEG-DASH and eD2k, plus YouTube/Bilibili clip selection and GitHub/HuggingFace mirror acceleration; native on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android with no account and no card

What you get

A cross-platform downloader written in Python and Qt (PySide6), GPL-3.0, 17,300 stars on GitHub, with v4.5.11 shipped on 20 November 2026. It covers HTTP, magnet/BT, FTP, M3U8, MPEG-DASH and eD2k; YouTube and Bilibili support clip selection, any quality and subtitles, playlists and multi-part episodes, and GitHub/HuggingFace downloads get mirror acceleration. A browser extension puts a download button next to the video and carries your login state over. Smart chunking avoids file merging, bandwidth is detected automatically, real-browser TLS fingerprints get past anti-bot checks, and you can change the URL, headers or proxy mid-download. It exposes an aria2-compatible RPC for third-party tools and has a Feature Packs plugin ecosystem. No account, no ads, no card.

Conditions

Free and open source under GPL-3.0, so just install the release; no account needed; YouTube/Bilibili downloads need your own signed-in session; Android needs 9.0 or later and Linux needs glibc 2.35+.

Steps

  1. Download the installer for your platform from gd.xychr.com or GitHub Releases (Windows, macOS, Linux, Android)
  2. Pick a download folder on first launch and enable browser takeover and plugins in settings if you want them
  3. Paste an HTTP, magnet or video link into a new task, or install the extension and click download on the page
  4. Turn on the aria2-compatible RPC in settings if scripts need to drive it

Watch out

Open-source software, so compile it yourself or use the official package rather than a repack; downloading copyrighted material (Bilibili and YouTube included) is still your own compliance risk; BT downloads carry seeding and bandwidth costs; plugins come from the community and individual ones can fall out of date.
